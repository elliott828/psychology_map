Conservation

Concept·守恒

Understanding that the amount stays the same even when the shape changes.

School
Cognitive Development Theory
In plain words
Grasping that an object's quantity, volume and similar properties do not change just because its appearance does.
Example
When the same amount of water is poured into a tall, thin glass, young children say there is “more.” Around age seven, most children understand that the amount is the same.
Common misuse
This is not a sign that “earlier is smarter.” It is a milestone in cognitive development, and the age at which children reach it varies quite a bit across cultures.
