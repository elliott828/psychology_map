Stimulus-Response

Concept·刺激—反应

Behaviorism's basic unit for describing behavior: an external stimulus triggers an observable response.

School
Behaviorism
In plain words
Watson and others argued that psychology should study only the regularities between observable stimuli and responses, and not invisible “inner minds.”
Example
When a bell rings, a dog salivates. When a red light comes on, a driver presses the brake.
Common misuse
Believing behaviorists denied that people have inner experiences. The early position was a methodological limit, and later behavior analysis also accepts private events, analyzing them as behavior.
