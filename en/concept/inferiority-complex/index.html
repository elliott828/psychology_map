Inferiority Complex

Concept·自卑情结

Feelings of inadequacy dominate life, showing up as withdrawal or bravado.

School
Individual Psychology
In plain words
Adler believed everyone feels some inferiority. It becomes a “complex” only when handled badly, with avoidance or showing off used as compensation.
Example
Someone who constantly brags about their achievements may actually be deeply anxious inside.
Common misuse
Having feelings of inferiority is not the same as having an inferiority complex, and neither means that a person is broken.
