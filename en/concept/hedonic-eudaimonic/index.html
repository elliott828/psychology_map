Hedonic vs. Eudaimonic Well-Being

Concept·快乐与实现

Well-being comes in two kinds: feeling good and living meaningfully.

School
Positive Psychology
In plain words
Hedonic well-being emphasizes pleasure and satisfaction. Eudaimonic well-being emphasizes growth, meaning and realizing one's potential.
Example
Enjoying a fine meal is the first kind. Working toward something important over many years is the second.
Common misuse
The two are not opposites, and neither should be treated as “higher” than the other.
