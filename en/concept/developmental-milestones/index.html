Developmental Milestones

Concept·发展里程碑

Abilities that most children acquire at roughly similar ages.

School
Child Study Movement
In plain words
Research on children's motor development, based on large-scale observation and measurement, has produced reference points such as sitting by a few months, walking around one year, and speaking short phrases by age two.
Example
At a pediatric checkup, the doctor asks whether the child can walk independently and whether they can say a two-word phrase.
Common misuse
Treating milestones as a pass mark that must be reached on time. The age at which a skill appears has a wide normal range, and only clear delays warrant evaluation.
