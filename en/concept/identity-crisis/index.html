Identity Crisis

Concept·同一性危机

A period when the question “Who am I?” becomes especially pressing.

School
Psychosocial Development
In plain words
Adolescents and young adults often explore and wrestle with their values, roles and future direction.
Example
After graduating from university, someone is unsure what they want to do and dissatisfied with the status quo, so they keep trying new things and questioning their choices.
Common misuse
Often treated as an illness. Erikson saw it as a normal developmental task, and handled well, it leads to a clearer sense of self.
