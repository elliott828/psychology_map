Synaptic Strengthening

Concept·突触强化

A cellular phenomenon in which connections grow stronger with use.

School
Hebbian Learning and Plasticity
In plain words
After a synapse is activated repeatedly, the receiving neuron responds more strongly to it, and this change can last a long time.
Example
When you memorize vocabulary, the same pathway is activated again and again. The corresponding connections become easier to call up.
Common misuse
Not all learning relies on this process, and it does not follow that “more practice always means better retention.”
