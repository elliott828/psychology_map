Shaping

Concept·塑造

Gradually rewarding small steps toward a goal.

School
Operant Conditioning
In plain words
First reward any behavior that moves toward the goal, then gradually raise the standard until the full new behavior is formed.
Example
Teaching a child to tie shoelaces: first reward picking up the laces, then crossing them, then tying the knot.
Common misuse
Often misused as “just reward them and it will work,” overlooking the child's own motivation and interests.
