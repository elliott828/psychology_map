Ego-Self Axis

Concept·自我—自性轴

The connection between the ego (the center of consciousness) and the Self (the whole psyche).

School
Post-Jungian and Archetypal Psychology
In plain words
Neumann and later Jungian analysts use this concept to describe how, in early childhood, the ego and the whole psyche are not yet separated. Later the ego differentiates, and it then needs to reconnect with the Self.
Example
After a major setback, a person feels that their life is being guided by something larger than themselves. This can be described as a rebuilding of the connection between ego and Self.
Common misuse
Treating it as an observable brain structure or an empirically tested developmental stage. It is a theoretical construct within analytical psychology and lacks independent empirical testing.
