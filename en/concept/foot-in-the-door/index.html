Foot-in-the-Door

Concept·登门槛效应

Agreeing to a small request makes it easier to agree to a larger one.

School
Conformity and Obedience
In plain words
Once someone agrees to a small request, they are more likely to agree to a bigger one later, because they want to stay consistent with their earlier behavior.
Example
You are first asked to sign a petition, and later asked to donate. The success rate is higher than asking for the donation directly.
Common misuse
The size of the effect varies with the situation. It is not a magic formula that makes any sales pitch work.
