Introspection

Concept·内省法

Trained participants report their immediate feelings and conscious contents.

School
Experimental Psychology
In plain words
In Wundt's laboratory, participants under tightly controlled stimuli reported the components of their sensations, in order to study how consciousness is built.
Example
Tasting a sip of wine and describing its sourness, sweetness and temperature separately, rather than just saying “it's good.”
Common misuse
Thinking any self-report counts as introspection. Wundt's introspection required training and standardized conditions. Casual self-impressions do not count.
