Psychological Flexibility

Concept·心理灵活性

Being able to act on your values even when it is uncomfortable.

School
Third-Wave Therapies
In plain words
The core idea of ACT: being willing to stay in contact with present thoughts and feelings, while choosing actions guided by your values.
Example
You still walk up to the podium while nervous, because you value sharing with others.
Common misuse
It is not “controlling your emotions” or “thinking positively.” It means not being driven around by your emotions.
