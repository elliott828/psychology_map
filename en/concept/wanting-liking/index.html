Wanting versus Liking

Concept·“想要”与“喜欢”

The drive to want something and the pleasure of enjoying it rely on different brain systems.

School
Reward Prediction Error
In plain words
Berridge's research shows that the dopamine system is more involved in “wanting” (motivation), while “liking” (enjoyment) is handled by other, smaller brain areas. The two can come apart.
Example
You cannot stop scrolling on your phone, yet you do not feel especially happy about it. Here, wanting outweighs liking.
Common misuse
Calling dopamine the “pleasure molecule.” Dopamine is mainly linked to expectation, motivation and learning, and it is not the same thing as pleasure itself.
