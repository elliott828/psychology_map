Effort Justification

Concept·努力合理化

The more effort put into something, the more people tend to think it is worth having.

School
Cognitive Dissonance
In plain words
In a study by Aronson and Mills, people who had to go through an embarrassing initiation to join a group later liked the group more, even though it was not especially interesting. Feeling that the effort was “wasted” would cause dissonance.
Example
A house you spent a lot of money and energy renovating is one you will still insist is great, even if it has flaws.
Common misuse
Treating it as a synonym for “sunk cost.” The sunk cost fallacy is about decisions. Effort justification is about changing attitudes to reduce dissonance.
