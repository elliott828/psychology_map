Mental Age

Concept·心理年龄

Matching a child's test performance to the typical level of children of a given age.

School
Intelligence Testing
In plain words
The approach of the Binet-Simon scale: if an 8-year-old can pass questions that most 10-year-olds can pass, their mental age is 10.
Example
A 6-year-old performs at the average level of an 8-year-old, so their mental age is 8.
Common misuse
Using “mental age” for adults or to describe someone as “acting like a person of a certain age.” The concept only fits developing children and loses its meaning in adulthood.
