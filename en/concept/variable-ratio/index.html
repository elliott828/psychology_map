Variable-Ratio Schedule

Concept·变比率强化

Not knowing which attempt will pay off is what keeps people hooked.

School
Operant Conditioning
In plain words
Rewards depend on how many times you act, but the number needed changes each time. The behavior becomes persistent and frequent as a result.
Example
Slot machines, the next video in a short-video feed, and gacha card-draw games all work this way: the next try might be the winner.
Common misuse
Often described as “people are designed to be addicted.” The schedule only describes a pattern of behavior. It does not mean everyone who experiences it becomes addicted.
