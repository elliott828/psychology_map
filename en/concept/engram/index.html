Engram

Concept·记忆痕迹（印迹）

The physical trace a specific memory leaves in the brain.

School
Hebbian Learning and Plasticity
In plain words
A specific group of neurons and their connections hold a memory. Reactivating them brings the memory back to mind.
Example
When you recall your mother's kitchen, a cluster of related neurons lights up together. That cluster is roughly the memory's trace.
Common misuse
“Memories are stored like a videotape in one particular spot” is wrong. A memory is spread across the brain, and it gets rewritten each time it is retrieved.
