Dissociation

Concept·解离

A separation of functions that normally integrate, such as consciousness, memory and identity.

School
Hypnosis, Hysteria and Dissociation
In plain words
Janet first used the term to describe strong emotional experiences that are not integrated into personal memory and instead exist separately from awareness. Ranging from everyday “spacing out” to severe dissociative disorders, it is a continuum.
Example
You drive a familiar route and arrive at your destination without remembering the drive. This is mild dissociation.
Common misuse
Treating all dissociation as a disease. Mild dissociation is very common. Dissociative identity disorder should also not be confused with schizophrenia.
