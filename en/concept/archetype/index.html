Archetype

Concept·原型

Typical figures that recur in dreams and myths.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
Jung's term for typical patterns in the collective unconscious, such as the mother, the hero, the sage or the trickster. They are expressed through symbols.
Example
Stories of the “hallero's journey” appear in every culture: leaving home, facing trials, returning.
Common misuse
Online, archetypes are often used to label every person with a “type.” That is neither Jung's intent nor backed by any measurement.
