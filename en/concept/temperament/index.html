Temperament

Concept·气质

Innate styles of reacting, such as being easily aroused or easily calmed.

School
Ecological Systems Theory
In plain words
Differences present from birth in the intensity of reactions, the speed of adapting and the basic emotional tone. They have some genetic basis and are also shaped by the environment.
Example
Some babies cry and fuss in a new environment, while others eat and sleep as usual.
Common misuse
The claim that “children are simply born that way” is an oversimplification. Temperament and parenting interact.
