Limbic System

Concept·边缘系统

A collective name for brain structures involved in emotion, motivation and memory.

School
Affective Neuroscience
In plain words
Refers to structures deep in the brain closely tied to emotion and memory, such as the amygdala, hippocampus and cingulate gyrus. It is a convenient category, not a clearly bounded “emotion organ.”
Example
Your heart races when you are suddenly startled. The amygdala is involved in the rapid response to threat.
Common misuse
Saying “reason lives in the cortex and emotion in the limbic system, and the two are opposed.” Modern research shows that emotion depends on networks spread across the whole brain, and the cortex is involved too.
