Deindividuation

Concept·去个性化

In a group, individual self-awareness and sense of responsibility decline.

School
Crowd Psychology
In plain words
Researchers after Le Bon found that when people are anonymous, in large crowds and emotionally aroused, they are more likely to do things they would not normally do, whether good or bad.
Example
Fans in the stands shout coarse words they would never use elsewhere.
Common misuse
Believing that “people lose their reason as soon as they join a crowd.” Research shows that group behavior often follows the group norms of the moment and is not necessarily destructive.
