Affective Consciousness

Concept·情感意识

Feeling itself is the most basic form of consciousness, arising from deep in the brainstem.

School
Neuropsychoanalysis
In plain words
Solms and colleagues propose that consciousness does not come only from the cortex. A more basic “feeling” arises from the arousal systems of the brainstem. This is close to the psychoanalytic emphasis on desire and affect.
Example
Even when you are not thinking about anything in particular, you can still sense whether you feel “comfortable” or “uncomfortable.”
Common misuse
Treating it as a broadly accepted conclusion. It is a contested hypothesis within the neuropsychoanalytic school.
