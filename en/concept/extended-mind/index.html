Extended Mind

Concept·延展心智

Cognitive processes can extend into tools and the environment outside the body.

School
Embodied Cognition
In plain words
Clark and Chalmers proposed that if an external tool, such as a notebook, functionally takes on the role of memory, then it can count as part of the cognitive system.
Example
A person who habitually stores phone numbers in their phone has handed part of their memory load over to the phone.
Common misuse
Taking “the extended mind” to mean “phones make people stupid.” It is a philosophical claim about where cognition's boundaries lie, not a verdict on whether technology is good or bad.
