Social Proof

Concept·社会认同（从众参照）

Looking at what others do to decide what you should do.

School
Conformity and Obedience
In plain words
When uncertain, people treat others' behavior as a clue to what is correct.
Example
If a restaurant has a line at the door, you assume the food must be good.
Common misuse
Do not confuse it with the “identity” in social identity theory. Here it means using others' behavior as a reference, not a sense of belonging.
