Good-Enough Mother

Concept·足够好的母亲

No need to be perfect, only reliable enough.

School
Object Relations
In plain words
At first, the caregiver closely adapts to the baby. Then gradually, reasonable failures occur, so the child learns to tolerate frustration.
Example
The baby cries, and Mom sometimes does not come right away. But most of the time, she responds.
Common misuse
Treating it as a heavy burden of “motherly duty” misreads it. Winnicott's point was precisely to reduce the pressure of perfection.
