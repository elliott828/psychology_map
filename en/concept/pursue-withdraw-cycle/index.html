Pursue-Withdraw Cycle

Concept·追逃循环

One partner pursues for a response, the other withdraws, and each reinforces the other in a vicious cycle.

School
Emotion-Focused Therapy
In plain words
Emotionally focused therapy holds that this is not a matter of either person's character. It is a defensive pattern both partners fall into when their need for attachment goes unheard.
Example
One person says, “Why do you always ignore me?” The other goes quiet and retreats to another room. The first pursues harder, and the second withdraws further.
Common misuse
Seeing the “pursuer” as the one who likes to fight and the “withdrawer” as cold. The key to therapy is helping both partners see the cycle itself.
