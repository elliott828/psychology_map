Categorical Diagnosis

Concept·类别诊断

Dividing mental problems into discrete categories of “present” or “absent.”

School
Modern Psychiatric Classification
In plain words
A tradition pioneered by Kraepelin: patients with similar patterns of symptoms are grouped into one disorder, which makes communication, research and treatment easier.
Example
A diagnosis of “major depressive disorder” means that the number and duration of symptoms have reached a set threshold.
Common misuse
Assuming there are natural, clear boundaries between categories. Many symptoms are distributed on a continuum, and the threshold is an agreed-upon convention. This is where dimensional models offer criticism.
