Just Noticeable Difference

Concept·最小可觉差

The smallest change that can just barely be detected.

School
Psychophysics
In plain words
For a person to notice that two weights differ, the difference must reach a fixed proportion of the original weight. That barely detectable difference is called the just noticeable difference.
Example
Holding 100 grams, you cannot feel an extra 2 grams. Holding 1,000 grams, you need an extra 20 grams before you feel a change.
Common misuse
Assuming that the size of a sensation corresponds one-to-one with the physical amount. The change in sensation is closer to a logarithmic relationship than a linear one.
