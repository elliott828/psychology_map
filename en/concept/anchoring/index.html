Anchoring Effect

Concept·锚定效应

A number heard first pulls later estimates off course.

School
Heuristics and Biases
In plain words
Before estimating an unknown quantity, seeing a number, even an irrelevant one, pulls the estimate toward it.
Example
A store prints a high original price, so the discounted price looks like a bargain.
Common misuse
Often exaggerated into “you cannot resist it at all.” The size of the effect varies with the situation and the expertise of the person.
