Preregistration

Concept·预注册

Writing down hypotheses and the analysis plan before doing the study.

School
Replication Crisis and Open Science
In plain words
Publicly registering a research plan before collecting data makes it hard to “manufacture significance” afterward by changing the hypothesis or switching analyses.
Example
A researcher states in advance, “I will compare groups A and B on outcome 2.” Afterward, only that result is reported, and the other analyses are labeled as exploratory.
Common misuse
This does not mean exploratory research has no value. It simply asks researchers to separate exploration from testing.
