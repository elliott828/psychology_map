Case Formulation

Concept·个案概念化

A therapist organizes a client's problems, their causes and what maintains them into a testable explanation.

School
Birth of Clinical Psychology
In plain words
It is not about attaching a diagnostic label. It answers: why do these difficulties appear at this point, which factors keep them going, and where should work begin?
Example
Two people both have “insomnia.” One is caused by job stress, the other by an irregular schedule, and the approaches are completely different.
Common misuse
Believing that once a diagnosis is named, formulation is unnecessary. People with the same diagnosis may need entirely different plans.
