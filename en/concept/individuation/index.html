Individuation

Concept·个体化

The process of becoming a whole, unique self.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
Gradually integrating unconscious contents into consciousness so that the personality becomes more complete. It usually happens after middle age.
Example
A person in midlife begins to question the goals others have set for them and rediscovers their own interests.
Common misuse
Often equated with “being yourself” or “self-actualization.” Jung stressed that it requires facing the shadow, and that it has no endpoint.
