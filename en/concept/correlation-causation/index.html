Correlation Is Not Causation

Concept·相关不等于因果

Two things changing together does not mean one causes the other.

School
Individual Differences
In plain words
Two correlated variables may be linked because A causes B, B causes A, or a third factor affects both.
Example
Ice cream sales and drowning deaths rise together because of summer weather, not because ice cream causes drowning.
Common misuse
Sometimes misused to mean “correlation is meaningless.” Correlation is a clue, and further study design is needed to test causation.
