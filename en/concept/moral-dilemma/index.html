Moral Dilemma

Concept·道德两难

A hypothetical situation with no simple right answer, used to observe how people reason.

School
Moral Development
In plain words
Kohlberg used stories such as “Heinz steals the drug” and focused not on whether the answer was “stealing is right or wrong,” but on which level of reasoning the justification belongs to.
Example
Asked whether a husband should steal medicine for his seriously ill wife, one child says, “He would get caught,” and another says, “Life matters more than property.”
Common misuse
Believing the test judges whether a person is “moral.” It measures reasoning style, which is not the same thing as real-world behavior.
