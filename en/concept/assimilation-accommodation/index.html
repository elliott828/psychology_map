Assimilation and Accommodation

Concept·同化与顺应

Understanding something new through an existing framework, or changing the framework to fit it.

School
Cognitive Development Theory
In plain words
When new information fits into existing ideas, this is assimilation. When it does not fit, the ideas must be revised, and that is accommodation.
Example
A child who first sees a cat calls it a “dog” (assimilation). After being corrected, the child creates a new category for “cat” (accommodation).
Common misuse
Often memorized as a pair of terms, while the key point is overlooked: the drive to learn comes from the imbalance between the two.
