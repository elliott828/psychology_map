Neuroticism

Concept·神经质

A tendency to readily experience negative emotions such as anxiety, anger and low mood.

School
Factor-Analytic Personality Models
In plain words
Eysenck treated it as a basic dimension of personality. People who score high have larger emotional swings and react strongly to stress. People who score low are more stable.
Example
Faced with the same curt email, a person high in neuroticism may lie awake all night, while someone low in neuroticism reads it, shrugs and forgets it.
Common misuse
Equating it with “mental illness.” It is a difference in degree within the normal population, and most people fall somewhere in the middle.
