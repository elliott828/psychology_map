Attachment Styles

Concept·依恋类型（安全／焦虑／回避）

Several common ways of relating intimately, from infancy into adult relationships.

School
Attachment Theory
In plain words
Stable tendencies in what people expect from close relationships, such as secure, anxious and avoidant. They are best seen as dimensions along a continuum, and they can change.
Example
An anxiously attached person constantly worries that a partner has not replied to a message. An avoidantly attached person pulls away as a relationship grows closer.
Common misuse
The most common misuse is treating it as a lifelong label, slapping a type onto yourself or others and treating that as settled.

Related myths

  • “Your attachment style was set in childhood, and it determines your love life for the rest of your life” Misleading
