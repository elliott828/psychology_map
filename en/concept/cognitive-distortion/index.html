Cognitive Distortion

Concept·认知扭曲

Common biased habits of thinking about problems.

School
Cognitive Therapies
In plain words
Patterns such as catastrophizing, all-or-nothing thinking, mind reading and overgeneralizing, which make things seem worse than they really are.
Example
After one slip in a presentation, you think, “I'm finished. Everyone thinks I'm an idiot.”
Common misuse
Do not use the term to accuse others of “distorting.” It is a tool for checking your own thoughts.
