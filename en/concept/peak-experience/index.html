Peak Experience

Concept·高峰体验

A brief, intense moment of meaningful experience.

School
Hierarchy of Needs and Self-Actualization
In plain words
Moments in which a person feels especially whole, joyful and connected to the world, such as while climbing a mountain, listening to music or in moments of love.
Example
The awe and stillness of watching the sunrise from a mountaintop.
Common misuse
Often treated as a goal that can be achieved by pursuing it. In fact, it usually arrives by chance, as a by-product.
