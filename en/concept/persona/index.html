Persona

Concept·人格面具

The role you present to others in society.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
The “mask” put on to meet social expectations, such as a professional role or a polite self.
Example
At work you are calm and professional, and only at home do you show your playful, relaxed side.
Common misuse
It is not “falseness.” It is normal social adaptation. The problem comes when you over-identify with the mask and forget who you are underneath.
