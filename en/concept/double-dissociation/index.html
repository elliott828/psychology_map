Double Dissociation

Concept·双重分离

Two opposite types of damage show that two functions are independent of each other.

School
Neuropsychology
In plain words
Patient A loses function 1 but keeps function 2, and Patient B shows the reverse. That suggests the two functions depend on different brain mechanisms.
Example
Some patients can recognize objects but cannot recognize faces, while others show the reverse. This suggests that object recognition and face recognition are at least partly independent.
Common misuse
Often read as “one brain area does one thing.” The finding only shows that the two abilities can come apart. It does not show that each maps onto a single brain area.
