Stimulus Generalization

Concept·刺激泛化

A learned response spreads to similar stimuli.

School
Classical Conditioning
In plain words
A response formed to one stimulus also appears toward similar stimuli. The more similar they are, the stronger the response.
Example
A child bitten by a dog becomes afraid of all large dogs afterward.
Common misuse
Generalization does not mean “overthinking.” It is a normal feature of learning, and it is also one of the mechanisms by which anxiety spreads.
