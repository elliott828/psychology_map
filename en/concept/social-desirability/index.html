Social Desirability

Concept·社会赞许性

When answering questionnaires, people tend to give answers that make them look good.

School
Test Theory (Reliability and Validity)
In plain words
On self-report items, people tend to choose answers that present themselves favorably, whether consciously or not.
Example
Asked “Would you cheat to win?” most people answer “No.”
Common misuse
This does not mean “everyone is lying.” It shows that purely self-reported measures need other evidence to back them up.
