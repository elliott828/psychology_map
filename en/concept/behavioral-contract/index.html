Behavioral Contract

Concept·行为契约

Goals and rewards are written down as an agreement in advance.

School
Applied Behavior Analysis
In plain words
Both parties write down the behavior to be changed and the reward for achieving it, then follow the agreement.
Example
A child and parents agree: finish homework five days a week, and get an extra half hour of gaming on the weekend.
Common misuse
Often dismissed as “buying off children with candy.” What matters is that the behavior is clearly defined, the reward is given promptly, and the reward can be gradually withdrawn.
