Mirroring

Concept·镜映

Caregivers “see” the child with admiration and responsiveness, so the child feels valued.

School
Self Psychology
In plain words
Kohut believed that children need to be genuinely seen and affirmed, and that a healthy sense of self develops from this. Long-term lack of it leaves the self fragile.
Example
A child draws a picture, and the parent looks at it carefully and says, “You put a lot of care into this.” The child feels seen.
Common misuse
Treating mirroring as nothing more than heaping on praise. It emphasizes responding to the child's real state, not indiscriminate compliments.
