Negative Reinforcement

Concept·负强化

Removing something unpleasant makes a behavior more frequent. It is not punishment.

School
Operant Conditioning
In plain words
Negative reinforcement is a way of increasing a behavior: something unpleasant is taken away after the behavior. Punishment, by contrast, reduces a behavior.
Example
You take a painkiller and the headache goes away, so next time you get a headache you are more likely to take one. You switch off a jarring alarm and are more likely to get up right away the next time.
Common misuse
Here “negative” means “removal,” not “bad.” It is often mistaken for punishment.
