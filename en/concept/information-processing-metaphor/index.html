Information-Processing Metaphor

Concept·信息加工隐喻

Treating the mind as a system that takes in, encodes, stores and retrieves information.

School
Cognitive Revolution
In plain words
The cognitive revolution borrowed the language of computers and information theory. Information enters, passes through a chain of processing stages, and yields an output, and each stage can be studied on its own.
Example
At a noisy party you hear someone say your name. The sound is first filtered, then recognized, and then it triggers your attention to shift toward the speaker.
Common misuse
Taking the brain to be a literal computer. The metaphor helps generate testable models, but the brain works very differently from current computers.
