The Unconscious

Concept·无意识

Mental processes that are outside awareness but still shape behavior.

School
Classical Psychoanalysis
In plain words
Much of our perception, motivation and judgment happens without our noticing. Modern cognitive science has amply confirmed this, though its content differs from Freud's account.
Example
You feel an unexplained dislike toward someone and cannot say why. In fact, they may remind you of someone from your past.
Common misuse
Often mythologized as a place “full of secrets.” In reality, most unconscious processing is automatic and ordinary.
