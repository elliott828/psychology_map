Loss Aversion

Concept·损失厌恶

The pain of losing is greater than the pleasure of gaining the same amount.

School
Heuristics and Biases
In plain words
For the same sum, people usually react more strongly to a loss than to a gain. A common estimate is about twice as strongly.
Example
Losing 100 yuan hurts, and it takes a gain of 200 yuan to feel that the two balance out.
Common misuse
Often used to explain every case of “hating to let go,” but the strength of the effect varies a great deal across people and situations.
