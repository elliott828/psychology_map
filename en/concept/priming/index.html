Priming

Concept·启动效应

Encountering one stimulus shapes how related content is processed afterward.

School
Cognitive Psychology
In plain words
Seeing one word makes related words easier and faster to recognize. This is called semantic priming.
Example
After seeing “doctor,” you recognize “nurse” faster.
Common misuse
The biggest misuse is stretching it into “glancing at a word changes your behavior.” Many social priming effects of this kind have failed to replicate.
