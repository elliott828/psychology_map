Differentiation of Self

Concept·自我分化

Staying true to your own thoughts and positions while remaining close to others.

School
Family Systems Therapy
In plain words
Bowen's concept: the ability to separate thinking from feeling under emotional pressure, to avoid being pulled into family emotions, without cutting off contact entirely.
Example
When relatives push you to get married, you listen patiently and state your own view clearly, without an argument.
Common misuse
Often misunderstood as “keeping distance from family.” It actually means being able to be close and independent at the same time.
