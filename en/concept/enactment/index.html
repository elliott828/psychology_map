Enactment

Concept·共演

Therapist and client unknowingly act out the old relationship patterns of both.

School
Relational Psychoanalysis
In plain words
Relational psychoanalysis holds that therapy is not one-way observation. The therapist's own emotions and reactions are also drawn in, replaying the relationships the client is used to.
Example
A client often feels abandoned. The therapist, without meaning to, is late a few times, and the client's response becomes a replay of an old relationship.
Common misuse
Treating enactment as the therapist's “mistake.” The relational approach holds that it is unavoidable. What matters is noticing it and talking about it together afterward.
