Effect Size

Concept·效应量

How large an effect is, not just whether it exists.

School
Replication Crisis and Open Science
In plain words
Statistical significance only says that a difference is unlikely to be pure chance. Effect size tells you how large the difference is.
Example
A method that raises average scores by only 0.1 points can be “significant” with a large enough sample, yet have almost no practical meaning.
Common misuse
Often overlooked. Many news reports say a result is “significant” without saying how large the effect is.
