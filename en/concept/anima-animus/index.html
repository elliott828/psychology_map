Anima and Animus

Concept·阿尼玛与阿尼姆斯

The image of the feminine in a man's psyche, and of the masculine in a woman's.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
Jung believed that everyone's unconscious contains an image of the opposite sex, which shapes how they project onto intimate partners.
Example
Why someone keeps being drawn to one particular type of person may partly be explained by this.
Common misuse
This binary framing of gender reflects strong limitations of its time and has little empirical testing. It should be treated as a metaphor.
