Validity

Concept·效度

Whether a test measures what it is meant to measure.

School
Test Theory (Reliability and Validity)
In plain words
How much evidence supports the meaning of a test score: it relates to what it should relate to, is unrelated to what it should be unrelated to, and predicts the outcomes it should predict.
Example
Measuring someone's intelligence by their shoe size could have high reliability, but its validity would be close to zero.
Common misuse
Often described as a property of the test itself. In fact, validity applies to the interpretation of scores for a particular purpose.
