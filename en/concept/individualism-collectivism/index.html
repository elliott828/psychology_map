Individualism vs. Collectivism

Concept·个人主义与集体主义

Whether a society emphasizes the individual or the group.

School
Cultural Psychology
In plain words
One cultural dimension: individualist cultures value personal goals and autonomy, while collectivist cultures value group harmony and responsibility.
Example
“Be yourself” is often encouraged in the United States, while in many East Asian families one is expected to consider what family members think.
Common misuse
It is wrong to use it to label every individual. There is great variation within any culture, and behavior changes with the situation.
