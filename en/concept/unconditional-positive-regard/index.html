Unconditional Positive Regard

Concept·无条件积极关注

Acceptance that does not depend on how you perform.

School
Person-Centered Therapy
In plain words
Accepting a person as they are, without the condition that they must meet your demands. This does not mean approving of every behavior.
Example
A child does poorly on a test, and a parent first says, “I can see you're really upset,” rather than, “Why didn't you study harder?”
Common misuse
Often misunderstood as “unconditional agreement” or “never setting limits.” Rogers meant accepting the person, not endorsing every behavior.
