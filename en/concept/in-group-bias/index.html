In-Group Bias

Concept·内群体偏好

A preference for members of the group you belong to.

School
Social Identity Theory
In plain words
When judging, allocating resources and evaluating, people tend to favor members of their own group.
Example
People from the same hometown, school or sports team more easily win trust and help.
Common misuse
This is not the same as hostility toward outsiders. Often it simply means being better to one's own group, not worse to others.
