Mindfulness

Concept·正念

Consciously paying attention to the present moment, without judgment.

School
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction
In plain words
Kabat-Zinn's definition: paying attention in a particular way, on purpose, in the present moment, and without judgment.
Example
When you eat a grape, you notice only its color, smell and texture, instead of scrolling on your phone at the same time.
Common misuse
Often equated with “relaxing” or “emptying your mind.” It refers to awareness, not to stopping thoughts from appearing.
