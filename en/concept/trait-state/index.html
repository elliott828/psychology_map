Trait vs. State

Concept·特质与状态

Stable tendencies must be distinguished from momentary states.

School
Trait Theory
In plain words
A trait is a long-lasting tendency that holds across situations. A state is how you feel and act at a given moment. The two are different.
Example
A usually calm person feels anxious before an exam today. This is state anxiety and does not necessarily mean high trait anxiety.
Common misuse
A common mistake is treating the result of a single measurement as proof that “this is simply who you are.”
