Synapse

Concept·突触

The contact point where signals pass between two neurons.

School
Neuron Doctrine
In plain words
The end of one neuron does not connect directly to the next. A very narrow gap separates them, and signals usually cross it via chemical messengers called neurotransmitters.
Example
As you read this sentence, visual signals pass through countless synapses, one stop after another, before they become meaning for you.
Common misuse
Thinking of neurons as wires that are simply “connected.” Synaptic transmission can be adjusted in strength, and that is exactly why learning can change the brain.
