Shadow

Concept·阴影

The side of yourself you refuse to admit.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
The traits a person will not accept, which have been pushed into the unconscious, and which are often projected onto others.
Example
Someone who is very bothered by “other people being selfish” may be refusing to admit their own desire to have things for themselves.
Common misuse
The shadow is not just “the bad side.” It also contains suppressed strengths and vitality.
