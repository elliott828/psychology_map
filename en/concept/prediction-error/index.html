Prediction Error

Concept·预测误差

The gap between outcome and expectation is what drives learning.

School
Classical Conditioning
In plain words
The brain updates only when what happens differs from what was expected. Events that match expectations exactly bring no new learning.
Example
You suddenly find out the owner of a coffee shop remembers your name. You feel especially happy because you did not expect it.
Common misuse
Often stretched into “whenever you are surprised, you are learning.” In fact, the error signal is just one mechanism of learning.
