Rumination

Concept·反刍思维

Chewing over the same problem repeatedly without making progress.

School
Cognitive Therapies
In plain words
Repeatedly thinking about one's misery, its causes and consequences without working to solve anything. It is closely linked to depression and anxiety.
Example
On a sleepless night, you keep asking yourself, “Why am I so hopeless?”
Common misuse
Not the same as “thinking carefully.” Rumination makes people more discouraged, while problem solving helps them move forward.
