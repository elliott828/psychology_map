Critical / Sensitive Period

Concept·关键期／敏感期

Some abilities form most easily during specific stages of development.

School
Ethology
In plain words
There is a window when the brain is especially sensitive to certain kinds of experience. After it closes, the same learning becomes harder, though not always impossible.
Example
Young children pick up an accent far more easily than adults do. A child with amblyopia (lazy eye) recovers better vision the earlier it is corrected.
Common misuse
Often misused as “your fate is sealed by age three.” Most sensitive periods fade gradually, and there is still room for catch-up learning later.
