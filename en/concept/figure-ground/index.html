Figure-Ground

Concept·图形与背景

Perception always treats some parts as the “figure” and the rest as the “ground.”

School
Gestalt Psychology
In plain words
When looking at a picture, we automatically treat some parts as prominent objects and the rest as background. The two can swap places.
Example
The Rubin vase can be seen as a vase, or as two faces in profile looking at each other.
Common misuse
Sometimes treated as a “personality test.” This is just a law of perception and has nothing to do with personality.
