Groupthink

Concept·群体迷思

In the pursuit of unity, a group stops rigorously testing its decisions.

School
Field Theory and Group Dynamics
In plain words
Highly cohesive groups that crave agreement may suppress dissent and make very risky decisions.
Example
Everyone nods in a meeting, no one raises objections, and only later does it become clear that the plan had obvious flaws.
Common misuse
As an explanatory model, it draws mostly on case analyses after the fact, and experimental support is limited. It should not be treated as a law that always applies.
