Openness to Experience

Concept·开放性

Interest in and acceptance of new ideas, art and experiences.

School
Big Five Personality
In plain words
One dimension of the Big Five. People high in openness are curious, imaginative and willing to try new things. People low in openness prefer the familiar, practical and stable.
Example
Willing to pick an unfamiliar small restaurant on a trip, and fond of reading books from different fields.
Common misuse
Treating openness as “broad-mindedness,” “morality” or “intelligence.” It is somewhat correlated with intelligence but is not the same thing, and neither end of the scale is better than the other.
