Retrieval Practice

Concept·提取练习（测试效应）

Trying to recall something on your own helps you learn it better than rereading.

School
Experimental Study of Memory
In plain words
After studying, actively trying to recall the material is better for long-term retention than reading it again.
Example
Quizzing yourself with flashcards, or closing the book and retelling the passage you just studied.
Common misuse
It should not be reduced to cramming for tests, since understanding the material is still needed. Nor does it mean “the more quizzes, the better.”
