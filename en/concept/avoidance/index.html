Avoidance and Safety Behaviors

Concept·回避与安全行为

Relieves anxiety in the short term, but keeps fear alive in the long term.

School
Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy
In plain words
Staying away from certain situations to avoid anxiety, or relying on “safety” behaviors. This brings immediate relief but prevents you from discovering that the danger is smaller than imagined.
Example
Someone who has always been afraid to give speeches makes excuses to skip every opportunity, so the anxiety never goes down.
Common misuse
It is not “cowardice.” It is a natural human reaction, and it changes gradually, with support.
