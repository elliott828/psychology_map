Predisposing, Precipitating and Perpetuating Factors

Concept·易感、诱发与维持因素

When analyzing a problem, causes are sorted into three categories by timing.

School
Biopsychosocial Model
In plain words
Predisposing factors are vulnerabilities that were already present. Precipitating factors are the events that trigger the problem. Perpetuating factors are the behaviors and circumstances that keep the problem going. Each of the three can include biological, psychological and social elements.
Example
Test anxiety: the predisposition is a naturally nervous temperament, the trigger is one bad exam, and the perpetuating factors are the avoidance and repeated self-blame that follow.
Common misuse
Looking only for “one root cause.” Clinically, what started a problem is often different from what keeps it going, and treatment usually targets the perpetuating factors.
