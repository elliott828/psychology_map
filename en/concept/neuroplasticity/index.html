Neuroplasticity

Concept·神经可塑性

The brain changes its connections and structure in response to experience.

School
Hebbian Learning and Plasticity
In plain words
Connections between neurons strengthen with use and weaken with disuse. The changes continue into adulthood, but they are smaller than in childhood.
Example
Taxi drivers have been found to have a larger back portion of the hippocampus. Blind people reading Braille also recruit the visual cortex to process touch.
Common misuse
Often inflated into a catch-all slogan that you can change anything you want. Real change takes a great deal of repetition and time.

Related myths

  • “People only use 10 percent of their brain” False
