Intrinsic Motivation

Concept·内在动机

Doing something because the activity itself is interesting.

School
Self-Determination Theory
In plain words
Doing something out of interest, curiosity and a sense of accomplishment, rather than for a reward or to avoid punishment.
Example
Doing puzzles or writing code not for money, but simply because it is fun.
Common misuse
“Intrinsic” does not mean “no outside support is needed.” An environment that supports autonomy, competence and connection greatly influences it.
