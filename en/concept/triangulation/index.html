Triangulation

Concept·三角关系

When two people are tense, a third person is pulled in to ease it.

School
Family Systems Therapy
In plain words
Conflict between two people is diverted by bringing in a third person, such as a child or a relative. The problem itself, however, stays unresolved.
Example
During a couple's argument, one partner keeps complaining to the child, and the child gets drawn in.
Common misuse
It is not a judgment about “who is to blame.” It is a description of a relationship pattern.
