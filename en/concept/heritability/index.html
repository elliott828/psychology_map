Heritability

Concept·遗传率

In a population, how much of the variation in a trait is linked to genetic differences.

School
Behavior Genetics
In plain words
It is a population statistic describing the source of differences. It is not a measure of how much of an individual's trait is “due to genes.”
Example
Height has a heritability of about 80 percent. This means that differences in height across a population mainly come from genetic differences. It does not mean that 80 percent of any one person's height is determined by genes.
Common misuse
The most common misuse is treating heritability as meaning “unchangeable.” Better nutrition can make a whole generation taller, yet heritability stays almost the same.
