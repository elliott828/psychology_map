Modeling

Concept·榜样与示范

People watch what others do and then copy it.

School
Social Learning Theory
In plain words
People observe and imitate others' behavior, especially people who are similar to them, respected, and seem to get good results.
Example
A child learns to swear not because anyone taught them to, but because they heard the adults talk that way.
Common misuse
“Television makes children violent” is an oversimplification. Imitation does happen, but long-term effects also depend on the individual and the environment.
