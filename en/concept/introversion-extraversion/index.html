Introversion and Extraversion

Concept·内向与外向

Whether energy is directed more inward or toward the outside world.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
Jung first proposed this pair of concepts. Modern personality psychology treats it as a continuum rather than two types.
Example
After a party, some people feel recharged, while others feel tired and need time alone.
Common misuse
Often mistaken for two fixed categories. Most people sit somewhere in the middle, and their position changes with the situation.
