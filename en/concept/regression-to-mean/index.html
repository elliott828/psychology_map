Regression to the Mean

Concept·回归平均

After an extreme result, the next result is usually closer to average.

School
Individual Differences
In plain words
For any measure with a random component, an extremely high or low reading is usually followed by a reading closer to the average. This is simply a statistical pattern.
Example
People who did unusually well on an exam usually do less well the next time. This does not mean they have declined.
Common misuse
The most common misuse is treating it as the effect of an intervention. “Grades fell after praise and improved after criticism” is often just regression to the mean.
