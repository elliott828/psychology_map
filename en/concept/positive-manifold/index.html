Positive Manifold

Concept·正向相关格局

Scores on different intelligence tests are almost always positively correlated with one another.

School
General Intelligence and Factor Analysis
In plain words
Spearman found that scores on verbal, mathematical and spatial tests tend to rise and fall together. This allows a shared common factor to be extracted, called g.
Example
People who score high on a vocabulary test also tend to score well on numerical reasoning.
Common misuse
Treating g as an “organ” in the brain or a fixed gift. g is a statistical common factor, and its biological basis and malleability are still debated.
