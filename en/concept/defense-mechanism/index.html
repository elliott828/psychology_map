Defense Mechanism

Concept·防御机制

Mental strategies people use, often without awareness, to reduce anxiety.

School
Ego Psychology
In plain words
Ways of coping that people use without realizing it when faced with thoughts or feelings they cannot easily bear, such as denial, rationalization and displacement.
Example
After being criticized, you immediately say, “He was just out to get me” (projection), or you make a joke to deflect the pain (humor).
Common misuse
The most common misuse is using it to “diagnose” other people. If someone rejects your view, they are called “defensive,” and that makes them impossible to argue with.
