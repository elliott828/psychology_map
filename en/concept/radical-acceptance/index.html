Radical Acceptance

Concept·彻底接纳

Fully acknowledging that reality is what it is, without approving of it.

School
Third-Wave Therapies
In plain words
A skill from DBT: once you stop fighting facts that have already happened, you have the energy to change what can be changed.
Example
After being laid off, you first accept that it has happened, and only then can you plan your next steps carefully.
Common misuse
Often misunderstood as “giving up” or “endorsing the wrong.” In fact, it is the precondition for change.
