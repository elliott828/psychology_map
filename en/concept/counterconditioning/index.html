Counterconditioning

Concept·对抗条件作用

Repeatedly pairing a fear-provoking stimulus with a response that is opposite to fear, such as relaxation.

School
Behavior Therapy
In plain words
A person afraid of dogs practices deep relaxation while approaching a dog gradually from a distance. Over time, “seeing a dog” becomes linked with “relaxation” instead of “tension.”
Example
Someone afraid of elevators first imagines an elevator while doing relaxation exercises, and then gradually walks up to a real one.
Common misuse
Thinking this means “forcing yourself to face it.” It proceeds in small steps, and at each step the client is kept in a state they can tolerate.
