Learning Styles

Concept·学习风格

Claims like “I am a visual learner” lack supporting evidence.

School
Multiple Intelligences
In plain words
The idea that each person has a preferred way of receiving information, and that matching teaching to that preference leads to better learning, has not been supported in controlled experiments.
Example
Students labeled “visual learners” who were shown only diagrams, and “auditory learners” who were only lectured to, did not perform better as a result.
Common misuse
This is one of the most widely believed education myths. Preferences are real, but there is no evidence that teaching to a preference makes learning more effective.

Related myths

  • “Everyone has a learning style (visual or auditory), and teaching that matches it works better” False
