Stereotype

Concept·刻板印象

A simplified generalization about a category of people.

School
Social Cognition
In plain words
Summarizing features believed to belong to a group, and then applying them to infer things about each individual in it.
Example
Believing that “science students are all bad at socializing,” and forming an opinion of a particular science student before you even meet them.
Common misuse
Stereotypes do not require prejudice to exist. They are a by-product of the mind's effort-saving shortcuts, but they can lead to unfair judgments.
