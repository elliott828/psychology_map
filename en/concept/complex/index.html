Complex

Concept·情结

A cluster of unconscious feelings and memories built around an emotional experience.

School
Analytical Psychology
In plain words
Memories and ideas charged with strong emotion gather into a knot that is triggered whenever something related comes up.
Example
Someone who was treated harshly as a child becomes tense whenever they meet an authority figure.
Common misuse
In everyday speech, “having a complex about something” is often stretched to mean “a hobby or fixation,” which differs from the original meaning.
