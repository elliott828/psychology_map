Binding Problem

Concept·“结合问题”

Color, shape and motion are processed separately. Why do we see a unified object?

School
Cognitive Neuroscience
In plain words
The brain processes an object's color, shape, location and motion in different regions, yet we experience it as one whole. How these pieces get joined together is still not fully understood.
Example
You see a red ball roll past. Red, round and moving are different patterns of neural activity, yet you do not mistakenly assign the red to the square next to it.
Common misuse
Seeing does not require a tiny person inside the brain assembling the picture. That idea itself is a misunderstanding to avoid.
