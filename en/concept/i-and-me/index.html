The I and the Me

Concept·主我与客我

James split the self into the part that knows and the part that is known.

School
Functionalism
In plain words
The “me” is the self you can describe: your body, roles and reputation. The “I” is the part that is thinking and experiencing, and that ties all of this together.
Example
When you say “I am an introvert,” the “me” is what is being described, and the “I” is the one saying it.
Common misuse
Imagining the “I” as a little person hidden in the brain. James treated it as the flow of thinking itself, not as a separate entity.
