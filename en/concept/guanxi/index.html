Guanxi

Concept·关系

Networks of personal connection in Chinese society, held together by reciprocity and obligation.

School
Indigenous Psychology
In plain words
Indigenous psychology researchers emphasize that in Chinese interpersonal life, guanxi is not just a matter of contacts. It is a network with obligations, emotions and face, and it shapes choices and decisions.
Example
When you need something done, you first think, “Do I know anyone who can help?” and ask through a connection rather than going through the formal process.
Common misuse
Equating guanxi simply with “backdoor dealings.” It includes family and friendship ties as well as informal mutual support.
