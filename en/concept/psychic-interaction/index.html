Psychic Interaction (Seishin Kōgo Sayō)

Concept·精神交互作用

A cycle in which attention focused on discomfort makes the discomfort grow stronger.

School
Morita Therapy
In plain words
Morita believed the core of neurosis is excessive attention to a certain sensation. The sensation is magnified, which draws more attention, forming a vicious cycle.
Example
Lying in bed watching the clock and worrying about insomnia, the more you worry, the more awake you become.
Common misuse
Believing it can be broken by simply “trying harder to overcome it.” Morita therapy instead accepts that the symptom exists and turns attention to doing what needs to be done.
