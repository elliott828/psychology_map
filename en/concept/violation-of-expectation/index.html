Violation-of-Expectation

Concept·违背预期范式

Infants who look longer at “impossible” events reveal what they expect.

School
Infant Cognition
In plain words
Since infants cannot talk, researchers show them “normal” and “impossible” scenes, such as an object passing through a solid wall. If they look longer at the impossible scene, it suggests they have expectations about the physical world.
Example
A toy is hidden behind a screen, and when the screen turns, the toy appears to pass straight through it. Infants stare at this event for longer.
Common misuse
Taking “looking longer” to mean “understood.” It can also reflect interest in novel stimuli, so interpretation requires proper control conditions.
