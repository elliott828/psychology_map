Inattentional Blindness

Concept·非注意盲视

Focusing on one thing can make you miss other obvious things.

School
Cognitive Psychology
In plain words
When attention is taken up by something, even objects in plain view may go unnoticed.
Example
In a study where viewers counted basketball passes, about half did not notice a gorilla walking through the frame.
Common misuse
This shows that attention is limited. It does not mean that some people have poor eyesight, and it does not mean you will miss things at all times.
