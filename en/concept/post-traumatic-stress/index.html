Post-Traumatic Stress Reactions

Concept·创伤后应激反应

Lasting reactions that follow an extreme event.

School
Trauma Studies and PTSD
In plain words
Intrusive memories, avoidance, negative changes in mood and thinking, and hypervigilance, lasting more than a month.
Example
For months after a car accident, you feel your heart race at the sound of brakes, and you are afraid to drive.
Common misuse
Not everyone who experiences trauma develops it. Most people recover naturally, and they should not be given the label of a patient.
