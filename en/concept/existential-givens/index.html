Existential Givens

Concept·存在的既定事实

Death, freedom, isolation and meaninglessness: four things no one can avoid.

School
Existential Psychotherapy
In plain words
Yalom's list of life conditions: we will die, we must make choices and take responsibility for them, we are fundamentally alone, and the world does not supply meaning on its own.
Example
The death of a loved one, a major choice, or facing the quiet of a solitary night can each bring these facts into focus.
Common misuse
This does not mean pessimism. Existential therapy holds that facing these facts can help people live more authentically.
