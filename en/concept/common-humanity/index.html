Common Humanity

Concept·共通人性

Recognizing that pain and failure are experiences everyone encounters.

School
Self-Compassion
In plain words
Neff describes three components of self-compassion: being kind to yourself, recognizing common humanity, and being mindfully aware without being swept away by emotion. Common humanity reduces the sense of isolation that comes with feeling “only I am like this.”
Example
After a failed exam, you think, “Many people have gone through this kind of setback,” rather than, “I'm really useless.”
Common misuse
Taking it to mean “others have it worse, so I should be content.” Common humanity is not about comparison. It is about reducing isolation.
