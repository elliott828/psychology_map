Diffusion of Responsibility

Concept·责任分散

The more people present, the less responsible each person feels.

School
Conformity and Obedience
In plain words
When many people are around, each one assumes that someone else will take care of it, and as a result, no one acts.
Example
Someone collapses in an elevator, and the people there look at each other, each waiting for someone else to speak up first.
Common misuse
This does not prove that “human nature is cold.” Clearly asking one specific person for help usually breaks the pattern.
