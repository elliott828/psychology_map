Cross-Situational Consistency

Concept·跨情境一致性

How consistent a person's behavior is across different settings.

School
Person-Situation Debate
In plain words
Mischel pointed out that a person's behavior in different situations is not strongly correlated. Yet for the same kind of situation, behavior is quite stable, in an “if-then” pattern.
Example
Someone who is assertive in meetings but easygoing at home is not being inconsistent.
Common misuse
Using this to claim that “personality does not exist.” The more accurate conclusion is that personality traits predict average behavior over the long term better than they predict behavior at a particular moment.
