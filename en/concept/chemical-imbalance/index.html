Chemical Imbalance Idea

Concept·“化学失衡”说

The idea that depression and other mood disorders are caused by a shortage of brain chemicals.

School
Psychopharmacology
In plain words
A popular claim holds that depression is caused by too little serotonin or similar substances, and that medication corrects the shortfall. It grew out of early findings about how drugs work and is a simplified metaphor.
Example
“Your serotonin is low” is a phrase often heard in pharmaceutical advertisements and clinic visits.
Common misuse
Treating it as a proven cause. Research has not identified a fixed “normal level,” and the way medications work is far more complex than “filling a shortage.”
