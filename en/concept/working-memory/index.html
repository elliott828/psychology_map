Working Memory

Concept·工作记忆

A limited capacity to hold and work with several things at once.

School
Cognitive Psychology
In plain words
This is the brain's space for temporarily holding and working with information, such as keeping an intermediate result in mind during mental arithmetic. Its capacity is very small, varies from person to person, and is taken up by stress.
Example
Jotting down an address while on the phone, or doing 47 × 6 in your head by first computing 40 × 6 and holding it while adding 42.
Common misuse
Often treated as a synonym for “memory.” In fact, it is a different system from long-term memory, and it cannot be noticeably improved with a few brain-training games.
