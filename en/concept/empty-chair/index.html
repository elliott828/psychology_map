Empty Chair

Concept·空椅子

Speaking to an empty chair as if a person were sitting in it, to say what was left unsaid.

School
Gestalt Therapy
In plain words
A therapy technique: you imagine the other person in the chair and say the feelings you never expressed, or switch roles and answer as that person.
Example
A person who lost their mother speaks to an empty chair: “I miss you so much,” and “I was really angry with you back then.”
Common misuse
It is not a performance. Its purpose is to let suppressed emotions come out, and it should be used with a trained therapist present.
