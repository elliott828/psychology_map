Private Speech

Concept·私语

Children talk to themselves while doing things, to help them think and regulate their behavior.

School
Sociocultural Theory
In plain words
Vygotsky argued that children first use external speech to communicate with others, and then “internalize” it, turning it into the inner language they use when thinking.
Example
A child building with blocks mutters, “First this one, then that one.”
Common misuse
Seeing a child's self-talk as a sign of immaturity or a problem. It is actually more common when a task is difficult, and it serves as self-guidance.
