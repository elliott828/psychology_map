Autotelic Activity

Concept·自成目的的活动

The activity is its own reward.

School
Flow
In plain words
Doing something is satisfying in itself, without needing an external reason.
Example
Some people will spend hours playing chess, playing the piano or climbing mountains simply because the activity absorbs them.
Common misuse
Not everything that is fun is autotelic. The activity needs to match one's abilities with a suitable challenge.
