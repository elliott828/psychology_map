Randomized Controlled Trial

Concept·随机对照试验

Randomly assigning participants to a treatment group and a control group, then comparing outcomes.

School
Evidence-Based Practice in Psychology
In plain words
Random assignment makes the groups roughly equal in other respects, so differences can be attributed to the treatment itself. It is one of the most important sources of evidence in evidence-based practice.
Example
200 patients with depression are randomly assigned to cognitive behavioral therapy or a waitlist, and their symptoms are compared after 12 weeks.
Common misuse
Assuming the results of a randomized controlled trial apply to every individual. It gives an average effect, and individual responses can vary widely.
