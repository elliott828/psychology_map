Free Association

Concept·自由联想

Say whatever comes to mind, without filtering.

School
Classical Psychoanalysis
In plain words
The client says everything that surfaces in their mind without judgment, so that avoided themes can be found.
Example
Lying on the couch and talking about a small event from childhood, you quickly find your thoughts drifting to a current relationship.
Common misuse
This is not just “saying whatever you like” in conversation. It is a therapeutic method carried out under the guidance of a trained practitioner.
