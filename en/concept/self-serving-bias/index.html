Self-Serving Bias

Concept·自利性偏差

Credit success to yourself, blame failure on circumstances.

School
Attribution Theory
In plain words
People tend to attribute success to their own ability and failure to external factors.
Example
“I did well on the exam because I'm smart. I did badly because the questions were unfair.”
Common misuse
The effect is weaker in some East Asian contexts, and it is not universal. In people with depression, it can even be reversed.
