Secure Base

Concept·安全基地

A child uses the caregiver as a starting point for exploring.

School
Attachment Theory
In plain words
The child knows they can return to the caregiver when something is difficult, and that knowledge gives them the confidence to venture out and explore.
Example
A one-year-old playing in a park glances back at Mom now and then, then runs off to play again.
Common misuse
Often misunderstood as “clinging all the time.” Children with secure attachment are actually more willing to be independent.
