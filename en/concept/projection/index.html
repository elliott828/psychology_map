Projection

Concept·投射

Attributing your own emotions or traits to other people.

School
Classical Psychoanalysis
In plain words
Seeing in others the emotions or traits you do not want to admit in yourself.
Example
You envy a colleague but keep feeling that the colleague envies you.
Common misuse
Often misused as a debate tactic: “You say I am like this, but really you are the one who is like this.”
