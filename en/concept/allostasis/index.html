Allostasis

Concept·稳态应变

The body copes with change by shifting its “set point.”

School
Stress Physiology
In plain words
Rather than holding its internal state fixed at one value, the body anticipates needs and adjusts actively. Under stress, for example, it raises blood pressure and blood sugar.
Example
Heart rate and blood pressure stay high all day during a deadline crunch. This is an adjustment made to meet the task, not a sign of things “going out of control.”
Common misuse
Mistakenly treated as a synonym for “stress is harmful.” Short-term adaptation is normal. The problem comes when recovery never happens over the long term.
