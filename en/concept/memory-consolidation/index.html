Memory Consolidation

Concept·记忆巩固

New memories take time to move from fragile to stable.

School
Molecular Basis of Memory
In plain words
What you have just learned is easily lost at first. Over hours to days (sometimes longer), nerve cells synthesize new proteins and change their synapses, and only then does the memory stabilize.
Example
Material you cram all night before an exam is often remembered better after a night's sleep, because sleep helps consolidate it.
Common misuse
Believing that once a memory is stored, it no longer changes. Each time you recall a memory, it becomes briefly unstable and is stored again, possibly rewritten in the process.
