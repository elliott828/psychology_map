Multidirectionality

Concept·多向性

Different abilities change in different directions across life. Some rise while others fall.

School
Life-Span Developmental Psychology
In plain words
Lifespan developmental psychology holds that development is not simply a climb followed by a decline. Some abilities, such as vocabulary, keep growing with age, while others, such as reaction speed, decline.
Example
A 50-year-old remembers new names more slowly than they did at 20, but handles complex social situations much better.
Common misuse
Assuming aging is only decline. The lifespan view emphasizes that gains and losses coexist, and that compensation is possible.
