Meaning in Life

Concept·生活意义感

Feeling that life has purpose, matters and makes sense.

School
Logotherapy
In plain words
It has three parts: life feels coherent, it has direction and goals, and one's own life matters.
Example
Caring for children, committing to work, faith and creative work can all give a sense of meaning.
Common misuse
Meaning should not become a single answer one must find, and it is not something to demand from someone in the middle of suffering.
