“The MBTI can accurately sort people into 16 fixed types”

Misreading·MBTI 能把人准确地分成 16 种固定类型。

Verdict：Largely unsupported

Personality differences are mostly distributed on continua, with no clear either-or categories. Many people get a different type when retested just a few weeks later, and its power to predict job performance is weak. The Big Five model is more reliable. The MBTI draws its ideas from Jung's psychological types, but Jung himself did not design this test.
