“People only use 10 percent of their brain”

Misreading·人只用了大脑的 10%。

Verdict：False

Brain imaging shows that nearly all brain regions are active over the course of a day, and damage to even a small area has visible consequences. The brain also consumes about one-fifth of the body's energy. If 90 percent were useless, evolution would not have kept it.
