“Maslow said human needs form a pyramid, and lower levels must be met before people pursue higher ones”

Misreading·马斯洛说人的需求像金字塔，必须先满足低层才会追求高层。

Verdict：Misleading

The pyramid diagram was not drawn by Maslow himself. It was produced later, mainly in management textbooks. Maslow described the order of needs as only a rough tendency, and the levels can overlap. A person can still pursue meaning while hungry. Cross-cultural research has also not confirmed a strict step-by-step order.
