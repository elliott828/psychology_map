“Firstborns are conscientious, second children are rebellious, and youngest children are spoiled. Birth order determines personality”

Misreading·老大责任心强、老二叛逆、老小爱撒娇，出生顺序决定性格。

Verdict：Largely unsupported

Large-sample studies, such as a 2015 analysis of tens of thousands of people, have found that birth order has very little effect on personality. Firstborns have a slight edge in intelligence and academic performance, but the difference is very small. Adler's claims were useful clinical observations, not established conclusions.
