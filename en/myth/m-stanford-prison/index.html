“The Stanford Prison Experiment proved that when good people are placed in a bad situation, they become abusers”

Misreading·斯坦福监狱实验证明了：把好人放进坏的情境，就会变成施虐者。

Verdict：False

Recordings and archives released later show that the experimenters instructed guards to be “tough,” and that participants knew they were playing a role. The study had strong demand characteristics, and many guards did not abuse anyone. It cannot serve as reliable evidence that situations make people do evil.
