“Everyone has a learning style (visual or auditory), and teaching that matches it works better”

Misreading·每个人有自己的学习风格（视觉型、听觉型），按风格教学效果更好。

Verdict：False

People do have preferences, but in controlled experiments, teaching matched to preferences has not produced better results. What works better is matching the content: learning a map is easier with diagrams, and learning pronunciation is easier with sound. Gardner's multiple intelligences are also not “learning styles.”
