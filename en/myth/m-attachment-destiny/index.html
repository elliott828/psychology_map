“Your attachment style was set in childhood, and it determines your love life for the rest of your life”

Misreading·你的依恋类型是童年定下来的，决定了你一生的爱情。

Verdict：Misleading

Infant attachment has only a moderate correlation with adult attachment, and adult attachment also changes with relationships and experiences. Online tests often compress the continuous dimensions of anxiety and avoidance into “types,” and that can lock people into a “this is just how I am” mindset.
