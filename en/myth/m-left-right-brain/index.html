“Some people are “left-brained” (rational), and others are “right-brained” (emotional)”

Misreading·有人是“左脑型”（理性），有人是“右脑型”（感性）。

Verdict：False

The two hemispheres do have specialized roles, for example language is lateralized to the left. In healthy people, however, the two halves are constantly working together, and there is no evidence that some people rely mainly on one side. Brain imaging studies have not found “left-brained” or “right-brained” types in the population. Split-brain research has been overgeneralized.
