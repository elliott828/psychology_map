“Depression is caused by too little serotonin in the brain, and medication replaces the missing chemical”

Misreading·抑郁症是因为脑内血清素不足，吃药就是补回缺的化学物质。

Verdict：Largely unsupported

Medications do act on these neurotransmitter systems, and they help many people. But there is no reliable evidence that people with depression generally have low serotonin levels. The “chemical imbalance” idea is largely a marketing and simplified-communication account. Depression has causes at several levels, including genes, stress, relationships and neural circuits.
