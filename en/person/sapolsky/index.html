Robert Sapolsky

Person·1957–·罗伯特·萨波尔斯基

American neuroendocrinologist who studies the biology of stress and behavior and is known for popular science writing

Lived
1957–
Schools
Stress Physiology、Ethology

Theories

  • Multi-level Biology of Behavior 2017The same behavior can be explained layer by layer across time scales: the nerve firing of the previous second, hormones of a few hours earlier, childhood experience, prenatal development, and culture and genetic evolution.
  • Allostatic Load 1993The body adjusts constantly to cope with stress. The cost of repeated adjustment over time builds up as 'load', showing up as an overall worsening of blood pressure, blood sugar, inflammation, and other markers.
  • Glucocorticoid Cascade Hypothesis 1986Stress hormones that stay high for a long time damage neurons in the hippocampus. The hippocampus helps apply the brakes on the stress response, which creates a cycle in which stress makes it harder to stop stress.

Books

  • 《Behave: The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst》 Robert Sapolsky, 2017
  • 《Why Zebras Don't Get Ulcers: The Acclaimed Guide to Stress, Stress-Related Diseases, and Coping》 Robert Sapolsky, 1994
  • 《Determined: A Science of Life without Free Will》 Robert Sapolsky, 2023
