John Bowlby

Person·1907–1990·约翰·鲍尔比

British psychoanalyst who founded attachment theory, linking psychoanalysis, ethology and developmental research

Lived
1907–1990
Schools
Attachment Theory、Object Relations

Theories

  • Attachment Behavioral System 1969Infants are born with a set of behaviors that seek closeness to a caregiver. They are activated by threat, in order to gain protection and comfort. The caregiver's responses shape the infant's internal expectations about relationships.
  • Adult Attachment 1987Adults also have attachment patterns in close relationships. Some people readily worry about being abandoned (anxiety), while others are reluctant to depend on others (avoidance).

Books

  • 《Attachment (Attachment and Loss, Vol. 1)》 John Bowlby, 1969
  • 《A Secure Base: Parent-Child Attachment and Healthy Human Development》 John Bowlby, 1988
