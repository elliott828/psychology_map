Brian Nosek

Person·1973–·布莱恩·诺塞克

American psychologist who led the Reproducibility Project in psychology and founded the Center for Open Science

Lived
1973–
Schools
Replication Crisis and Open Science、Large-Scale Replication Projects

Theories

  • Reproducibility Project: Psychology 2015A project tried to repeat 100 studies published in three leading journals. Fewer than half succeeded, which prompted a rethinking of practices across the field.
  • Many Labs Replication Project 2014Dozens of labs run the same protocol and each repeat the same set of classic experiments. The results are then pooled. Some effects reappear reliably, while others nearly vanish.
