Marie-Louise von Franz

Person·1915–1998·玛丽-路易丝·冯·弗兰茨

Swiss Jungian analyst who studied fairy tales and alchemy

Lived
1915–1998
Schools
Post-Jungian and Archetypal Psychology、Analytical Psychology

Theories

  • Archetypal Psychology 1975The psyche is not run by the ego. It is a theater of many images. Symptoms are the soul's way of speaking, and therapy should listen to these images rather than try to 'fix' them.

Books

  • 《The Interpretation of Fairy Tales》 Marie-Louise von Franz, 1970
