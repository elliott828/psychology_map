Elliot Aronson

Person·1932–·埃利奥特·阿伦森

American social psychologist who studied cognitive dissonance and invented the "jigsaw classroom" to reduce prejudice

Lived
1932–
Schools
Cognitive Dissonance、Prejudice Research

Theories

  • Stereotype Threat 1995When people worry that their performance will confirm negative views about their group, they actually perform worse.

Books

  • 《The Social Animal》 Elliot Aronson, 1972
  • 《Mistakes Were Made (But Not by Me): Why We Justify Foolish Beliefs, Bad Decisions, and Hurtful Acts》 Carol Tavris, Elliot Aronson, 2007
