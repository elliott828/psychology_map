Michael Gazzaniga

Person·1939–·迈克尔·加扎尼加

American neuroscientist who studied split-brain patients and is one of the founders of cognitive neuroscience

Lived
1939–
Schools
Split-Brain Research、Cognitive Neuroscience

Theories

  • Hemispheric Specialization 1968For most people, language is handled mainly by the left hemisphere, while spatial processing and face processing rely more on the right. This is a tendency, not a division of 'thinking styles'.
  • Left-Brain Interpreter 1978In split-brain patients, the left hemisphere makes up a plausible-sounding reason for behavior that the right hemisphere initiated. People may often be giving themselves reasons after the fact.

Books

  • 《Who's in Charge? Free Will and the Science of the Brain》 Michael Gazzaniga, 2011
  • 《Some Functional Effects of Sectioning the Cerebral Commissures in Man》 Michael S. Gazzaniga, Joseph E. Bogen, Roger W. Sperry, 1962
  • 《Cognitive Neuroscience: The Biology of the Mind》 Michael S. Gazzaniga, Richard B. Ivry, George R. Mangun, 1998
