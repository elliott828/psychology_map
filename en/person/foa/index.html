Edna Foa

Person·1937–2026·埃德娜·福阿

Israeli-born American psychologist and founder of prolonged exposure therapy

Lived
1937–2026
Schools
Trauma Studies and PTSD

Theories

  • Prolonged Exposure 1986In a safe therapy setting, the client repeatedly recounts the trauma and faces the memories and the situations they have been avoiding. This continues until fear decreases and the memory is reintegrated.

Books

  • 《Exposure and Response (Ritual) Prevention for Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder: Therapist Guide》 Edna B. Foa, Elna Yadin, Tracey K. Lichner, 2012
  • 《Prolonged Exposure Therapy for PTSD: Emotional Processing of Traumatic Experiences, Therapist Guide》 Edna B. Foa, Elizabeth A. Hembree, Barbara Olasov Rothbaum, 2007
