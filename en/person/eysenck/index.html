Hans Eysenck

Person·1916–1997·汉斯·艾森克

German-born British psychologist who proposed the PEN model of personality and promoted behavior therapy; later criticized over data issues

Lived
1916–1997
Schools
Factor-Analytic Personality Models、Behavior Therapy

Theories

  • Eysenck PEN Model 1967The core of personality is a small number of dimensions, each with a biological basis. For example, extraverts have lower cortical arousal, so they seek more stimulation.

Books

  • 《Dimensions of Personality》 Hans J. Eysenck, 1947
