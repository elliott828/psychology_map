George A. Miller

Person·1920–2012·乔治·米勒

American psychologist, a central figure in the cognitive revolution and author of "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two"

Lived
1920–2012
Schools
Cognitive Revolution、Cognitive Neuroscience

Theories

  • Information-Processing View 1958People are treated as information channels with limited capacity, and information must pass through stages such as selection, encoding, and storage. This vocabulary became the common model in cognitive research.
  • The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two 1956The number of units a person can hold in short-term memory is limited, about seven. But this can be expanded through chunking, which combines several items into one.
