B. F. Skinner

Person·1904–1990·B. F. 斯金纳

American psychologist who founded operant conditioning and radical behaviorism

Lived
1904–1990
Schools
Operant Conditioning、Behaviorism、Applied Behavior Analysis

Theories

  • Radical Behaviorism 1945Inner thoughts and feelings also count as behavior, only they are not visible. To explain behavior, you only need to find the antecedents and consequences in the environment, without assuming an inner 'entity' in the mind.
  • Reinforcement and Shaping 1953A complex behavior is broken into small steps, and each step closer to the goal is reinforced. This gradually shapes a new behavior, such as training pigeons to play table tennis.
  • Schedules of Reinforcement 1957Whether rewards come on a regular or random schedule produces different rhythms of behavior. Unpredictable rewards, as in slot machines, make it hardest for people to stop.

Books

  • 《About Behaviorism》 B. F. Skinner, 1974
  • 《Science and Human Behavior》 B. F. Skinner, 1953
  • 《Beyond Freedom and Dignity》 B. F. Skinner, 1971
  • 《The Behavior of Organisms: An Experimental Analysis》 B. F. Skinner, 1938
