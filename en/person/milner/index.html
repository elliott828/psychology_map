Brenda Milner

Person·1918–·布伦达·米尔纳

British-born Canadian neuropsychologist who studied patient H.M. in depth and helped establish a multi-system view of memory

Lived
1918–
Schools
Neuropsychology

Theories

  • Multiple Memory Systems 1980There is more than one kind of memory. Facts and events that can be stated depend on the hippocampus, while skill memories such as riding a bike or typing depend on other brain areas. This is why people with amnesia can still learn new skills.

Books

  • 《Loss of Recent Memory After Bilateral Hippocampal Lesions》 William B. Scoville, Brenda Milner, 1957
