Alfred Adler

Person·1870–1937·阿尔弗雷德·阿德勒

Austrian physician who founded individual psychology and was once an important member of Freud's circle

Lived
1870–1937
Schools
Individual Psychology

Theories

  • Inferiority and Compensation 1907People are born with a sense of inferiority, and they respond by striving for superiority and compensating for their flaws. This can become a motive force, or it can grow into an excessive 'inferiority complex'.
  • Social Interest 1927The sign of mental health is care for and contribution to others and to the community, not a focus on one's own superiority.
  • Birth Order and Personality 1927The firstborn is more like a deposed king who has lost the throne, and the youngest is easily spoiled. These ideas are widely repeated, but large-sample studies have not supported them.

Books

  • 《Understanding Human Nature》 Alfred Adler, 1927
  • 《What Life Should Mean to You》 Alfred Adler, 1931
