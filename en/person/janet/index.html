Pierre Janet

Person·1859–1947·皮埃尔·让内

French psychologist and physician who proposed the concept of dissociation, overshadowed for many years by Freud

Lived
1859–1947
Schools
Hypnosis, Hysteria and Dissociation

Theories

  • Psychological Automatism 1889Some experiences are 'split off' from the main consciousness and operate like a separate small system. This causes forgetfulness, paralysis, or states that feel like being 'another person'.

Books

  • 《L'automatisme psychologique》 Pierre Janet, 1889
