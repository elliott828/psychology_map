Karl Friston

Person·1959–·卡尔·弗里斯顿

British neuroscientist who proposed the free energy principle

Lived
1959–
Schools
Predictive Processing

Theories

  • Free-Energy Principle 2010All living systems try to reduce their 'surprise' about the states they are in. Perception and action are both ways of reducing surprise.
  • Computational Psychiatry 2012Symptoms such as hallucinations, delusions, and addiction are understood as biased learning and inference in the brain. Mathematical models describe these biases.

Books

  • 《Computational Psychiatry》 P. Read Montague, Raymond J. Dolan, Karl J. Friston, Peter Dayan, 2012
