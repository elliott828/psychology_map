Albert Bandura

Person·1925–2021·阿尔伯特·班杜拉

Canadian-born American psychologist who proposed observational learning and self-efficacy, linking behaviorist and cognitive traditions

Lived
1925–2021
Schools
Social Learning Theory

Theories

  • Observational Learning 1961Seeing someone else do something and get a good result makes you more likely to do it yourself. Seeing a bad result makes you avoid it. No direct trial is needed.
  • Self-Efficacy Theory 1977How strongly a person believes 'I can do this' affects whether they try and how long they persist. Experiences of success, role models, and encouragement can all raise it.
  • Reciprocal Determinism 1978A person's thoughts, behavior, and environment shape one another. None of the three one-way determines the others.
  • Cognitive-Affective Personality System 1995Personality is not 'a person who is always extraverted'. It is 'extraverted in some situations and quiet in others'. The stable pattern is this if-then profile.

Books

  • 《Social Learning Theory》 Albert Bandura, 1977
  • 《Self-Efficacy: The Exercise of Control》 Albert Bandura, 1997
