Kwang-Kuo Hwang

Person·1945–·黄光国

Taiwanese psychologist who proposed a model of renqing and face, and advocated the indigenization of the social sciences

Lived
1945–
Schools
Indigenous Psychology

Theories

  • Face and Favor 1987In Chinese social exchange, favor (renqing) is a resource and face (mianzi) reflects social standing. Together they form a 'power game' of mutual exchange.

Books

  • 《Face and Favor: The Chinese Power Game》 Kwang-Kuo Hwang, 1987
