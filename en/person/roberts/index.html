Brent Roberts

Person·布伦特·罗伯茨

American psychologist who studies how personality changes with age

Schools
Big Five Personality

Theories

  • Personality Continuity and Maturity 2000A person's position in the population on personality traits is quite stable from middle age onward. At the same time, most people become more conscientious, more settled, and less anxious as they age.

Books

  • 《The Rank-Order Consistency of Personality Traits From Childhood to Old Age: A Quantitative Review of Longitudinal Studies》 Brent W. Roberts, Wendy F. DelVecchio, 2000
