Jean-Martin Charcot

Person·1825–1893·让—马丁·沙可

French neurologist who studied hysteria using hypnosis; Freud studied under him

Lived
1825–1893
Schools
Hypnosis, Hysteria and Dissociation

Theories

  • Charcot's Hysteria and Hypnosis 1887Hysteria was not 'faking illness'. It was a real disorder that could be reproduced through hypnosis. Its symptoms came from psychological causes rather than bodily injury.
