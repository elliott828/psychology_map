Raymond Cattell

Person·1905–1998·雷蒙德·卡特尔

British-born American psychologist who proposed fluid and crystallized intelligence and the 16 personality factors

Lived
1905–1998
Schools
General Intelligence and Factor Analysis、Factor-Analytic Personality Models

Theories

  • Fluid and Crystallized Intelligence 1963Fluid intelligence is the ability to handle new problems, and it declines gradually after middle age. Crystallized intelligence is accumulated knowledge and skill, which can be maintained into old age.
  • Cattell-Horn-Carroll Model 1993Abilities can be arranged in three layers. At the top is general intelligence, g. In the middle are about ten broad abilities. At the bottom are many specific skills.
  • Cattell 16PF 1949Factor analysis was used to extract 16 'source traits', and a questionnaire was built to measure them.

Books

  • 《The Scientific Analysis of Personality》 Raymond B. Cattell, 1965
