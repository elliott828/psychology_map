Urie Bronfenbrenner

Person·1917–2005·尤里·布朗芬布伦纳

American developmental psychologist who proposed ecological systems theory and helped establish the Head Start program

Lived
1917–2005
Schools
Ecological Systems Theory

Theories

  • Nested Environmental Systems 1979The environment is arranged from near to far. Closest is what the child experiences directly, such as home and school. Next are the links between those settings, then indirect influences such as a parent's job, then culture and institutions, all over time.

Books

  • 《The Ecology of Human Development: Experiments by Nature and Design》 Urie Bronfenbrenner, 1979
