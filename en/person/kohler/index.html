Wolfgang Kohler

Person·1887–1967·沃尔夫冈·苛勒

German psychologist known for his experiments on insight in chimpanzees

Lived
1887–1967
Schools
Gestalt Psychology

Theories

  • Gestalt Principles of Perception 1923Perception automatically groups elements that are close together, look similar, or form a closed shape. The whole is more than the sum of its parts.
  • Insight in Problem Solving 1917Solving a problem is often not a matter of step-by-step trial and error. It can come suddenly as an 'aha' after the problem is reinterpreted.

Books

  • 《Gestalt Psychology: An Introduction to New Concepts in Modern Psychology》 Wolfgang Kohler, 1929
  • 《The Mentality of Apes》 Wolfgang Köhler, 1917
