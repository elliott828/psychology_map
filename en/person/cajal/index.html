Santiago Ramon y Cajal

Person·1852–1934·圣地亚哥·拉蒙-卡哈尔

Spanish histologist who used silver staining to show that neurons are independent cells

Lived
1852–1934
Schools
Neuron Doctrine

Theories

  • Neuron Theory 1888The nervous system is not one continuous network. It is made of countless separate nerve cells that pass signals through contact with one another. This doctrine replaced the 'reticular theory' and laid the groundwork for today's talk of neural circuits.

Books

  • 《Advice for a Young Investigator》 Santiago Ramón y Cajal, 1897
