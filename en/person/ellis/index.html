Albert Ellis

Person·1913–2007·阿尔伯特·艾利斯

American psychologist and founder of rational emotive behavior therapy, who had trained in psychoanalysis

Lived
1913–2007
Schools
Cognitive Therapies

Theories

  • REBT ABC Model 1955Emotions are caused not by the event (A) but by your beliefs about the event (B), and beliefs have consequences (C). Emotions are changed by challenging irrational beliefs.
