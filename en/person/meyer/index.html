Victor Meyer

Person·维克托·迈耶

British clinical psychologist who first reported exposure and response prevention for obsessive-compulsive disorder

Schools
Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy

Theories

  • Exposure and Response Prevention 1966People with obsessive-compulsive disorder are exposed to the things that cause anxiety while not performing rituals such as handwashing. Anxiety naturally declines, and the compulsions weaken with it.
