Paul Baltes

Person·1939–2006·保罗·巴尔特斯

German developmental psychologist and a leading founder of lifespan developmental psychology

Lived
1939–2006
Schools
Life-Span Developmental Psychology

Theories

  • Life-Span Propositions 1987Development is lifelong, multidirectional, and involves both gains and losses. It is shaped by three kinds of influence: age, history, and individual life events.
  • Selection, Optimization and Compensation 1990As resources decline, older adults keep functioning by narrowing their goals (selection), concentrating practice on what matters (optimization), and finding alternative ways of doing things (compensation).

Books

  • 《Theoretical Propositions of Life-Span Developmental Psychology: On the Dynamics Between Growth and Decline》 Paul B. Baltes, 1987
  • 《Successful Aging: Perspectives from the Behavioral Sciences》 Paul B. Baltes, Margret M. Baltes (eds.), 1990
