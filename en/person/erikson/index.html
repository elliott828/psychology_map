Erik Erikson

Person·1902–1994·埃里克·埃里克森

German-born American psychoanalyst who proposed eight stages of psychosocial development and the concept of "identity crisis"

Lived
1902–1994
Schools
Psychosocial Development、Life-Span Developmental Psychology

Theories

  • Eight Stages of Man 1950Eight stages center on trust, autonomy, initiative, industry, identity, intimacy, generativity, and integrity. Failing to handle a stage's task leaves lasting difficulties.

Books

  • 《Childhood and Society》 Erik Erikson, 1950
  • 《Identity: Youth and Crisis》 Erik H. Erikson, 1968
