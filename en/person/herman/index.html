Judith Herman

Person·1942–·朱迪思·赫尔曼

American psychiatrist, author of "Trauma and Recovery," who proposed complex trauma

Lived
1942–
Schools
Trauma Studies and PTSD、ICD-11 and Complex PTSD

Theories

  • Three-Stage Model of Trauma Recovery 1992Recovery has three steps: establishing safety and stability, remembering and mourning the trauma, and reconnecting with life and other people.
  • Complex PTSD 1992The effects of long-term, repeated trauma, such as childhood abuse or prolonged domestic violence, go beyond flashbacks and avoidance. They include difficulties with emotion regulation, a negative sense of self, and relationship problems.

Books

  • 《Trauma and Recovery: The Aftermath of Violence—From Domestic Abuse to Political Terror》 Judith Herman, 1992
