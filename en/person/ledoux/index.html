Joseph LeDoux

Person·1949–·约瑟夫·勒杜

American neuroscientist who studies fear and the amygdala circuits that underlie it

Lived
1949–
Schools
Affective Neuroscience

Theories

  • Left-Brain Interpreter 1978In split-brain patients, the left hemisphere makes up a plausible-sounding reason for behavior that the right hemisphere initiated. People may often be giving themselves reasons after the fact.
  • Amygdala Fear Circuit 1996A threatening stimulus can bypass the cortex and trigger the amygdala directly through the thalamus. This means a person can feel afraid before they have fully thought it through.

Books

  • 《The Emotional Brain: The Mysterious Underpinnings of Emotional Life》 Joseph LeDoux, 1996
