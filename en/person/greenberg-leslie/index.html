Leslie Greenberg

Person·1945–·莱斯利·格林伯格

Canadian psychologist and founder of emotion-focused therapy

Lived
1945–
Schools
Emotion-Focused Therapy、Gestalt Therapy

Theories

  • Two-Chair Technique 1979The client sits in two chairs and takes turns speaking for each side of an inner conflict. Or the client speaks to a person imagined in an empty chair and says what was left unsaid.
  • Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy 1988Repeated conflict between partners is seen as a cycle in which attachment needs cannot be expressed. Through helping each partner voice hidden emotions, safety in the relationship is rebuilt.

Books

  • 《Emotionally Focused Therapy for Couples》 Leslie S. Greenberg, Susan M. Johnson, 1988
