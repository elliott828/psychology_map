Tim Lomas

Person·蒂姆·洛马斯

British psychologist who, with Ivtzan, proposed "second-wave positive psychology" and studies the dialectic between positive and negative aspects of well-being

Schools
Second Wave Positive Psychology

Theories

  • Second Wave Positive Psychology 2016Argues that a good life is not only about pursuing positive experiences. Anxiety and sadness also have value, and joy and suffering are often intertwined, so well-being research should study both together.

Books

  • 《Second Wave Positive Psychology: Exploring the Positive–Negative Dialectics of Wellbeing》 Tim Lomas, Itai Ivtzan, 2016
