John B. Watson

Person·1878–1958·约翰·华生

American psychologist who proclaimed behaviorism and later moved into the advertising industry

Lived
1878–1958
Schools
Behaviorism、Classical Conditioning

Theories

  • Conditioned Emotional Response (Little Albert) 1920A baby was startled by a loud noise when he saw a white rat. After a few pairings, he cried whenever he saw a rat. This is cited as proof that phobias can be learned, but the evidence behind it is much thinner than textbooks suggest.

Books

  • 《Behaviorism》 John B. Watson, 1924
  • 《Psychology as the Behaviorist Views It》 John B. Watson, 1913
