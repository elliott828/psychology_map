Jerome Bruner

Person·1915–2016·杰罗姆·布鲁纳

American psychologist who took part in the cognitive revolution, introduced the idea of "scaffolding," and promoted cognitive approaches to education

Lived
1915–2016
Schools
Sociocultural Theory、Cognitive Revolution

Theories

  • Scaffolding 1976Adults give just enough help when a child needs it, then gradually withdraw it as the child's ability grows, much like the scaffolding used in building.

Books
