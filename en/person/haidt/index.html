Jonathan Haidt

Person·1963–·乔纳森·海特

American social psychologist who studies moral intuitions and political division

Lived
1963–
Schools
Cultural Psychology、Social Cognition、Moral Foundations Theory、Screens and Adolescent Mental Health

Theories

  • Smartphones and Adolescent Mental Health 2017Argues that since the early 2010s, the spread of smartphones and social media has left teenagers lonelier, more anxious, and sleeping less. Critics argue the effect is very small and that other factors cannot be ruled out.
  • Moral Foundations Theory 2004Argues that human moral judgment rests on several intuitions, such as care, fairness, loyalty, authority, and purity. People with different political views weight these differently.

Books

  • 《The Righteous Mind: Why Good People Are Divided by Politics and Religion》 Jonathan Haidt, 2012
  • 《The Happiness Hypothesis: Finding Modern Truth in Ancient Wisdom》 Jonathan Haidt, 2006
  • 《The Anxious Generation: How the Great Rewiring of Childhood Is Causing an Epidemic of Mental Illness》 Jonathan Haidt, 2024
