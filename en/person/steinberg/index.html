Laurence Steinberg

Person·1952–·劳伦斯·斯坦伯格

American developmental psychologist who studies adolescent risk-taking and proposed a dual-systems model of adolescent brain development

Lived

Schools

Theories

  • Dual Systems Model of Adolescent Brain Development 2008The drive for novelty and reward strengthens early in adolescence, while the systems responsible for planning and self-control mature later. In this period of 'gas before brakes', risk-taking becomes more likely.
