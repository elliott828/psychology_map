Martin Seligman

Person·1942–·马丁·塞利格曼

American psychologist who moved from research on learned helplessness to positive psychology

Lived
1942–
Schools
Learned Helplessness、Positive Psychology

Theories

  • Explanatory Style (Reformulation) 1978When something bad happens, people who habitually see the cause as 'my fault, lasting, and affecting everything' are more likely to fall into helplessness and depression.
  • Learned Helplessness Revised 2016Helplessness is not something 'learned'. It is the default reaction to stress. What actually has to be learned is 'I can control this'.
  • PERMA Model of Well-Being 2011Well-being has five parts: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment.

Books

  • 《Learned Optimism: How to Change Your Mind and Your Life》 Martin Seligman, 1990
  • 《Flourish: A Visionary New Understanding of Happiness and Well-being》 Martin Seligman, 2011
  • 《Authentic Happiness: Using the New Positive Psychology to Realize Your Potential for Lasting Fulfillment》 Martin Seligman, 2002
  • 《Helplessness: On Depression, Development, and Death》 Martin E. P. Seligman, 1975
