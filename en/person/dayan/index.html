Peter Dayan

Person·1965–·彼得·戴扬

British computational neuroscientist who studies reinforcement learning and computational psychiatry

Lived
1965–
Schools
Predictive Processing、Reward Prediction Error

Theories

  • Computational Psychiatry 2012Symptoms such as hallucinations, delusions, and addiction are understood as biased learning and inference in the brain. Mathematical models describe these biases.
  • Dopamine Reward Prediction Error 1997Dopamine neurons do not simply fire to a reward itself. They fire to outcomes that are better or worse than expected: an unexpected reward increases firing, an expected one leaves it unchanged, and a disappointment decreases it.

Books

  • 《A Neural Substrate of Prediction and Reward》 Wolfram Schultz, Peter Dayan, P. Read Montague, 1997
  • 《Computational Psychiatry》 P. Read Montague, Raymond J. Dolan, Karl J. Friston, Peter Dayan, 2012
