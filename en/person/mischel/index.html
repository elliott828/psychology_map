Walter Mischel

Person·1930–2018·沃尔特·米歇尔

Austrian-born American psychologist who led the person-situation debate and conducted the marshmallow study of delayed gratification

Lived
1930–2018
Schools
Person-Situation Debate

Theories

  • Cognitive-Affective Personality System 1995Personality is not 'a person who is always extraverted'. It is 'extraverted in some situations and quiet in others'. The stable pattern is this if-then profile.

Books

  • 《The Marshmallow Test: Mastering Self-Control》 Walter Mischel, 2014
  • 《Personality and Assessment》 Walter Mischel, 1968
