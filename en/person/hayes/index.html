Steven C. Hayes

Person·1948–·史蒂文·海斯

American psychologist and founder of acceptance and commitment therapy

Lived
1948–
Schools
Third-Wave Therapies、Process-Based Therapy

Theories

  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy 1999Six processes: acceptance, cognitive defusion, being present, self as context, values, and committed action. The goal is to keep doing valued things even when pain is present.
  • Process-Based Therapy 2018Instead of asking 'which therapy works for which diagnosis', it asks 'which processes maintain this person's problem, and what methods can change those processes'.

Books

  • 《Process-Based CBT: The Science and Core Clinical Competencies of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy》 Steven C. Hayes, Stefan G. Hofmann, 2018
