Isabel Briggs Myers

Person·1897–1980·伊莎贝尔·布里格斯·迈尔斯

American writer who, together with her mother, developed the MBTI based on Jung's typology

Lived
1897–1980
Schools
Trait Theory

Theories

  • Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI) 1962Turns Jung's typology into a self-report questionnaire. People are placed at one pole on each of four pairs (extraversion-introversion, sensing-intuition, thinking-feeling, judging-perceiving), which combine into 16 types.

Books

  • 《Gifts Differing: Understanding Personality Type》 Isabel Briggs Myers, Peter B. Myers, 1980
