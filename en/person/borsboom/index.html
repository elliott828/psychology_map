Denny Borsboom

Person·1973–·丹尼·博斯布姆

Dutch psychologist who proposed viewing mental disorders as networks of interacting symptoms

Lived
1973–
Schools
Network Psychometrics

Theories

  • Symptom Network Theory of Mental Disorders 2010Argues that disorders such as depression are not sets of symptoms caused by a single underlying 'disease'. They are networks of symptoms, such as insomnia, fatigue, and hopelessness, that trigger and reinforce one another.

Books

  • 《Network Analysis: An Integrative Approach to the Structure of Psychopathology》 Denny Borsboom, Angélique O. J. Cramer, 2013
