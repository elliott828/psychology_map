Ernst Heinrich Weber

Person·1795–1878·恩斯特·韦伯

German physiologist who found that the just noticeable difference in sensation is proportional to stimulus intensity

Lived
1795–1878
Schools
Psychophysics

Theories

  • Weber-Fechner Law 1860A stimulus must increase by a fixed proportion before a person notices a change. In a noisy room, you have to raise your voice a lot before anyone hears you.
