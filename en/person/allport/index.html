Gordon Allport

Person·1897–1967·戈登·奥尔波特

American psychologist who founded trait theory of personality and pioneered the study of prejudice

Lived
1897–1967
Schools
Trait Theory、Prejudice Research

Theories

  • Lexical Hypothesis 1936In human language, every important individual difference gradually acquires a word to describe it. So the basic dimensions of personality can be found by analyzing the dictionary.
  • Contact Hypothesis 1954When people from different groups meet under conditions of equal status, shared goals, cooperation, and institutional support, prejudice decreases.

Books

  • 《The Nature of Prejudice》 Gordon Allport, 1954
  • 《Personality: A Psychological Interpretation》 Gordon W. Allport, 1937
