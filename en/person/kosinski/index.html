Michal Kosinski

Person·1982–·米哈尔·科辛斯基

Computational social scientist who studies how digital footprints reveal personality and who has recently tested the "mind" abilities of large language models

Lived
1982–
Schools
Digital Footprints and Personality Prediction、Large Language Models and Cognition

Theories

  • Theory-of-Mind Tests for Language Models 2023Gives large language models human theory-of-mind tasks to see whether they understand that others can have thoughts different from their own. The results were surprising, but there is no consensus on how to interpret them.
  • Likes Predict Personality 2013The posts a person likes on social media can predict their gender, political leanings, and Big Five personality scores fairly accurately. This shows that digital footprints carry private information.

Books

  • 《Private Traits and Attributes Are Predictable from Digital Records of Human Behavior》 Michal Kosinski, David Stillwell, Thore Graepel, 2013
  • 《Evaluating Large Language Models in Theory of Mind Tasks》 Michal Kosinski, 2024
