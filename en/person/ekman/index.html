Paul Ekman

Person·1934–2025·保罗·埃克曼

American psychologist who studied facial expressions and the cross-cultural universality of emotion

Lived
1934–2025
Schools
Affective Neuroscience

Theories

  • Basic Emotions 1971Six or seven emotions, such as happiness, anger, and fear, have facial expressions that are common across cultures. This view is widely used in popular science and continues to be questioned by researchers.
