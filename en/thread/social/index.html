Society and Culture

Thread

How do other people and culture enter the inner life?

Schools (by date)

  • Crowd Psychology 1895In groups, people are subject to suggestion and emotional contagion. Their behavior differs from when they are alone: more impulsive and more extreme. This is one of the earliest systematic accounts in social psychology.
  • Field Theory and Group Dynamics 1936Behavior depends not only on the person or only on the environment, but on the 'field' formed by both. From this he founded group dynamics, studies of leadership styles and action research.
  • Conformity and Obedience 1951Under group pressure and authority, ordinary people act against their own judgment or conscience. These experiments made people aware of the power of the situation.
  • Prejudice Research 1954Prejudice is not a problem of a few bad people but the result of ordinary people's categorization, group membership and competition. Contact under the right conditions can reduce it.
  • Cognitive Dissonance 1957When a person's ideas and behavior contradict each other, they feel uncomfortable and tend to change their ideas to ease the discomfort, rather than changing their behavior.
  • Attribution Theory 1958People look for causes of their own and others' behavior. Whether they attribute it to personality or to the situation affects how they evaluate others and how they respond.
  • Indigenous Psychology 1971Psychology developed in the West does not always apply to other societies. Each culture should study its own psychology with its own concepts and methods, such as 'amae,' 'relationship' and 'face.'
  • Social Identity Theory 1979People treat the groups they belong to as part of the 'self,' so they favor their own group and draw self-esteem from the group's status.
  • Social Cognition 1984Uses the methods of cognitive psychology to study how people understand others and themselves: how they categorize people, form impressions and remember information about others, and as a result develop biases and errors.
  • Cultural Psychology 1991Culture is not just an external background. It is an inherent part of what shapes the self, emotion and ways of thinking, and many conclusions treated as 'universal' hold only in Western samples.
  • WEIRD Samples Critique 2010Most psychology participants come from Western, educated, industrialized, rich and democratic societies. On many tasks, these people are not 'representative of humanity.'
  • Moral Foundations Theory 2012Human moral judgments come from several intuitions: care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity and liberty. Conservatives and liberals weight them differently.
  • Misinformation and Polarization 2018Studies why people believe and share false information, how groups move toward polarization, and which corrections work.
