Humanism and Meaning

Thread

How do people live out meaning?

Schools (by date)

  • Hierarchy of Needs and Self-Actualization 1943Human motivation moves from survival, safety, belonging and esteem up to self-actualization. Higher growth needs become the main drive only after more basic needs are met.
  • Logotherapy 1946A person's most basic motivation is the search for meaning in life. Even in suffering that cannot be changed, one can choose an attitude toward it, and find meaning in doing so.
  • Gestalt Therapy 1951Emphasizes awareness of the 'here and now' and the processing of unfinished emotions. Techniques such as the empty chair and role play are used to act out inner conflicts.
  • Person-Centered Therapy 1951The key to therapy lies not in technique but in whether the therapist offers empathy, genuineness and unconditional acceptance. In such a relationship, people naturally change in the direction of growth.
  • Existential Psychotherapy 1958Anxiety and suffering often come from avoiding the basic conditions of human life: death, freedom, isolation and meaninglessness. Therapy helps people face them.
  • Flow 1975When the difficulty of a task matches your ability, the goals are clear and feedback is immediate, people enter a state of absorbed, enjoyable engagement.
  • Self-Determination Theory 1985People have three basic psychological needs: autonomy, competence and relatedness. When these needs are met, intrinsic motivation is stronger and people are healthier.
  • Emotion-Focused Therapy 1988Treats emotion as the core of change: first helping people become aware of and accept their real feelings, and then using those feelings to change patterns in relationships or in the self.
  • Positive Psychology 1998Psychology should study not only illness but also how people live well: well-being, strengths, meaning, engagement and good relationships.
  • Self-Compassion 2003When facing failure or suffering, treat yourself the way you would treat a friend: kindly rather than harshly, recognizing that suffering is shared by all people, and not being overwhelmed by emotion.
  • Second Wave Positive Psychology 2016Well-being is not only about positive emotion. Suffering, failure and conflicts over meaning are also part of a good life and should be studied within positive psychology.
