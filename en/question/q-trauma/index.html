Trauma

Question·创伤

How different threads explain it

  • Conditioned Reflex: Acquisition, Extinction, Generalization Learning and BehaviorThe vigilance and avoidance after trauma involve learning: cues become linked with danger, and this can be weakened through extinction.
  • Amygdala Fear Circuit Brain and BiologyLoops linking threatening memories and bodily reactions can trigger a person even when they are actually safe.
  • Psychological Automatism Unconscious and DepthJanet first described this: a sudden, intense fright can split part of the experience off from consciousness, leaving intrusive fragments.
  • Prolonged Exposure Clinical and Mental HealthThe treatments with good evidence work by facing memories and avoided situations again under safe conditions, rather than continuing to escape.
  • Will to Meaning Humanism and MeaningFrankl held that suffering itself has no meaning, but people can find meaning in what they do with it. This is not a demand placed on victims.
