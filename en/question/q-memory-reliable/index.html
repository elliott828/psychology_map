Can Memory Be Trusted?

Question·记忆可靠吗

How different threads explain it

  • Reconstructive Memory Mind and CognitionRecalling is a reconstruction, not playing back a videotape, so every act of remembering may rewrite the memory.
  • Misinformation Effect Mind and CognitionSuggestive information received after the fact can quietly change a memory, and people are just as confident in the altered memory.
  • Multiple Memory Systems Brain and BiologyFacts, skills and emotional memories rely on different systems. Amnesic patients can still learn new skills.
  • Repression Unconscious and DepthThe idea that “repressed memories can be recovered exactly as they were” lacks evidence, but people can certainly choose to avoid remembering consciously.
  • Prolonged Exposure Clinical and Mental HealthIn trauma therapy, retelling a memory changes its emotional weight rather than replacing the facts.
