Focus

Question·专注

How different threads explain it

  • Working Memory Model Mind and CognitionWorking memory has limited capacity, and handling several things at once means they crowd each other out. So-called “multitasking” is really rapid switching.
  • Inattentional Blindness Mind and CognitionWhen attention is locked onto one thing, you can miss other obvious things in plain view.
  • Flow Model Humanism and MeaningPeople are most absorbed when the challenge matches their ability, the goal is clear and feedback comes promptly.
  • MBSR Eight-Week Program Clinical and Mental HealthMindfulness training practices the skill of bringing attention back, and it helps with distraction to some degree, but its effects should not be overstated.
  • Schedules of Reinforcement Learning and BehaviorNotifications and refreshing are unpredictable rewards, which make it hard to put the phone down. That is not a personal failure of willpower.
