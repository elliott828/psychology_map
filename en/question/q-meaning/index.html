Meaning in Life

Question·意义感

How different threads explain it

  • Will to Meaning Humanism and MeaningEven in the most difficult circumstances, people can choose their attitude and find meaning in responsibility, love and creativity.
  • Terror Management Theory Humanism and MeaningAwareness of death makes people rely more on cultural values and self-esteem to find meaning. The experimental evidence for this theory is quite contested.
  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy Clinical and Mental HealthYou do not need to find the answer first. Clarify what you value and act on it in the present, and a sense of meaning often comes from the action itself.
  • Independent and Interdependent Self Society and CultureSources of meaning differ across cultures. Some place more weight on personal achievement, others on family and relationships.
  • Hedonic Adaptation Humanism and MeaningHappiness adapts quickly, so constantly raising the level of pleasure cannot produce lasting meaning.
