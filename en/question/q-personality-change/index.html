Can Personality Change?

Question·性格能改吗

How different threads explain it

  • Personality Continuity and Maturity Individual Differences and MeasurementA person's rank within a group is quite stable, but on average people become more responsible and even-tempered as they age.
  • Three Laws of Behavior Genetics Brain and BiologyPersonality differences are moderately heritable. That does not mean they cannot change, only that change does not happen overnight.
  • Cognitive-Affective Personality System Individual Differences and MeasurementBehavior is not consistent everywhere. The “if-then” patterns within particular kinds of situations are more stable, and changing the situation is also a way of changing behavior.
  • Cognitive-Behavior Modification Clinical and Mental HealthSpecific thoughts and behavior patterns can be changed through practice, which is more achievable than changing “the whole personality.”
  • Growth Mindset Humanism and MeaningBelieving change is possible helps, but large-scale trials show that simply “changing your thoughts” produces very small changes.
