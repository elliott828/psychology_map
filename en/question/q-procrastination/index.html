Procrastination

Question·拖延

How different threads explain it

  • Negative Reinforcement Learning and BehaviorThe moment you put a task off, your anxiety drops for a while, and that relief reinforces the procrastination. The longer you put it off, the scarier starting becomes.
  • Heuristics and Biases Mind and CognitionPeople tend to overestimate their future selves and underestimate how long a task will take. The ease of the present always seems more appealing than the benefits later on.
  • Cognitive Distortion Clinical and Mental HealthThoughts like “If I do this badly, it means I'm no good” make starting feel dangerous, so you avoid it first.
  • Basic Psychological Needs Humanism and MeaningWhen a task is imposed by others and has little to do with you, your sense of autonomy is low and so is your motivation. Finding your own reason for it is often more useful than forcing yourself.
