Conformity

Question·从众

How different threads explain it

  • Asch Conformity Experiments Society and CultureEven when the majority is plainly wrong, about a third of people can be pulled into agreeing with it, because people do not want to stand against the group.
  • Social Identity Theory (Core) Society and CultureWe follow the norms of groups we identify with, and pay little attention to the views of outside groups.
  • Six Principles of Influence Society and Culture“Everyone is doing it” is one of the most commonly used shortcuts for persuasion, and it is especially effective when people are uncertain.
  • Observational Learning Learning and BehaviorWhen we see others do something and get good results, we tend to do it too, without needing to be told.
  • Evolutionary Psychology Brain and BiologySome suggest that following the group had survival value in ancestral environments, but explanations of this kind are hard to test.
