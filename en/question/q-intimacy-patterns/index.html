Repeating Patterns in Close Relationships

Question·亲密关系中的重复模式

How different threads explain it

  • Adult Attachment Lifespan DevelopmentPeople carry a set of expectations about whether it is safe to get close to others. Anxious people pursue, avoidant people withdraw, and the cycle repeats itself.
  • Transference Unconscious and DepthWe bring the way we related to important people in the past into the person in front of us, and so our partner ends up playing a role that is not really theirs.
  • Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy Humanism and MeaningBehind arguments there is often an unspoken need for attachment. Getting both sides to say the hidden feelings out loud can break the cycle.
  • Observational Learning Learning and BehaviorWatching how our parents got along teaches us what a close relationship “should be,” and we unconsciously reenact it.
  • Triangulation Clinical and Mental HealthWhen a couple is tense, they bring in a third person, such as a child, to ease things. The relationship becomes more stable, but the problem is put aside.
