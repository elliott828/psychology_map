Anxiety

Question·焦虑

How different threads explain it

  • Amygdala Fear Circuit Brain and BiologyThreat signals can trigger bodily reactions without going through thought, so knowing “there's nothing to fear” does not control a racing heartbeat.
  • Extinction and Spontaneous Recovery Learning and BehaviorFear is learned, and it can be unlearned through repeated experiences of safety. But avoidance keeps it alive.
  • Cognitive Model of Panic Clinical and Mental HealthInterpreting a faster heartbeat as “something bad is about to happen” intensifies anxiety and creates a cycle.
  • Ultimate Concerns Humanism and MeaningSome anxiety does not come from anything specific. It comes from a vague sense of the big questions: death, freedom and meaninglessness.
  • Defense Mechanism Unconscious and DepthAnxiety is also a signal that a conflict has been pushed away. Defenses can ease it, but the cost is that you cannot see the real cause clearly.
