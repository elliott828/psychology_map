Family of Origin

Question·原生家庭

How different threads explain it

  • Attachment Behavioral System Lifespan DevelopmentThe way early caregivers respond shapes a child's expectations about relationships, but it is not a lifelong fate.
  • Three Laws of Behavior Genetics Brain and BiologyChildren raised in the same family are far less alike than people assume, and part of the reason is that they share genes rather than only a home environment.
  • Parenting Styles Lifespan DevelopmentWarm and consistent parenting is linked to better outcomes for children, but a correlation cannot be stated as parents “causing” those outcomes.
  • Bowen Family Systems Theory Clinical and Mental HealthEmotional patterns are passed down through families across generations, and children often end up carrying anxiety the family never dealt with.
  • Nested Environmental Systems Lifespan DevelopmentParents also live within work, money and community. Problems in a family often come from pressures further out.
