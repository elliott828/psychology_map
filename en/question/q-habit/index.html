Habits

Question·习惯

How different threads explain it

  • Habit Loop Learning and BehaviorA habit is the repetition of a cue, an action and a reward. Changing the cue or the reward works better than relying on willpower alone.
  • Schedules of Reinforcement Learning and BehaviorUnpredictable rewards, such as lottery draws or refreshing a feed, keep people returning again and again, and they are the hardest habits to quit.
  • Dual-Process Theory Mind and CognitionHabits run automatically in “System 1.” Changing one requires consciously interrupting it and repeating a new way of doing things.
  • Behavioral Activation Clinical and Mental HealthActing first and waiting for the feeling to follow is an effective way to change habits of depression and avoidance.
  • Long-Term Potentiation Brain and BiologyRepetition strengthens the relevant synapses. This is the biological basis that makes habits hard to change, and also why they can be relearned.
