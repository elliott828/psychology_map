Inferiority

Question·自卑

How different threads explain it

  • Inferiority and Compensation Unconscious and DepthEveryone has a sense of falling short. Turning it into effort is compensation. Turning it into avoidance or showing off is what becomes a “complex.”
  • Self-Efficacy Theory Learning and BehaviorThe belief “I can do this” comes from personal success, role models and encouragement, not from self-talk alone.
  • Social Identity Theory (Core) Society and CultureA group's standing affects individuals' self-esteem. Members of devalued groups are more likely to internalize negative judgments.
  • Cognitive Model of Depression Clinical and Mental HealthWhen a core negative belief about yourself is treated as fact, you selectively remember only the evidence that supports it, and the inferiority is maintained.
  • Basic Psychological Needs Humanism and MeaningFeeling competent is one of the basic human needs. Going a long time without being recognized wears away at self-worth.
