Health Psychology

Application·1978·健康心理学

Studies the relationship between stress, behavior and chronic illness, and designs interventions for smoking cessation, medication adherence and pain management.

Domain
健康
Landmark
The American Psychological Association establishes its Health Psychology division (Division 38)

Relations to other schools

  • ← Applies Transactional Model of Stress and CopingThe appraisal and coping model is used for chronic illness and stress management.
  • ← Applies Biopsychosocial ModelHealth psychology uses this framework to integrate biological and psychosocial factors.
  • ← Applies Mindfulness-Based Stress ReductionMindfulness courses are used in pain, stress and chronic illness care.
