Everyday Self-Help

Application·1980·日常自助

Turns methods such as CBT into self-help books, apps and exercises for people who do not enter treatment. It also includes peer groups such as Alcoholics Anonymous. Some of these methods are supported by controlled trials.

Domain
自助
Landmark
Burns, Feeling Good: The New Mood Therapy, a self-help book based on cognitive therapy
