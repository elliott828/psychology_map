DSM Categories Versus Dimensional Models

Debate·2013·DSM 与维度模型

DSM-5 was published in 2013 and adopted dimensional approaches only to a limited extent. RDoC (2010) and HiTOP (2017) are research-driven alternatives. Clinical practice still relies mainly on categorical diagnosis.

The two sides

  • DSM / ICD Diagnostic SystemsCategorical diagnoses make communication, research, insurance and clinical decisions easier, and they have greatly improved reliability.
  • Dimensional Models (RDoC / HiTOP)Categories produce a great deal of comorbidity and heterogeneity. Mental health problems should be organized along continuous dimensions and in terms of functioning.
