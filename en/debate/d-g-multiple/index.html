One Intelligence or Many?

Debate·1983·智力是一个还是多个

Gardner proposed multiple intelligences in 1983. Empirical studies have found that different abilities still correlate with each other, and that “multiple” lacks testable measures. Its influence on educational practice, however, has been large.

The two sides

  • General Intelligence and Factor AnalysisScores on different cognitive tests are positively correlated, which points to a shared general ability that predicts academic and occupational success.
  • Multiple IntelligencesIntelligence consists of several relatively independent abilities. Focusing only on language and logic overlooks other talents.
