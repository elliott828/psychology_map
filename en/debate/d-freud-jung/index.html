The Freud-Jung Split

Debate·1913·弗洛伊德与荣格的决裂

The two men met in 1907. In 1912, Jung's “Symbols of Transformation” redefined libido, which made their disagreement public, and by 1913 they had openly parted ways. The split involved theoretical differences as well as personal relations, power and the question of who would be the successor.

The two sides

  • Classical PsychoanalysisLibido is essentially a sexual drive. Religion and myth should be reduced to repressed desire, and the status of the “sexual theory” must be defended.
  • Analytical PsychologyLibido is general psychic energy. Symbols and myths point toward the future and carry meaning of their own. They are not merely disguises for the past.
