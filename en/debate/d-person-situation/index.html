Person Versus Situation

Debate·1968·人—情境之争

Mischel's critique in 1968 set off more than a decade of debate. The later compromise view is that averaging behavior across many occasions lets traits predict quite well, while for any single situation, the situation itself exerts the stronger pull.

The two sides

  • Trait TheoryPersonality consists of stable tendencies that predict behavior across time and situations, and it is worth measuring.
  • Person-Situation DebateTraits predict single acts of behavior poorly. The situation is the main determinant.
