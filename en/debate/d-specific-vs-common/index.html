“Specific Techniques” or “Common Factors”

Debate·1975·“特定技术”还是“共同因素”

Luborsky's 1975 “Dodo bird verdict” and Wampold's 2001 contextual model framed the debate. The current, more balanced view is that both matter: specific techniques are especially important for conditions such as phobias and obsessive-compulsive disorder.

The two sides

  • Cognitive-Behavioral TherapyFor specific disorders, tested techniques such as exposure and behavioral activation should be used, since their effects differ, and evidence-based therapies should be promoted.
  • Common FactorsThe average effects of different therapies are similar, and the relationship, expectations and individual qualities of the therapist explain most of the outcome.
