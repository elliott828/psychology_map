The “Recovered Memory” Controversy

Debate·1994·“恢复的记忆”之争

The “memory wars” in the United States in the 1990s broke up many families, and many cases were later overturned. The consensus is that cases of genuinely forgotten and later recalled experiences do exist, but suggestive techniques can also produce false memories.

The two sides

  • RepressionTraumatic events can be repressed and later recovered in therapy, and the client's recollections should be believed.
  • Misinformation EffectMemory can be reshaped by suggestion. “Recovered” memories produced in therapy may be false memories that were implanted.
