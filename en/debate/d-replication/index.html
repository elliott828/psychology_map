The Replication Crisis

Debate·2015·可重复性危机

The Open Science Collaboration's 2015 replication project escalated the discussion. Most researchers now agree that preregistration and larger samples are needed. The disagreement is over how many published findings are false.

The two sides

  • Replication Crisis and Open ScienceMany published findings cannot be replicated because of small samples, flexibility in analysis and publication bias. Reform is necessary.
  • Social CognitionExperimental effects are sensitive to context, so a failed replication does not necessarily mean the original effect does not exist. Social priming and related areas have become the focus of the debate.
