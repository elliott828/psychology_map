Behaviorism and the Cognitive Revolution

Debate·1959·行为主义与认知革命

Chomsky's 1959 review of Skinner's “Verbal Behavior” is often taken as the turning point. The laws of learning remain valid, however, and cognitive behavioral therapy is a blend of the two traditions.

The two sides

  • BehaviorismScience can only study observable behavior. Internal processes are not proper objects of science, and behavior is shaped by the environment.
  • Cognitive RevolutionLanguage and thought cannot be explained without internal representations and rules. The way the mind processes information has to be studied directly.
