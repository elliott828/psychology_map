Nature and Nurture

Debate·1960·先天与后天

This either-or split has largely been replaced by the idea of gene-environment interaction. Today's debate centers on how much each contributes, how to measure it, and what it means for education and policy.

The two sides

  • Behavior GeneticsTwin and adoption studies show that almost all traits have a genetic component, and that the shared family environment matters less than people imagine.
  • Ecological Systems TheoryDevelopment happens in nested environments. Genes exert their effects through the environment, and heritability does not mean that a trait cannot be changed.
