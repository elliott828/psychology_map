Experimental Psychology

School·1879·实验心理学

Studies mental processes through experiments with controlled conditions and repeated measurement, and trains observers to report their own sensations (introspection).

Historical influence ●●●●●·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Mind and Cognition
Landmark
Wundt establishes the first psychology laboratory at the University of Leipzig
What the evidence says
The practice of making psychology an experimental discipline continues to this day; the introspective analyses of Wundt and Titchener have themselves been abandoned.
Key figure
Wilhelm Wundt

Core theories

  • Mental Chronometry 1868To find out how long a mental step takes, compare reaction times on tasks with and without that step.
  • Structuralism and Introspection 1898Consciousness is broken down into its most basic sensations, images, and feelings. Observers are trained to report their own experiences.

Key concepts
