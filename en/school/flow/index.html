Flow

School·1975·心流

When the difficulty of a task matches your ability, the goals are clear and feedback is immediate, people enter a state of absorbed, enjoyable engagement.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Humanism and Meaning
Landmark
Csikszentmihalyi's Beyond Boredom and Anxiety
What the evidence says
'Engagement is highest when challenge and skill match' has considerable support, but flow is measured in inconsistent ways, and the causal relationships are still being tested.
Key figure
Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

Core theories

  • Flow Model 1990To enter flow you need clear goals, immediate feedback, and a challenge matched to your skill. Too easy and you become bored; too hard and you become anxious.
