Embodied Cognition

School·1991·具身认知

The mind is not only in the brain. It is formed by the brain, the body and the environment together, and thinking often relies on bodily movement and space.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Mind and Cognition
Landmark
Varela, Thompson and Rosch's The Embodied Mind
What the evidence says
The broad view that perception and action are closely linked has support; some single experiments claiming that 'body posture affects judgment' have failed to replicate.
Key figure
Francisco Varela

Core theories

  • Affordances 1979What we directly perceive is not just shape and color, but what the environment lets us do. A chair is 'sit-on-able' and a door handle is 'pull-able'.
  • Conceptual Metaphor Theory 1980We understand abstract concepts through concrete bodily experience. Phrases like 'argument is war' and 'time is money' reflect how we think.
