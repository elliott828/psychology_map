Cognitive Therapies

School·1955·认知取向治疗

Emotions are not caused directly by events but by our interpretation of events. Therapy helps people identify and test these automatic thoughts.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Ellis begins to propose rational therapy (Beck's Depression, 1967)
What the evidence says
There is much controlled-trial support for depression and anxiety disorders. The mechanism by which cognitive change produces benefit is considered to need more testing.
Key figure
Albert Ellis

Core theories

  • REBT ABC Model 1955Emotions are caused not by the event (A) but by your beliefs about the event (B), and beliefs have consequences (C). Emotions are changed by challenging irrational beliefs.
  • Cognitive Model of Depression 1967Depressed people hold a negative 'cognitive triad' about themselves, the world, and the future, and treat these views as facts. Therapy changes mood by testing these thoughts.

Key people

Key concepts

  • Cognitive DistortionCommon biased habits of thinking about problems.
  • RuminationChewing over the same problem repeatedly without making progress.
