Network Psychometrics

School·2013·症状网络模型

Does not treat symptoms as expressions of a single underlying 'disease' but as a network in which they trigger one another: insomnia causes fatigue, fatigue causes low mood, and low mood causes insomnia.

Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Borsboom and Cramer publish the review 'The Network Approach to Psychopathology'
What the evidence says
Network models offer a new way of describing disorders. Whether network structures are stable and replicable, and whether they can guide intervention, is still questioned.
Key figure
Denny Borsboom

Core theories

  • Symptom Network Theory of Mental Disorders 2010Argues that disorders such as depression are not sets of symptoms caused by a single underlying 'disease'. They are networks of symptoms, such as insomnia, fatigue, and hopelessness, that trigger and reinforce one another.
