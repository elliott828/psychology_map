Self Psychology

School·1971·自体心理学

Whether a person has a stable sense of self-worth depends on whether caregivers saw them and mirrored and idealized them in early life. The core of treatment is empathic understanding.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Landmark

What the evidence says
There is much support for the value of empathy in treatment, but the developmental theory of the self lacks rigorous testing.
Key figure
Heinz Kohut

Core theories

  • Selfobject and Empathy 1971People need others to mirror their worth and to idealize them. These others are called 'selfobjects'. When they are missing, the self becomes fragile.

Key concepts
