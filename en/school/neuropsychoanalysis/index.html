Neuropsychoanalysis

School·2000·神经精神分析

Uses case studies of brain damage and imaging research to test and revise psychoanalytic concepts such as dreams, the ego and emotion.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Unconscious and Depth
Landmark
The International Neuropsychoanalysis Society is founded
What the evidence says
It proposes some testable connections (such as between dreams and the dopamine system), but overall the evidence is still limited. Critics argue that it tends to fit brain activity into existing psychoanalytic concepts.
Key figure
Mark Solms

Core theories

  • Dreaming and the Forebrain Mechanism 2000Studies of patients with brain damage show that REM sleep and dreaming do not always coincide. Damage to certain forebrain regions linked to motivation and reward can stop dreaming.

Key people

  • Mark Solms 1961–

Key concepts

  • Affective ConsciousnessFeeling itself is the most basic form of consciousness, arising from deep in the brainstem.
