Emotion-Focused Therapy

School·1988·情绪聚焦治疗

Treats emotion as the core of change: first helping people become aware of and accept their real feelings, and then using those feelings to change patterns in relationships or in the self.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Humanism and Meaning
Landmark
Greenberg and Johnson's Emotionally Focused Therapy for Couples
What the evidence says
The couple version has support from several controlled trials and meta-analyses. The individual version has some evidence for depression and similar conditions.
Key figure
Leslie Greenberg

Core theories

  • Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy 1988Repeated conflict between partners is seen as a cycle in which attachment needs cannot be expressed. Through helping each partner voice hidden emotions, safety in the relationship is rebuilt.

Key people

  • Leslie Greenberg 1945–
  • Sue Johnson 1947–2024

Key concepts

  • Pursue-Withdraw CycleOne partner pursues for a response, the other withdraws, and each reinforces the other in a vicious cycle.
