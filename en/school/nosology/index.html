Modern Psychiatric Classification

School·1899·现代精神病学分类

Mental disorders are not independent symptoms. They can be grouped into different 'disease units' according to onset, course and outcome.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Kraepelin's sixth edition of his textbook distinguishes dementia praecox from manic-depressive illness by course
What the evidence says
The method of grouping by course and symptoms is still in use. Kraepelin's dichotomy itself has been substantially revised.
Key figure
Emil Kraepelin

Core theories

  • Kraepelin's Dichotomy 1899Psychotic illnesses are divided into two major groups: one begins early in life and declines (dementia praecox), and the other occurs in episodes and can recover (manic-depressive illness).
  • Concept of Schizophrenia 1911The core of the illness is not necessarily decline into dementia. It is a 'splitting' between thought, emotion, and reality, and its course varies.
