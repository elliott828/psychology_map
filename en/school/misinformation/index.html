Misinformation and Polarization

School·2018·错误信息与极化研究

Studies why people believe and share false information, how groups move toward polarization, and which corrections work.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
A large-scale analysis of how true and false news spread on Twitter is published in Science
What the evidence says
Research finds that false news spreads faster and farther. The actual role of 'echo chambers' in polarization, and the effect of platform interventions, are still debated.
Key figure
Soroush Vosoughi

Core theories

  • Inoculation Theory 1961Like a vaccine, people are first exposed to a small dose of a false argument along with a refutation. Afterward they are harder to persuade by the full disinformation.
