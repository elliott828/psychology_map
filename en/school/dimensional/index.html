Dimensional Models (RDoC / HiTOP)

School·2010·维度化模型（RDoC／HiTOP）

Mental problems are not categories of 'present or absent' but continuous dimensions (such as depression, fear and thought disorder). Research should classify by function and brain circuits rather than by diagnostic labels.

Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
The NIMH releases the RDoC (Research Domain Criteria) framework
What the evidence says
The HiTOP dimensional structure has substantial data support. RDoC, as a research framework, has not yet produced clear clinical tools.
Key figure
Thomas Insel

Core theories

  • Research Domain Criteria 2010Studies psychiatric problems across genes, circuits, and behavior, using functional dimensions such as negative valence, positive valence, cognition, social processes, and arousal, rather than diagnostic categories.
  • The p Factor 2014Psychiatric problems are correlated with one another. This suggests there may be a common 'general psychopathology factor' behind them, similar to g in intelligence testing.
  • Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology 2017Based on how symptoms co-occur, arranged in layers from bottom to top: specific symptoms, syndromes, subfactors, spectra, and at the top a general 'p factor'.
