Large Language Models and Cognition

School·2022·大语言模型与认知研究

On one hand, uses psychological tasks to test whether language models have abilities such as reasoning and theory of mind. On the other hand, uses them to generate materials, simulate participants and analyze text.

Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●●

Thread
Mind and Cognition
Landmark
ChatGPT is released, and language models become both objects and tools of psychological research
What the evidence says
Models perform close to humans on some psychological tests, but there is no consensus on whether they 'understand,' or on how their processing resembles human processing.
Key figure
Michal Kosinski

Core theories

  • Theory-of-Mind Tests for Language Models 2023Gives large language models human theory-of-mind tasks to see whether they understand that others can have thoughts different from their own. The results were surprising, but there is no consensus on how to interpret them.
