Digital Mental Health

School·2017·数字心理健康

Provides assessment and treatment through programs, phone apps or online courses, so that regions with few resources can also get support, while raising issues of data privacy and quality.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
The US FDA approves the first prescription digital therapeutic
What the evidence says
Internet-delivered CBT and apps have many randomized trials, but in the real world users' adherence is low and effects are smaller than in face-to-face treatment.
Key figure
Alison Darcy

Core theories

  • CBT Delivered by a Conversational Agent 2017Breaks structured cognitive behavioral exercises, such as identifying thoughts and reappraising them, into everyday chat conversations. This gives people who have little access to therapists some of the help.
