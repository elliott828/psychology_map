Moral Foundations Theory

School·2012·道德基础理论

Human moral judgments come from several intuitions: care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity and liberty. Conservatives and liberals weight them differently.

Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Haidt's The Righteous Mind: moral intuitions have several foundations, and political differences lie in which ones are valued
What the evidence says
The number of foundations and how they are divided is disputed, and the scales have poor measurement invariance across cultures. The view that moral intuition comes before reasoning has more support.
Key figure
Jonathan Haidt
