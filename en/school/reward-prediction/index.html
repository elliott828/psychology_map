Reward Prediction Error

School·1997·奖赏预测误差

Dopamine neurons fire when a reward is unexpected and do not fire when a reward is fully predicted. This is exactly the 'prediction error' used in reinforcement learning algorithms.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
Schultz, Dayan and Montague's 'A Neural Substrate of Prediction and Reward'
What the evidence says
Dopamine neurons encode the difference between actual reward and expectation, a finding repeatedly confirmed in monkeys, rats and humans. How far it explains motivation and addiction is still debated.
Key figure
Wolfram Schultz

Core theories

  • Dopamine Reward Prediction Error 1997Dopamine neurons do not simply fire to a reward itself. They fire to outcomes that are better or worse than expected: an unexpected reward increases firing, an expected one leaves it unchanged, and a disappointment decreases it.
