Ego Psychology

School·1936·自我心理学

Focuses on how the ego regulates conflicts among instinct, reality and conscience, using defense mechanisms to protect the self. Adaptation becomes the focus of analysis.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Unconscious and Depth
Landmark
Anna Freud's The Ego and the Mechanisms of Defence
What the evidence says
Research on defense mechanisms (such as the relationship between the maturity of defenses and adaptation) has some empirical support, but the overall structural theory of the ego lacks testing.
Key figure
Anna Freud

Core theories

  • Ego Defense Mechanisms 1936To reduce anxiety, the ego unconsciously uses methods such as denial, projection, rationalization, and sublimation to cope with conflict.
  • Hierarchy of Defenses 1977Defenses can be sorted into pathological (such as projection), immature, neurotic, and mature (such as humor and sublimation). The more mature the defense, the better it supports adjustment.

Key people

Key concepts

  • Defense MechanismMental strategies people use, often without awareness, to reduce anxiety.
