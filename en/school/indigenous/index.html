Indigenous Psychology

School·1971·本土心理学

Psychology developed in the West does not always apply to other societies. Each culture should study its own psychology with its own concepts and methods, such as 'amae,' 'relationship' and 'face.'

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Doi Takeo's The Anatomy of Dependence ('amae'); Chinese indigenous psychology, developed by Yang Kuo-shu in the 1980s
What the evidence says
It has proposed many illuminating concepts, but most have not yet been subjected to systematic, replicable empirical testing.
Key figure
Takeo Doi

Core theories

  • Amae 1971The wish to be indulged by others and to depend on their goodwill. It is common in Japanese families and workplaces. Doi argued it is the key to understanding the Japanese mind.
  • Face and Favor 1987In Chinese social exchange, favor (renqing) is a resource and face (mianzi) reflects social standing. Together they form a 'power game' of mutual exchange.
