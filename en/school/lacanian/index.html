Lacanian Psychoanalysis

School·1953·拉康派

The unconscious is not a storehouse of instincts but is structured like a language. A person becomes a subject within the discourse of the 'Other.'

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Landmark
Lacan's Rome Report, 'Return to Freud,' treats the unconscious as structured like language
What the evidence says
It is used mainly in philosophy, literary theory and clinical circles in continental Europe and Latin America, and it is not tested by empirical methods.
