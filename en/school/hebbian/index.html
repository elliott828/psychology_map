Hebbian Learning and Plasticity

School·1949·赫布学习与神经可塑性

Learning means changes in the connections between neurons: cells that often fire together become more strongly connected. This became a basic rule for later neural network models.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
Hebb's The Organization of Behavior proposes that neurons active at the same time strengthen their connection
What the evidence says
Synaptic plasticity phenomena such as long-term potentiation provide much experimental support for this idea.
Key figure
Donald Hebb

Core theories

  • Cell Assembly 1949A group of neurons that are repeatedly activated together forms an 'assembly'. This assembly is the physical form of a concept or memory in the brain.
  • Long-Term Potentiation 1973Giving a neural pathway a few strong stimuli makes its signal transmission stronger for hours to weeks, as if it has 'remembered' the stimulation.

Key people

  • Donald Hebb 1904–1985
  • Terje Lomo 1935–
  • Tim Bliss 1940–

Key concepts

  • EngramThe physical trace a specific memory leaves in the brain.
  • NeuroplasticityThe brain changes its connections and structure in response to experience.
  • Synaptic StrengtheningA cellular phenomenon in which connections grow stronger with use.
