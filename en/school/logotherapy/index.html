Logotherapy

School·1946·意义治疗

A person's most basic motivation is the search for meaning in life. Even in suffering that cannot be changed, one can choose an attitude toward it, and find meaning in doing so.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Humanism and Meaning
Landmark
Frankl's Man's Search for Meaning (German edition, 1946)
What the evidence says
There is much research support for 'a sense of meaning is related to mental health,' but there are few controlled trials of logotherapy itself.
Key figure
Viktor Frankl

Core theories

  • Will to Meaning 1946A person's most basic drive is not pleasure or power, but the search for life's meaning. A person can choose their attitude in any situation.
  • Paradoxical Intention 1960The things we most try to avoid are the most likely to happen. So deliberately wishing for them to happen can break the cycle of anxiety.
