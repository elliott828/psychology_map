Post-Jungian and Archetypal Psychology

School·1949·后荣格与原型心理学

Developments after Jung: Neumann and Fordham studied the development of consciousness and the ego; Hillman developed an image-centered archetypal psychology. There is also sandplay and cross-cultural application.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Landmark

What the evidence says
Some claims of the developmental school can be compared with attachment research, while archetypal psychology belongs to the interpretive tradition.
Key figure
Erich Neumann

Core theories

  • Developmental School of Analytical Psychology 1944Fordham argued that the 'self' exists from infancy and unfolds gradually through the relationship with the mother. Neumann traced the development of consciousness through myth.
  • Sandplay Therapy 1966The client arranges small objects in a sand tray and builds scenes, expressing in symbolic form inner states that are hard to put into words. The therapist accompanies them.
  • Archetypal Psychology 1975The psyche is not run by the ego. It is a theater of many images. Symptoms are the soul's way of speaking, and therapy should listen to these images rather than try to 'fix' them.
