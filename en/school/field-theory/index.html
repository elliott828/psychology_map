Field Theory and Group Dynamics

School·1936·场论与群体动力

Behavior depends not only on the person or only on the environment, but on the 'field' formed by both. From this he founded group dynamics, studies of leadership styles and action research.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Lewin's Principles of Topological Psychology: behavior is a function of person and environment
What the evidence says
'Behavior is shaped jointly by the person and the situation' is a widely accepted framework. The specific models he proposed (life space, the three steps of change) are more heuristic tools.
Key figure
Kurt Lewin

Core theories

  • Life Space and Field Theory 1936A person's behavior is determined by their 'life space' at the moment: their own state plus the environment as they perceive it. Both are required.
  • Leadership Climates Experiment 1939The same group of boys, under authoritarian leadership, showed more dependence and aggression. Under democratic leadership they were more cooperative. Under laissez-faire leadership, they were less productive.
  • Expectancy Theory of Work Motivation 1964Whether a person puts in effort depends on three beliefs: that effort will lead to performance, that performance will lead to rewards, and that the rewards are valuable to them.
  • Three Levels of Organizational Culture 1985Organizational culture has three levels: visible behaviors and artifacts, publicly stated values, and unconscious basic assumptions. The deepest layer is the hardest to change.
