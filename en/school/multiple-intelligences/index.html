Multiple Intelligences

School·1983·多元智力

Intelligence is not one thing. Language, logic-mathematics, spatial, musical, bodily-kinesthetic, interpersonal and intrapersonal abilities are distinct, but schools value only one or two of them.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Gardner's Frames of Mind proposes seven relatively independent intelligences
What the evidence says
The 'intelligences' are not fully independent of one another; they remain positively correlated, and they lack testable measures. Still, they have had a large influence on educational practice.
Key figure
Howard Gardner

Core theories

  • Triarchic Theory of Intelligence 1985Intelligence includes analytical, creative, and practical abilities. Schools and IQ tests mainly measure only analytical ability.
  • Eight Intelligences 1999Linguistic, logical-mathematical, spatial, musical, bodily-kinesthetic, interpersonal, intrapersonal, and naturalist intelligences. Each person has different strengths, so education should be tailored to the learner.
