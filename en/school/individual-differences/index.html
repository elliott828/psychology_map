Individual Differences

School·1869·个体差异研究

Studies not only general laws of human beings but also the differences between people, and invents statistical methods for measuring those differences.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Galton's Hereditary Genius uses family-tree statistics to study the inheritance of ability
What the evidence says
Statistical methods such as correlation and regression are still in use; his claims that 'genius is determined by heredity' and his eugenics views have been rejected.
Key figure
Francis Galton

Core theories

  • Hereditary Genius and Eugenics 1869Outstanding people often come from the same families. From this, Galton concluded that talent is mainly inherited. He advocated improving humanity through selective breeding, a claim later used to justify discrimination and forced sterilization.
  • Correlation and Regression 1888The degree to which two variables change together can be quantified. Extreme values tend to be closer to the average on the next measurement. This is 'regression'.

Key people

  • Adolphe Quetelet 1796–1874
  • Francis Galton 1822–1911
  • Karl Pearson 1857–1936

Key concepts

  • Regression to the MeanAfter an extreme result, the next result is usually closer to average.
  • Correlation Is Not CausationTwo things changing together does not mean one causes the other.
