Existential Psychotherapy

School·1958·存在主义心理治疗

Anxiety and suffering often come from avoiding the basic conditions of human life: death, freedom, isolation and meaninglessness. Therapy helps people face them.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Humanism and Meaning
Landmark
Rollo May and others edit Existence, bringing European existentialism into American psychology
What the evidence says
Its themes (death, freedom, isolation, meaninglessness) have some empirical research, but the evidence for the therapy itself is limited.
Key figure
Rollo May

Core theories

  • Ultimate Concerns 1980People must face four unavoidable matters: death, freedom (and responsibility), isolation, and meaninglessness.
  • Terror Management Theory 1986Knowing we will die, people are driven by this fear to defend their cultural worldview and self-esteem. When reminded of death, people become more hostile toward different views.
