Child Study Movement

School·1883·儿童研究运动

From the late nineteenth century, researchers began systematically collecting data on children's development through questionnaires and observation. Children became a subject worth studying in their own right.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○
