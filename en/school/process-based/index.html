Process-Based Therapy

School·2018·基于过程的治疗

Stops arguing about which therapy is better and asks: for this person, which processes of change (such as flexibility and motivation) need to change? Then it selects the matching methods.

Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○
