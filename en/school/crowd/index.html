Crowd Psychology

School·1895·群体心理学

In groups, people are subject to suggestion and emotional contagion. Their behavior differs from when they are alone: more impulsive and more extreme. This is one of the earliest systematic accounts in social psychology.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Le Bon's The Crowd: individuals in a group lose their judgment and are swept along by emotion
What the evidence says
The claim that 'groups make people stupid and fanatical' is oversimplified. Later research found that group behavior mostly follows the group's own norms.
Key figure
Gustave Le Bon

Core theories

  • Contagion and Suggestibility in Crowds 1895In a crowd, individual responsibility is diluted, emotions spread quickly by contagion, and people are more easily swept along by simple slogans.
