Replication Crisis and Open Science

School·2011·可重复性危机与开放科学

Many published psychology findings fail to hold up in replication. Causes include small samples, flexible data analysis and publishing only positive results. Reforms include preregistration, open data and large-sample collaboration.

Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Simmons and colleagues publish 'False-Positive Psychology,' showing how flexible analysis can 'prove' absurd conclusions
What the evidence says
In the large-scale 2015 replication project, roughly a third to 40 percent of studies replicated. Reforms such as preregistration have already noticeably improved research quality.
Key figure
Joseph Simmons

Core theories

  • Researcher Degrees of Freedom and p-hacking 2011If you try several analysis methods on the same data and look at many outcomes, you can nearly always find a 'significant' result. So published significant results may be false.
  • Reproducibility Project: Psychology 2015A project tried to repeat 100 studies published in three leading journals. Fewer than half succeeded, which prompted a rethinking of practices across the field.
