Psychiatric Genomics

School·2014·精神疾病基因组学

Uses genome data from hundreds of thousands of people to find sites associated with mental disorders. The conclusion is that these disorders are caused by hundreds or thousands of variants, each with a tiny effect.

Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
The Psychiatric Genomics Consortium reports 108 associated genetic loci for schizophrenia in Nature
What the evidence says
Several loci have been replicated in independent samples. Each locus has a very small effect, and together they explain only part of the genetic risk.
Key figure
Patrick F. Sullivan

Core theories

  • Polygenic Architecture of Psychiatric Disorders 2009Schizophrenia, depression, and similar disorders are not determined by a single 'causal gene'. They arise from many gene variants with tiny effects, which add up, together with environmental factors, to create risk.
