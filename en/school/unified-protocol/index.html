Unified Protocol

School·2011·跨诊断统一方案

Different anxiety and depressive disorders share mechanisms such as emotional avoidance and rumination, so the same set of skills training is used to address many problems.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Barlow and colleagues publish a unified treatment protocol for emotional disorders
What the evidence says
Randomized controlled trials show effects similar to protocols aimed at a single disorder, but the number of studies and the long-term follow-up remain limited.
Key figure
David H. Barlow

Core theories

  • Unified Protocol for Transdiagnostic Treatment 2011Common emotional disorders such as anxiety and depression share a common process: intense negative emotion and avoidance of it. So one protocol teaches people to face and regulate emotion.
