Biopsychosocial Model

School·1977·生物—心理—社会模型

Illness and health cannot be explained by the body alone. They must be viewed at three levels, biological, psychological and social, and in terms of their interaction.

Core theories

  • Engel's Critique of the Biomedical Model 1977It is not enough for doctors to look only at biological markers of disease. They must also consider the patient's psychological state, family, and social circumstances.
  • Diathesis-Stress Model 1977Mental disorders are triggered by an interaction between an inherited or early-formed vulnerability and later stressful events.
