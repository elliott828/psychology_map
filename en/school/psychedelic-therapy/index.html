Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy

School·2021·迷幻药辅助治疗

Under the supervision of trained therapists, patients take drugs such as MDMA or psilocybin, combined with psychotherapy before and after, for trauma and depression.

Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Results of phase 3 trials of MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD are published
What the evidence says
Early trials show large effects, but most participants can guess whether they received the drug, which breaks blinding. In 2024 the US FDA did not approve the MDMA therapy; the evidence is still under evaluation.
Key figure
Jennifer M. Mitchell

Core theories

  • Psilocybin and Mystical-Type Experience 2006Taking psilocybin in a controlled setting with support can produce intense 'mystical-type' experiences in some people. These people report improvements in meaning and mood months later.
