ABCD Study

School·2015·青少年脑认知发展队列

Follows more than 10,000 nine- and ten-year-old children for ten years, repeatedly collecting brain imaging, cognitive tests and life surveys to see how the brain changes with experience.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Lifespan Development
Landmark
The Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development study launches in the United States, following more than 10,000 children
What the evidence says
It provides an unprecedented scale of data. Its findings also show that brain-behavior associations are often very small and require thousands of people to detect reliably.
Key figure
B. J. Casey

Core theories

  • Dual Systems Model of Adolescent Brain Development 2008The drive for novelty and reward strengthens early in adolescence, while the systems responsible for planning and self-control mature later. In this period of 'gas before brakes', risk-taking becomes more likely.
