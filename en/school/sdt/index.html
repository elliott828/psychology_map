Self-Determination Theory

School·1985·自我决定理论

People have three basic psychological needs: autonomy, competence and relatedness. When these needs are met, intrinsic motivation is stronger and people are healthier.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Humanism and Meaning
Landmark
Deci and Ryan's Intrinsic Motivation and Self-Determination in Human Behavior
What the evidence says
The three needs (autonomy, competence and relatedness) have wide support across cultures and domains, and there is debate about whether these 'basic needs' are universal.
Key figure
Edward Deci

Core theories

  • Overjustification Effect 1973An activity done out of interest can lose that interest once external rewards 'buy' it. The intrinsic motivation may decline.
  • Basic Psychological Needs 2000When the three needs of autonomy, competence, and relatedness are met, people are more engaged and happier. When they are controlled, motivation actually declines.
