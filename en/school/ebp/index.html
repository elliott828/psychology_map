Evidence-Based Practice in Psychology

School·1995·循证心理实践

Clinical decisions should combine three things: the best available research evidence, the therapist's professional experience, and the patient's characteristics and preferences.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
A working group of American Psychological Association Division 12 publishes its report on 'empirically supported treatments'
What the evidence says
It is a methodological framework rather than a therapy. How to balance 'evidence' against 'individual differences' is still debated.
Key figure
Dianne L. Chambless

Core theories

  • Empirically Supported Treatments 1998Lists treatments judged 'effective' for different conditions, based on evidence from randomized controlled trials.
  • Three Components of EBPP 2006Three components are required together: the best available research evidence, clinical expertise, and the characteristics of the patient, including culture and preferences.

Key people

  • Dianne L. Chambless 1948–2023

Key concepts

  • Randomized Controlled TrialRandomly assigning participants to a treatment group and a control group, then comparing outcomes.
