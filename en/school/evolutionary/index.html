Evolutionary Psychology

School·1992·进化心理学

Treats psychological mechanisms as products of natural selection in the ancestral environment, and starts from the question 'what adaptive problem does it solve?' to propose testable hypotheses.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
Barkow, Cosmides and Tooby edit The Adapted Mind
What the evidence says
Some specific predictions, such as vigilance toward cheaters, have support, but many have failed to replicate; the overall framework of 'massive modularity' is highly controversial.
Key figure
Leda Cosmides

Core theories

  • Kin Selection and Inclusive Fitness 1964Helping relatives who share your genes can still pass your genes on. So altruistic behavior can be kept by natural selection.
  • Cheater-Detection Module 1989The same logic puzzle gets far more correct answers when framed as catching someone who breaks a rule. This suggests humans may have evolved a specialized reasoning mechanism for spotting cheaters in reciprocal exchange.
  • Sexual Strategies Theory 1993Men and women show statistical differences in their preferences for short-term and long-term mates. These differences are explained as the result of unequal parental investment.
