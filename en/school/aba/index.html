Applied Behavior Analysis

School·1968·应用行为分析

Uses functional analysis to identify the antecedents and consequences of behavior, then uses reinforcement, prompting and other means to change specific, measurable behaviors.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Learning and Behavior
Landmark
The Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis begins publication; Baer, Wolf and Risley propose seven characteristics of the field
What the evidence says
Much single-subject research has accumulated in special education, developmental disabilities and animal training; findings on intensive intervention for autism are mixed and raise ethical debates.
Key figure
Donald Baer

Core theories

  • Seven Dimensions of ABA 1968A behavioral intervention should have seven features: it targets socially important behavior, it is measurable, it demonstrates cause, it is clearly described, it has a theoretical basis, it is effective, and its effects last.
  • Early Intensive Behavioral Intervention 1987Young autistic children receive several dozen hours of behavioral training a week to improve language and social skills.
