WEIRD Samples Critique

School·2010·样本多样性批评（WEIRD）

Most psychology participants come from Western, educated, industrialized, rich and democratic societies. On many tasks, these people are not 'representative of humanity.'

Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan's 'The Weirdest People in the World?'
What the evidence says
Analyses of the sources of samples in published research, and cross-cultural comparisons, provide strong support for this critique.

Joseph Henrich
