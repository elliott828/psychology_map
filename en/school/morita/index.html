Morita Therapy

School·1919·森田疗法

The more anxiety is pushed away, the worse it gets. The principle is 'go with the flow, do what needs to be done': accept that the symptoms exist while doing what needs doing.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Shoma Morita founds this therapy for patients with neurotic tendencies
What the evidence says
There are some clinical studies and case reports in Japan, China and elsewhere, but large-scale controlled trials are lacking.
Key figure
Shoma Morita

Core theories

  • Morita's Model of Shinkeishitsu 1928Neurotic people pay excessive attention to their discomfort, and the more they focus on it, the worse it gets. Treatment is to help them accept their symptoms while returning to everyday action.

Key people

  • Shoma Morita 1874–1938

Key concepts

  • Psychic Interaction (Seishin Kōgo Sayō)A cycle in which attention focused on discomfort makes the discomfort grow stronger.
