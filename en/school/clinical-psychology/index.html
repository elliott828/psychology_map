Birth of Clinical Psychology

School·1896·临床心理学诞生

Psychology no longer studies only 'ordinary people' in the laboratory. It begins to assess and help specific individuals who are struggling.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Witmer opens a psychological clinic at the University of Pennsylvania to use psychological research to help children with learning difficulties
What the evidence says
This marks the starting point of a profession rather than a theory to be tested. The 'scientist-practitioner' training model later became the standard.
