Screens and Adolescent Mental Health

School·2017·屏幕与青少年心理之争

Depression and anxiety among adolescents have risen over the past decade. Is this related to smartphones and social media? One side points to time trends; the other notes that associations at the individual level are very small.

Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

Thread
Lifespan Development
Landmark
Twenge's iGen proposes that smartphones are linked to the rise in adolescent depression
What the evidence says
Large-sample studies find only a very small negative correlation between screen use and well-being. The direction of causation and the role of specific social media use are still debated.
Key figure
Jean Twenge

Core theories

  • Smartphones and Adolescent Mental Health 2017Argues that since the early 2010s, the spread of smartphones and social media has left teenagers lonelier, more anxious, and sleeping less. Critics argue the effect is very small and that other factors cannot be ruled out.
