Digital Footprints and Personality Prediction

School·2013·数字足迹与人格预测

Likes, text and location that people leave online can be used to estimate traits such as the Big Five, which takes personality measurement beyond questionnaires.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Kosinski and colleagues predict personality traits from social media likes
What the evidence says
The accuracy is slightly higher than predictions from acquaintances' ratings, but predictions for individuals still carry substantial error. It also raises controversies over privacy and manipulation.
Key figure
Michal Kosinski

Core theories

  • Likes Predict Personality 2013The posts a person likes on social media can predict their gender, political leanings, and Big Five personality scores fairly accurately. This shows that digital footprints carry private information.
