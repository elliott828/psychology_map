Behavior Genetics

School·1960·行为遗传学

Uses twins, adoptive families and genetic data to estimate how much of the difference in a trait comes from genes and how much from the environment.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
Fuller and Thompson publish Behavior Genetics
What the evidence says
Twin and adoption studies repeatedly find that most psychological traits have a moderate genetic component; this is one of the most robust conclusions in psychology.
Key figure
Robert Plomin

Core theories

  • Three Laws of Behavior Genetics 2000First, all human traits are influenced by genes. Second, growing up in the same family has less effect than genes do. Third, a considerable part of the variation cannot be explained by genes or family.
