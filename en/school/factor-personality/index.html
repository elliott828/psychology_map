Factor-Analytic Personality Models

School·1947·因素人格模型

Uses factor analysis to distill a few basic dimensions from large numbers of personality descriptions. Eysenck proposed two or three, and Cattell sixteen.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Eysenck's Dimensions of Personality uses factor analysis to propose extraversion and neuroticism
What the evidence says
Extraversion and neuroticism have been widely replicated; Eysenck's third dimension (psychoticism) and Cattell's 16 factors have not been stably replicated.
Key figure
Hans Eysenck

Core theories

  • Cattell 16PF 1949Factor analysis was used to extract 16 'source traits', and a questionnaire was built to measure them.
  • Eysenck PEN Model 1967The core of personality is a small number of dimensions, each with a biological basis. For example, extraverts have lower cortical arousal, so they seek more stimulation.
