Predictive Processing

School·2010·预测加工

The brain does not passively receive signals. It continually predicts what it will feel next, and adjusts only when prediction and input differ.

Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Mind and Cognition
Landmark
Friston publishes a review of the free-energy principle
What the evidence says
As a framework it unifies many findings, but it is so flexible that it is unclear which results would overturn it.
Key figure
Karl Friston

Core theories

  • Free-Energy Principle 2010All living systems try to reduce their 'surprise' about the states they are in. Perception and action are both ways of reducing surprise.
  • Computational Psychiatry 2012Symptoms such as hallucinations, delusions, and addiction are understood as biased learning and inference in the brain. Mathematical models describe these biases.
