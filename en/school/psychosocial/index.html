Psychosocial Development

School·1950·心理社会发展阶段

Each stage of life has a core psychological conflict, such as 'identity versus role confusion' in adolescence. How well it is handled affects later development.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Lifespan Development
Landmark
Erikson's Childhood and Society proposes eight stages of life
What the evidence says
There is much research on the concept of identity, but the order and universality of the eight stages lack direct testing.
Key figure
Erik Erikson

Core theories

  • Eight Stages of Man 1950Eight stages center on trust, autonomy, initiative, industry, identity, intimacy, generativity, and integrity. Failing to handle a stage's task leaves lasting difficulties.
  • Identity Statuses 1966Identity can be sorted into four statuses based on whether a person has explored options and whether they have made a commitment: achievement, moratorium, foreclosure, and diffusion.

Key concepts
