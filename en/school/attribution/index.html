Attribution Theory

School·1958·归因理论

People look for causes of their own and others' behavior. Whether they attribute it to personality or to the situation affects how they evaluate others and how they respond.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Heider's The Psychology of Interpersonal Relations: people look for the causes of others' behavior like naive scientists
What the evidence says
The tendency to attribute others' behavior to their personality is robust in Western samples, weaker in East Asian samples, and varies clearly across conditions.
Key figure
Fritz Heider

Core theories

  • Fundamental Attribution Error 1977When others are late, it is because they are irresponsible. When we are late, it is because of traffic. People tend to explain others by their personality, but explain themselves by the situation.
