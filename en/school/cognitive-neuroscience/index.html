Cognitive Neuroscience

School·1979·认知神经科学

Combines the task designs of cognitive psychology with lesion studies, brain imaging and electrophysiology, asking how thinking, memory and attention are carried out in the brain.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
Gazzaniga and cognitive psychologist George Miller collaborate in the late 1970s to name this field
What the evidence says
Brain imaging has found stable functional networks, but small samples, statistical thresholds and the 'reverse inference' problem make many individual findings hard to replicate.
Key figure
Michael Gazzaniga

Core theories

  • Neural Correlates of Consciousness 1990Rather than asking directly what consciousness is, this approach looks for differences in brain activity between trials where people report seeing something and trials where they do not.
