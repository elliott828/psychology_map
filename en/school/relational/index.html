Relational Psychoanalysis

School·1983·关系精神分析

Analysis is not a doctor objectively observing a patient but an interaction between two people. The therapist's own feelings and reactions are part of the treatment.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Unconscious and Depth
Landmark
Greenberg and Mitchell's Object Relations in Psychoanalytic Theory
What the evidence says
There is substantial evidence that the therapeutic relationship relates to outcomes. The specific claims about mutual construction come mainly from clinical reflection.
Key figure
Stephen Mitchell

Core theories

  • Intersubjectivity and Mutual Recognition 1988In therapy, both people are subjects. Change happens through mutual recognition and interaction, not through one-sided interpretation.

Key people

  • Stephen Mitchell 1946–2000
  • Jessica Benjamin 1946–

Key concepts

  • EnactmentTherapist and client unknowingly act out the old relationship patterns of both.
