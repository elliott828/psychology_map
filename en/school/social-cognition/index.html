Social Cognition

School·1984·社会认知

Uses the methods of cognitive psychology to study how people understand others and themselves: how they categorize people, form impressions and remember information about others, and as a result develop biases and errors.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Fiske and Taylor's Social Cognition
What the evidence says
The distinctions among schemas, stereotypes, and controlled versus automatic processing have considerable support. Many social priming effects have failed to replicate.
Key figure
Susan Fiske

Core theories

  • Self-Fulfilling Prophecy 1968When teachers believe certain students have potential, those students may later perform better. This is linked to the attention and feedback teachers give without intending to.
  • Social Priming 1996Being unconsciously exposed to words related to 'the elderly' makes people walk more slowly. The finding was highly influential, but it has largely failed to replicate.
