Evidence for Psychodynamic Therapy

School·2010·心理动力治疗的实证研究

Uses randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses to test long-term and short-term psychodynamic therapy. It finds effects similar to other therapies, and gains that continue to grow after treatment ends.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

What the evidence says
Meta-analyses find psychodynamic therapy roughly comparable to other therapies, but questions of methodological quality and researcher allegiance remain.
