Ecological Systems Theory

School·1979·生态系统理论

Children live in nested layers of environment: family, school, community policies and culture, and the historical era. Development is the result of the person's ongoing interaction with these environments.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Lifespan Development
Landmark
Bronfenbrenner's The Ecology of Human Development
What the evidence says
It is a framework that places family, school, community, institutions and era side by side to organize research. It does not make specific predictions on its own.
Key figure
Urie Bronfenbrenner

Core theories

  • Parenting Styles 1966Parents can be divided into: warm and rule-setting (authoritative), demanding with little warmth (authoritarian), and warm with few rules (permissive).
  • Nested Environmental Systems 1979The environment is arranged from near to far. Closest is what the child experiences directly, such as home and school. Next are the links between those settings, then indirect influences such as a parent's job, then culture and institutions, all over time.
