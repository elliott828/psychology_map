Sociocultural Theory

School·1934·社会文化理论

Children first accomplish things through interaction with others, then internalize those interactions as their own thinking. Language and symbols are tools of thought.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Lifespan Development
Landmark
Vygotsky's Thought and Language: higher mental functions come from social interaction and are mediated by symbolic tools
What the evidence says
Ideas such as 'language and cultural tools shape thinking' and the 'zone of proximal development' have considerable support in instructional research, but many core concepts are hard to test directly.
Key figure
Lev Vygotsky

Core theories

  • Zone of Proximal Development 1934Between what a child can do alone and what they can do with help, there is a zone that is best suited for learning. Teaching should target this zone.
  • Scaffolding 1976Adults give just enough help when a child needs it, then gradually withdraw it as the child's ability grows, much like the scaffolding used in building.

Key people

  • Lev Vygotsky 1896–1934
  • Jerome Bruner 1915–2016

Key concepts

  • Private SpeechChildren talk to themselves while doing things, to help them think and regulate their behavior.
