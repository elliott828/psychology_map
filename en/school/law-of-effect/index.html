Law of Effect

School·1898·效果律

The consequences of a behavior determine how likely it is to occur again: actions that satisfy an animal are kept, and those that cause discomfort are eliminated.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Learning and Behavior
Landmark
Thorndike's puzzle box experiment: cats learn by trial and error to open the door, and actions that produce good results are kept
What the evidence says
'Behavior with good outcomes is more likely to be repeated' is a basic regularity that has been confirmed many times; only Thorndike's 'connection' explanation is no longer mainstream.
Key figure
Edward Thorndike

Core theories

  • Identical Elements Theory of Transfer 1901How much learning one thing helps with another depends on how many components the two share. So studying Latin does not make people better at thinking in general.
