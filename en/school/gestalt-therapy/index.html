Gestalt Therapy

School·1951·格式塔治疗

Emphasizes awareness of the 'here and now' and the processing of unfinished emotions. Techniques such as the empty chair and role play are used to act out inner conflicts.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Humanism and Meaning
Landmark
Perls, Hefferline and Goodman's Gestalt Therapy
What the evidence says
Techniques such as the empty chair have been effective in some studies, but controlled studies of the school as a whole are few.
Key figure
Fritz Perls

Core theories

  • Two-Chair Technique 1979The client sits in two chairs and takes turns speaking for each side of an inner conflict. Or the client speaks to a person imagined in an empty chair and says what was left unsaid.

Key people

  • Fritz Perls 1893–1970
  • Paul Goodman 1911–1972
  • Leslie Greenberg 1945–

Key concepts

  • Empty ChairSpeaking to an empty chair as if a person were sitting in it, to say what was left unsaid.
