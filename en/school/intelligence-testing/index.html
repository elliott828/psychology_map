Intelligence Testing

School·1905·智力测验

Uses standardized tasks to measure cognitive ability and converts the results into comparable scores (IQ). The original purpose was to help schools identify children who needed extra support.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●●

Thread
Individual Differences and Measurement
Landmark
Binet and Simon publish the first intelligence scale, used to identify children who needed special help
What the evidence says
IQ scores have a moderate power to predict academic and occupational performance; the cultural fairness of tests and their history of misuse need critical examination.
Key figure
Alfred Binet

Core theories

  • IQ and the Stanford-Binet Scale 1916The 'intelligence quotient' was originally mental age divided by chronological age. It was later changed to a standard score comparing a person with peers, with 100 as the average.
  • Flynn Effect 1984Each generation scores higher on IQ tests than the one before. This shows test scores are not determined entirely by genes. Nutrition, education, and environment also play a role.
