Functionalism

School·1890·机能主义

Rather than asking what the mind is made of, it asks what the mind does and how it helps survival. It also urges psychology to respond to practical problems such as education and work.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Mind and Cognition
Landmark
James's Principles of Psychology: consciousness is a flowing stream, and we should ask what it is for in adaptation
What the evidence says
It is more a research orientation than a testable theory; its sense of problems was inherited by later cognitive and applied psychology.
Key figure
William James

Core theories

  • James-Lange Theory of Emotion 1884We do not cry because we are sad. We feel sad because we cry. The feeling of bodily changes is the emotion itself.
  • Stream of Consciousness 1890Consciousness is not a string of isolated sensations. It is a continuous, shifting flow with fringes around it, and each person's consciousness is private.

Key people

Key concepts
