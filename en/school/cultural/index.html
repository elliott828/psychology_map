Cultural Psychology

School·1991·文化心理学

Culture is not just an external background. It is an inherent part of what shapes the self, emotion and ways of thinking, and many conclusions treated as 'universal' hold only in Western samples.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

Thread
Society and Culture
Landmark
Markus and Kitayama publish Culture and the Self
What the evidence says
Differences between Eastern and Western views of the self have been replicated in several studies, but the effect sizes are moderate and individual variation is large. They should not be reduced to 'Eastern collectivism, Western individualism.'
Key figure
Hazel Rose Markus

Core theories

  • Independent and Interdependent Self 1991Western cultures tend to see the self as an independent individual with stable traits. East Asian cultures tend to see the self as part of a web of relationships.
