ICD-11 and Complex PTSD

School·2019·ICD-11 与复杂性 PTSD

The World Health Organization's new diagnostic system lists the consequences of long-term repeated trauma separately as 'complex PTSD,' and restructures many diagnoses.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
The World Health Assembly adopts ICD-11
What the evidence says
Complex PTSD can be distinguished from PTSD in samples from several countries, but its overlap with borderline personality disorder is still debated.
Key figure
Judith Herman

Core theories

  • Complex PTSD 1992The effects of long-term, repeated trauma, such as childhood abuse or prolonged domestic violence, go beyond flashbacks and avoidance. They include difficulties with emotion regulation, a negative sense of self, and relationship problems.
