Human Connectome

School·2010·人脑连接组

Rather than looking at brain regions one at a time, maps the connections across the whole brain as a graph and studies how patterns of connection relate to individual differences and disease.

Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
The US National Institutes of Health launches the Human Connectome Project
What the evidence says
Large-scale imaging data are public and widely used. The relationships between connectivity patterns and specific behaviors and symptoms are still weak.
