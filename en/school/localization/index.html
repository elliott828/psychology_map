Cerebral Localization

School·1861·脑功能定位

Different mental functions depend on different parts of the brain. The starting point was a patient who could only say the syllable 'Tan.' After his death, an autopsy showed damage to the left frontal lobe.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Thread
Brain and Biology
Landmark
Broca reports aphasia cases: patients with left frontal damage understood speech but could not speak
What the evidence says
Language, motor and visual functions do rely on relatively fixed brain areas, but the strict 'one area, one function' idea has been revised by network views.
Key figure
Paul Broca

Core theories

  • Wernicke-Geschwind Model 1874The motor part of speech lies in the front of the frontal lobe, and the comprehension part lies in the back of the temporal lobe. The two are linked by nerve fibers. Modern research holds that language involves a wider network.
  • Cortical Homunculus 1937During surgery, stimulating different points on the cortex makes patients feel or move different parts of the body. The hand and mouth take up a large share of the cortex.
