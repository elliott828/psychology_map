Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction

School·1979·正念减压

An eight-week group course that teaches people to deliberately and nonjudgmentally direct attention to the present breath, body and thoughts, in order to cope with stress and pain.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Thread
Clinical and Mental Health
Landmark
Kabat-Zinn opens a stress reduction clinic at the University of Massachusetts Medical Center
What the evidence says
There is evidence of moderate effects for stress, chronic pain and recurrent depression. Study quality varies, and effects have sometimes been overstated in promotion.
Key figure
Jon Kabat-Zinn

Core theories

  • MBSR Eight-Week Program 1979An eight-week course of body scan, sitting meditation, yoga, and daily practice. Participants learn to notice the present moment with a non-judgmental attitude.

Key people
