Neural Correlates of Consciousness

Theory·1990·意识的神经相关物

Rather than asking directly what consciousness is, this approach looks for differences in brain activity between trials where people report seeing something and trials where they do not.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Cognitive Neuroscience
Landmark
Crick and Koch proposed searching for neural correlates of consciousness, starting with visual awareness.
What the evidence says
Activity linked to reports of consciousness has been found. But the question of why there is subjective experience remains unanswered, and the competing theories are hard to adjudicate.
