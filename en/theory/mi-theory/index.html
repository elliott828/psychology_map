Eight Intelligences

Theory·1999·八种智能

Linguistic, logical-mathematical, spatial, musical, bodily-kinesthetic, interpersonal, intrapersonal, and naturalist intelligences. Each person has different strengths, so education should be tailored to the learner.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Multiple Intelligences
Landmark
Gardner, 'Multiple Intelligences: New Horizons', added the naturalist intelligence.
What the evidence says
No evidence has shown these intelligences are independent of each other. The measurement of each intelligence lacks validity evidence.
