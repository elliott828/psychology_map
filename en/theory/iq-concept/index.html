IQ and the Stanford-Binet Scale

Theory·1916·智商的概念与斯坦福—比奈量表

The 'intelligence quotient' was originally mental age divided by chronological age. It was later changed to a standard score comparing a person with peers, with 100 as the average.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Intelligence Testing
Landmark
Terman revised the Binet scale into the Stanford-Binet scale, adopting Stern's concept of the IQ.
What the evidence says
IQ scores are stable and predict academic performance. But they measure a limited set of cognitive skills.
