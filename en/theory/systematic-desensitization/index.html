Systematic Desensitization

Theory·1958·系统脱敏

The person first learns to relax, then imagines or faces the feared situation step by step, from mild to severe. Anxiety is gradually reduced.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Behavior Therapy
Landmark
Wolpe, 'Psychotherapy by Reciprocal Inhibition'.
What the evidence says
It is effective for specific phobias. The explanation that relaxation inhibits anxiety has been replaced by exposure theory.
