Face and Favor

Theory·1987·人情与面子模型

In Chinese social exchange, favor (renqing) is a resource and face (mianzi) reflects social standing. Together they form a 'power game' of mutual exchange.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Indigenous Psychology
Landmark
Hwang Kwang-kuo, 'Face and Favor: The Chinese Power Game'.
What the evidence says
The model provides a theoretical framework and has been used in research in some Chinese societies. Confirmatory evidence is limited.
