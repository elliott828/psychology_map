Latent Learning

Theory·1930·潜伏学习

Without food rewards, rats seem not to have learned the maze. Once rewards are introduced, they immediately follow the route they already knew. Learning happened, but it was not shown in behavior.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Neo-Behaviorism
Landmark
Tolman and Honzik found that rats without rewards were still learning the maze.
What the evidence says
The finding has been repeated many times. It shows that learning and the performance of learned behavior can be separated.
