Two-Chair Technique

Theory·1979·两把椅子技术

The client sits in two chairs and takes turns speaking for each side of an inner conflict. Or the client speaks to a person imagined in an empty chair and says what was left unsaid.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Gestalt Therapy
Landmark
Greenberg published 'Resolving Splits: Use of the Two-Chair Technique'.
What the evidence says
Controlled studies support it for inner conflict and unfinished business. It is one of the best-supported techniques in emotion-focused therapy.

Key people

  • Leslie Greenberg 1945–
