Drive Theory and Unconscious Conflict

Theory·1905·驱力与无意识冲突

People are driven by instincts for sex and aggression, which conflict with the demands of reality and morality. The conflict is pushed into the unconscious and then appears as symptoms, dreams, and slips of the tongue.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Classical Psychoanalysis
Landmark
Freud, 'Three Essays on the Theory of Sexuality'.
What the evidence says
The claims that sex and aggression are basic drives, and that early stages determine personality, lack evidentiary support and have been replaced by later models. The 'unconscious conflict' perspective remains influential.
