Overjustification Effect

Theory·1973·过度理由效应

An activity done out of interest can lose that interest once external rewards 'buy' it. The intrinsic motivation may decline.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Self-Determination Theory
Landmark
Lepper, Greene, and Nisbett: children who liked drawing and were given rewards later drew less.
What the evidence says
Replicated many times in education and work settings. But not all rewards undermine interest, and the effect has limits.

Key people

  • Mark Lepper 1944–
