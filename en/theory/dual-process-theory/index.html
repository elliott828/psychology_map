Dual-Process Theory

Theory·2011·双系统（系统 1 与系统 2）

Thinking has a fast, automatic mode (System 1) and a slow, effortful mode (System 2). Many errors come from System 1 going unchecked by System 2.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Heuristics and Biases
Landmark
Kahneman, 'Thinking, Fast and Slow', turned the fast-intuition and slow-analysis distinction into a popular account.
What the evidence says
The distinction between fast and slow processing has a lot of evidence. But whether these are truly two independent 'systems' is questioned, and some priming studies cited in the book have failed to replicate.
