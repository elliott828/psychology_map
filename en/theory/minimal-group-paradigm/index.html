Minimal Group Paradigm

Theory·1971·最小群体范式

Simply being randomly assigned to a 'red group' or a 'blue group' leads people to give more resources to members of their own group.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Social Identity Theory
Landmark
Tajfel and colleagues found that random group assignment alone is enough to produce in-group favoritism.
What the evidence says
Replicated in many countries. But the strength of the favoritism and the explanations for its motivation vary.
