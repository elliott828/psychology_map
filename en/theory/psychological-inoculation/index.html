Inoculation Theory

Theory·1961·心理接种理论

Like a vaccine, people are first exposed to a small dose of a false argument along with a refutation. Afterward they are harder to persuade by the full disinformation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Misinformation and Polarization
Landmark
McGuire published experiments on resistance to persuasion (1961).
What the evidence says
Lab and some online studies show that giving a weakened false argument followed by refutation increases resistance. Long-term effects and effectiveness in real-world settings are still being studied.
