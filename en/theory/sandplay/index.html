Sandplay Therapy

Theory·1966·沙盘游戏

The client arranges small objects in a sand tray and builds scenes, expressing in symbolic form inner states that are hard to put into words. The therapist accompanies them.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Post-Jungian and Archetypal Psychology
Landmark
Kalff, 'Sandplay', with Kawai Hayao introducing it to Japan in 1965.
What the evidence says
There are many case studies of its use in child therapy, but few controlled studies. Evidence of its effectiveness is still limited.

Key people

  • Dora Kalff 1904–1990
  • Hayao Kawai 1928–2007
