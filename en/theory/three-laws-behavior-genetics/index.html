Three Laws of Behavior Genetics

Theory·2000·行为遗传学三定律

First, all human traits are influenced by genes. Second, growing up in the same family has less effect than genes do. Third, a considerable part of the variation cannot be explained by genes or family.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Behavior Genetics
Landmark
Turkheimer proposed the three empirical laws.
What the evidence says
The three laws hold up across many twin and adoption studies. They describe differences across populations and do not predict the fate of any one person.
