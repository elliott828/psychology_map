General Adaptation Syndrome

Theory·1936·一般适应综合征

Whatever the stimulus, the body passes through three stages: alarm, resistance, and exhaustion. Staying in the resistance stage for a long time wears the body down.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Stress Physiology
Landmark
Selye published a short note in Nature proposing the three stages of alarm, resistance, and exhaustion.
What the evidence says
The broad claim that long-term stress causes illness holds up. The three-stage model and the idea that stress is a nonspecific response have been revised.
