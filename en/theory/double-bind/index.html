Double Bind Theory

Theory·1956·双重束缚

A child who repeatedly receives two contradictory messages, and can neither avoid them nor comment on them, is caught in a bind.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Family Systems Therapy
Landmark
Bateson, Jackson, Haley, and Weakland, 'Toward a Theory of Schizophrenia'.
What the evidence says
The causal claim that contradictory communication causes schizophrenia has not been confirmed. But it encouraged research on family communication.
