Hierarchy of Needs

Theory·1943·需求层次

Physiological, safety, love and belonging, esteem, and self-actualization. These are the need categories Maslow arranged. He did not think they were strictly 'unlocked' one level at a time.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Hierarchy of Needs and Self-Actualization
Landmark
Maslow, 'A Theory of Human Motivation'.
What the evidence says
Need satisfaction is linked to well-being. But the 'level-by-level' order has not been confirmed in cross-cultural research. The pyramid diagram did not come from Maslow himself.
