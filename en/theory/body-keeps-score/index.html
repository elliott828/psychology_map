The Body Keeps the Score

Theory·1994·身体与创伤

Trauma is not only a matter of thoughts. It also stays in the body, showing up as hypervigilance, numbness, and physical symptoms. So treatment needs to attend to bodily sensations.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

Trauma Studies and PTSD
Landmark
van der Kolk, 'The Body Keeps the Score'.
What the evidence says
There is considerable evidence linking trauma to bodily reactions and autonomic dysregulation. But the evidence for specific methods such as yoga and somatic experiencing is uneven, and some claims go beyond the evidence.
