Long-Term Potentiation

Theory·1973·长时程增强

Giving a neural pathway a few strong stimuli makes its signal transmission stronger for hours to weeks, as if it has 'remembered' the stimulation.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Hebbian Learning and Plasticity
Landmark
Bliss and Lomo found that high-frequency stimulation produced lasting strengthening of synapses in the rabbit hippocampus.
What the evidence says
It is currently the most widely accepted cellular model of learning and memory. How it maps onto memory at the level of behavior is still being studied.
