DSM-III Operational Criteria

Theory·1980·DSM-III 的操作化诊断

Each disorder is defined by how many symptoms are present and how long they last. This reduced disagreement between doctors, but it also made diagnosis more like a checklist.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
DSM / ICD Diagnostic Systems
Landmark
DSM-III, led by Spitzer, introduced explicit symptom checklists and exclusion criteria.
What the evidence says
Inter-rater reliability clearly improved. But that does not mean diagnostic validity improved, and the 'neo-Kraepelinian' tendency has been criticized.
