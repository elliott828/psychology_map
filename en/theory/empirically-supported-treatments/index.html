Empirically Supported Treatments

Theory·1998·经验支持的疗法清单

Lists treatments judged 'effective' for different conditions, based on evidence from randomized controlled trials.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Evidence-Based Practice in Psychology
Landmark
Chambless and colleagues, 'Update on Empirically Validated Therapies, II'.
What the evidence says
The lists drew attention to outcome research. But they have been criticized for neglecting common factors and individual differences.
