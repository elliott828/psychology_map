Scaffolding

Theory·1976·脚手架

Adults give just enough help when a child needs it, then gradually withdraw it as the child's ability grows, much like the scaffolding used in building.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Sociocultural Theory
Landmark
Wood, Bruner, and Ross described how adults gradually withdraw support.
What the evidence says
Much educational research supports it as a teaching principle. But there is no standard for when to withdraw support.
