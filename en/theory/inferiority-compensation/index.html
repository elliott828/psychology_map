Inferiority and Compensation

Theory·1907·自卑与补偿

People are born with a sense of inferiority, and they respond by striving for superiority and compensating for their flaws. This can become a motive force, or it can grow into an excessive 'inferiority complex'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Individual Psychology
Landmark
Adler, 'Study of Organ Inferiority and Its Psychical Compensation'.
What the evidence says
'Feelings of inferiority drive behavior' is a common clinical observation. But it has not been tested as the core motive of everyone.
