Fluid and Crystallized Intelligence

Theory·1963·流体智力与晶体智力

Fluid intelligence is the ability to handle new problems, and it declines gradually after middle age. Crystallized intelligence is accumulated knowledge and skill, which can be maintained into old age.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
General Intelligence and Factor Analysis
Landmark
Cattell published the theory of fluid and crystallized intelligence.
What the evidence says
Fluid abilities decline earlier with age, while crystallized abilities last longer. This has been supported by lifespan research.
