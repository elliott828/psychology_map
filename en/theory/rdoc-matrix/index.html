Research Domain Criteria

Theory·2010·RDoC 研究领域标准

Studies psychiatric problems across genes, circuits, and behavior, using functional dimensions such as negative valence, positive valence, cognition, social processes, and arousal, rather than diagnostic categories.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Dimensional Models (RDoC / HiTOP)
Landmark
Insel and colleagues, 'Research Domain Criteria (RDoC): Toward a New Classification Framework'.
What the evidence says
It is a research framework for organizing studies of neural circuits and functional dimensions. It has not yet become a clinical tool.

  • Thomas Insel 1951–
