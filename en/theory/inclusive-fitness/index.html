Kin Selection and Inclusive Fitness

Theory·1964·亲缘选择与广义适合度

Helping relatives who share your genes can still pass your genes on. So altruistic behavior can be kept by natural selection.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School

Landmark

What the evidence says
It has been tested extensively in insects, birds, and other organisms. Its application to humans depends more on analogy.
