Left-Brain Interpreter

Theory·1978·左脑解释器

In split-brain patients, the left hemisphere makes up a plausible-sounding reason for behavior that the right hemisphere initiated. People may often be giving themselves reasons after the fact.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Split-Brain Research
Landmark
Gazzaniga and LeDoux described the left hemisphere fabricating reasons for behavior in 'The Integrated Mind'.
What the evidence says
The claim comes from a small number of split-brain patients. It is appealing, but there is little solid evidence from healthy people.

Key people
