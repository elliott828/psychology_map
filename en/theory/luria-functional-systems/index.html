Dynamic Localization of Higher Functions

Theory·1962·高级心理功能的动态定位

Complex mental activity depends on several brain areas working together. Damage to one area can affect many functions. Systematic case analysis is needed to find which link has been damaged.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Neuropsychology
Landmark
Luria, 'Higher Cortical Functions in Man'.
What the evidence says
The view that functions are carried out by systems made of several brain areas is now broadly accepted. The specific factor-analysis framework has been replaced in part by modern network research.
