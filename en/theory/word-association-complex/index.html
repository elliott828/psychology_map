Word Association and Complexes

Theory·1906·词语联想实验与情结

When certain words touch emotional issues, people respond more slowly or cannot answer. Jung called these clusters of emotionally charged memories 'complexes'. Around 1907, Jung also used a galvanometer to record changes in skin conductance when subjects heard stimulus words, measuring emotional reactions this way.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung measured emotional reactions with word association tests at the Burghölzli clinic.
What the evidence says
Later research supports the link between response delay and emotional reaction. The broad explanatory use of 'complexes' has not been tested.
