Flynn Effect

Theory·1984·弗林效应

Each generation scores higher on IQ tests than the one before. This shows test scores are not determined entirely by genes. Nutrition, education, and environment also play a role.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Intelligence Testing
Landmark
Flynn published data showing that American IQ test scores kept rising through the twentieth century.
What the evidence says
Several countries have found test scores rising generation by generation through the 20th century. In recent years, reversals have appeared in places such as Nordic countries, and the causes are still being discussed.
