CBT Delivered by a Conversational Agent

Theory·2017·对话程序提供认知行为治疗

Breaks structured cognitive behavioral exercises, such as identifying thoughts and reappraising them, into everyday chat conversations. This gives people who have little access to therapists some of the help.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Digital Mental Health
Landmark
Fitzpatrick, Darcy, and Vierhile published a randomized controlled trial of Woebot (2017).
What the evidence says
Early trials had small samples and short durations, and the control conditions were weak. Evidence on long-term effects and on serious problems is lacking.
