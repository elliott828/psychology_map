Ego Defense Mechanisms

Theory·1936·自我的防御机制

To reduce anxiety, the ego unconsciously uses methods such as denial, projection, rationalization, and sublimation to cope with conflict.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Ego Psychology
Landmark
Anna Freud, 'The Ego and the Mechanisms of Defence'.
What the evidence says
Empirical evidence supports the existence of defenses and their link to adjustment, including how mature they are. But the lists of defenses differ widely.
