James-Lange Theory of Emotion

Theory·1884·詹姆斯—兰格情绪理论

We do not cry because we are sad. We feel sad because we cry. The feeling of bodily changes is the emotion itself.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Functionalism
Landmark
James published 'What Is an Emotion?'
What the evidence says
There is some support for the idea that bodily feedback shapes emotional experience. But 'bodily reaction first, emotion second' is not the only route.
