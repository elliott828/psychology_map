Nested Environmental Systems

Theory·1979·嵌套的环境系统

The environment is arranged from near to far. Closest is what the child experiences directly, such as home and school. Next are the links between those settings, then indirect influences such as a parent's job, then culture and institutions, all over time.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Ecological Systems Theory
Landmark
Bronfenbrenner distinguished the microsystem, mesosystem, exosystem, and macrosystem.
What the evidence says
Many disciplines use it as an analytic framework. It does not make specific predictions about developmental outcomes.
