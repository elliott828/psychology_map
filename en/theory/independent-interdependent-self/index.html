Independent and Interdependent Self

Theory·1991·独立与互依的自我

Western cultures tend to see the self as an independent individual with stable traits. East Asian cultures tend to see the self as part of a web of relationships.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Cultural Psychology
Landmark
Markus and Kitayama, 'Culture and the Self'.
What the evidence says
There is considerable support for cross-cultural differences in self-construal. But the differences among individuals, and within East Asia, are substantial.
