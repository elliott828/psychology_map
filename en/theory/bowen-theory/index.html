Bowen Family Systems Theory

Theory·1966·鲍恩家庭系统理论

Anxiety is passed down through families. People with low levels of self-differentiation are easily pulled into emotional triangles and pass conflict on to the next generation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Family Systems Therapy
Landmark
Bowen, 'Family Therapy in Clinical Practice'.
What the evidence says
The concept of 'differentiation of self' has some supporting research. The overall theory has not been tested.
