Fundamental Attribution Error

Theory·1977·基本归因错误

When others are late, it is because they are irresponsible. When we are late, it is because of traffic. People tend to explain others by their personality, but explain themselves by the situation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Attribution Theory
Landmark
Ross proposed that people underestimate the role of situations when explaining others' behavior.
What the evidence says
It is robust in Western samples and weaker in East Asia and India. It is also strongly affected by the task and the cues given.
