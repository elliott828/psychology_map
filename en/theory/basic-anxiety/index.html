Basic Anxiety and Neurotic Trends

Theory·1937·基本焦虑与神经症趋势

Children raised in environments that lack security develop 'basic anxiety'. They then adopt one of three strategies: moving toward others, moving against others, or moving away from others.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School

Landmark

What the evidence says
Her criticism of views on women and her emphasis on cultural factors were influential. The specific categories have not been tested.
