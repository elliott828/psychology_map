Dopamine Reward Prediction Error

Theory·1997·多巴胺的奖赏预测误差

Dopamine neurons do not simply fire to a reward itself. They fire to outcomes that are better or worse than expected: an unexpected reward increases firing, an expected one leaves it unchanged, and a disappointment decreases it.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Landmark
Schultz, Dayan, and Montague, 'A Neural Substrate of Prediction and Reward', in Science (1997).
What the evidence says
Several labs have replicated the signal in monkeys, rats, and humans. Dopamine also contributes to motivation and movement, so it cannot be reduced to 'prediction error' alone.
