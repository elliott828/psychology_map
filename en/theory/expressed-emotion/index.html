Expressed Emotion

Theory·1972·表达性情绪

When family members are highly critical, hostile, or overinvolved with a patient, the patient is more likely to relapse. Teaching families to reduce these behaviors helps prevent relapse.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Family Systems Therapy
Landmark
Brown, Birley, and Wing published work showing that family criticism, hostility, and overinvolvement predict relapse in schizophrenia.
What the evidence says
Many studies repeatedly find higher relapse rates in families with high expressed emotion. The direction of causation is still debated, and patients may simply be harder to cope with.
