Cognitive Map

Theory·1948·认知地图

Rats in a maze do not just learn 'turn left, turn right' movements. They form an overall representation of space in the brain, so when a path is blocked, they can take a new route.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Neo-Behaviorism
Landmark
Tolman published 'Cognitive Maps in Rats and Men'.
What the evidence says
The discovery of place cells in the hippocampus and grid cells in the entorhinal cortex has provided direct neural evidence for cognitive maps.
