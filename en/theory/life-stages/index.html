Stages of Life and the Midlife Transition

Theory·1930·人生的阶段与中年转折

The first half of life is spent building a career and adapting to the outer world. After midlife, the task turns inward, toward finding life's meaning and moving toward individuation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung, 'The Stages of Life' (German 'Die Lebenswende'), written around 1930 and included in 'Modern Man in Search of a Soul' (1931).
What the evidence says
It is one of the early sources of adult development research. Later longitudinal studies do not support the idea that a 'midlife crisis' is universal. Most people do not go through dramatic upheaval in midlife.
