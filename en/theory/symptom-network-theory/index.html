Symptom Network Theory of Mental Disorders

Theory·2010·症状网络理论

Argues that disorders such as depression are not sets of symptoms caused by a single underlying 'disease'. They are networks of symptoms, such as insomnia, fatigue, and hopelessness, that trigger and reinforce one another.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Network Psychometrics
Landmark
Cramer, Waldorp, van der Maas, and Borsboom, 'Comorbidity: A Network Perspective' (2010).
What the evidence says
It provides new statistical and conceptual tools. But the stability of the networks and their causal interpretation have been questioned, and the empirical conclusions are still being tested.
