Bystander Effect

Theory·1968·旁观者效应

The more people around, the less likely any one person is to step in to help. Responsibility is spread out, and people watch one another's reactions.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Conformity and Obedience
Landmark
Darley and Latané published 'Bystander Intervention in Emergencies'.
What the evidence says
The effect is robust in laboratory settings. For real, dangerous emergencies, a 2011 meta-analysis found the effect of the number of bystanders was actually smaller.
