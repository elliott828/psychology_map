Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy

Theory·2002·正念认知疗法

Teaches people who have been depressed several times to see thoughts as 'just thoughts' rather than facts when their mood begins to sink, so they avoid slipping back into relapse.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Third-Wave Therapies
Landmark
Segal, Williams, and Teasdale, 'Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression'.
What the evidence says
There is strong evidence for preventing relapse in people with depression who have had three or more episodes.
