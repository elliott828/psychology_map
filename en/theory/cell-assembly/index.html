Cell Assembly

Theory·1949·细胞集合

A group of neurons that are repeatedly activated together forms an 'assembly'. This assembly is the physical form of a concept or memory in the brain.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Hebbian Learning and Plasticity
Landmark
Hebb proposed cell assemblies and phase sequences in 'The Organization of Behavior'.
What the evidence says
Direct experimental evidence now exists for memory traces (engrams). But Hebb's specific idea is only one of the possible mechanisms.
