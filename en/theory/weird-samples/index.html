WEIRD Samples

Theory·2010·WEIRD 样本问题

Most psychology participants come from Western, educated, wealthy, industrialized, democratic societies, yet their results are treated as representative of all humanity.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
WEIRD Samples Critique
Landmark
Henrich, Heine, and Norenzayan, 'The Weirdest People in the World?'
What the evidence says
A systematic review of published cross-cultural research found that samples from these Western, educated, industrialized, rich, and democratic societies are outliers on many tasks.
