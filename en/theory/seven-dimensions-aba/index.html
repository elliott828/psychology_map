Seven Dimensions of ABA

Theory·1968·应用行为分析的七个维度

A behavioral intervention should have seven features: it targets socially important behavior, it is measurable, it demonstrates cause, it is clearly described, it has a theoretical basis, it is effective, and its effects last.
