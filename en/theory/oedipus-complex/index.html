Oedipus Complex

Theory·1910·俄狄浦斯情结

Children around three to four are attracted to the opposite-sex parent and hostile toward the same-sex parent. How these conflicts are resolved determines later personality.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Classical Psychoanalysis
Landmark
Freud used the term in 'On the Psychology of Love'.
What the evidence says
There is no reliable evidence that children universally have sexual desire for the opposite-sex parent and hostility toward the same-sex parent.
