Birth Order and Personality

Theory·1927·出生顺序与人格

The firstborn is more like a deposed king who has lost the throne, and the youngest is easily spoiled. These ideas are widely repeated, but large-sample studies have not supported them.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Individual Psychology
Landmark
Adler's descriptions of the firstborn, second child, and youngest child.
What the evidence says
Large-sample studies, such as a 2015 analysis of more than 20,000 people, found birth order has very little or no effect on personality.
