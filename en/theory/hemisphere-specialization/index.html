Hemispheric Specialization

Theory·1968·大脑半球功能分工

For most people, language is handled mainly by the left hemisphere, while spatial processing and face processing rely more on the right. This is a tendency, not a division of 'thinking styles'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Split-Brain Research
Landmark
Sperry and Gazzaniga described differences between the two hemispheres in handling language and spatial tasks.
What the evidence says
The division of labor, with language leaning left and spatial attention leaning right, holds up. But in healthy people the two hemispheres are always cooperating. There are no 'left-brained' or 'right-brained' people.
