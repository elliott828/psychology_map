Repression

Theory·1915·压抑与“被压抑的记忆”

Painful material is pushed out of consciousness and stored in the unconscious. It may later return under suitable conditions.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Classical Psychoanalysis
Landmark
Freud, 'Repression'.
What the evidence says
Much memory research does not support the idea that trauma is unconsciously repressed and later recovered intact. Deliberate suppression and forgetting do exist.

Key people

  • Sigmund Freud 1856–1939

Relations to other schools

  • ← Critiques Misinformation EffectExperiments show memories can be rewritten by suggestion, undermining the idea that repressed memories can be recovered intact.
