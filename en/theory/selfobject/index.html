Selfobject and Empathy

Theory·1971·自体客体与共情

People need others to mirror their worth and to idealize them. These others are called 'selfobjects'. When they are missing, the self becomes fragile.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Self Psychology
Landmark
Kohut, 'The Analysis of the Self'.
What the evidence says
The evidence that empathy helps the therapeutic alliance is reliable. Selfobject theory itself rests on clinical observation.
