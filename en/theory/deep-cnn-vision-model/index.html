Deep Convolutional Networks as Models of Vision

Theory·2012·深度卷积网络作为视觉的模型

Argues that deep networks trained on large numbers of images can serve as computational models of the visual system. Higher layers correspond to more abstract features, and the architecture resembles aspects of the brain's visual pathways.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Deep Learning as Cognitive Models
Landmark
Krizhevsky, Sutskever, and Hinton's deep convolutional network won the ImageNet recognition competition by a wide margin (2012).
What the evidence says
The internal representations of trained networks show some similarity to activity in monkey visual cortex. But learning methods, data volume, and the brain differ greatly, and whether they can serve as mechanistic models of the brain is still debated.
