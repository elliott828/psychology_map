Moral Foundations Theory

Theory·2004·道德基础理论

Argues that human moral judgment rests on several intuitions, such as care, fairness, loyalty, authority, and purity. People with different political views weight these differently.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Moral Foundations Theory
Landmark
Haidt and Joseph, 'Intuitive Ethics' (2004), proposed several innate moral intuitions.
What the evidence says
Questionnaires can distinguish political positions across several cultures. But the number of 'foundations', and whether they are truly innate, remain under criticism and revision.
