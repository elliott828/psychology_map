Internal Objects and Early Care

Theory·1935·内在客体与早期照护关系

Infants form internal images of 'good' and 'bad' from their interactions with the mother. These internal objects shape how they perceive others later on.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Object Relations
Landmark
Klein, 'A Contribution to the Psychogenesis of Manic-Depressive States'.
What the evidence says
Attachment research indirectly supports the idea that early relationships shape later interpersonal relations and self-image. Klein's specific 'positions' lack empirical testing.
