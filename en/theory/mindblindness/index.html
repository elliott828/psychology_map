Mindblindness Hypothesis

Theory·1985·孤独症的“心盲”假说

Autistic children find it hard to understand other people's thoughts and feelings because they lack, or acquire late, a 'theory of mind'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Theory of Mind
Landmark
Baron-Cohen, Leslie, and Frith, 'Does the Autistic Child Have a "Theory of Mind"?'
What the evidence says
Many autistic children have difficulty on false-belief tasks, but not all, and the theory cannot explain every feature of autism.
