Dialectical Behavior Therapy

Theory·1993·辩证行为疗法

Holds a dialectic between accepting the current situation and changing it. Clients are taught four skill sets: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness.

Historical influence ●●●●●·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Third-Wave Therapies
Landmark
Linehan, 'Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of Borderline Personality Disorder'.
What the evidence says
Several randomized controlled trials support it for borderline personality disorder, self-harm, and suicidal behavior.
