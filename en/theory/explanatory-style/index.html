Explanatory Style (Reformulation)

Theory·1978·解释风格与归因重构

When something bad happens, people who habitually see the cause as 'my fault, lasting, and affecting everything' are more likely to fall into helplessness and depression.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Learned Helplessness
Landmark
Abramson, Seligman, and Teasdale proposed the reformulation of attributions.
What the evidence says
Many studies support a link between a pessimistic explanatory style and depression. The link is moderate in strength, and the causation runs in both directions.
