Structural Model: Id, Ego, Superego

Theory·1923·本我、自我与超我

The mind is divided into the id, which seeks gratification; the ego, which takes reality into account; and the superego, which internalizes norms. Symptoms arise from conflict among the three.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School

Landmark
Freud, 'The Ego and the Id'.
What the evidence says
This is an explanatory framework for describing inner conflict. It makes no falsifiable predictions.
