Necessary and Sufficient Conditions

Theory·1957·治疗的必要充分条件

When the therapist provides empathy, congruence (genuineness), and unconditional positive regard, the client changes in constructive ways.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Person-Centered Therapy
Landmark
Rogers published 'The Necessary and Sufficient Conditions of Therapeutic Personality Change'.
What the evidence says
Empathy, genuineness, and unconditional positive regard are linked to therapy outcomes, with a moderate effect. But they are not 'sufficient' on their own.
