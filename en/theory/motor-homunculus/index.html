Cortical Homunculus

Theory·1937·皮层躯体定位图（小人图）

During surgery, stimulating different points on the cortex makes patients feel or move different parts of the body. The hand and mouth take up a large share of the cortex.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Cerebral Localization
Landmark
Penfield and Boldrey published a map of the human cortex from electrical stimulation.
What the evidence says
The body map laid out across the sensory and motor cortex has been verified many times. Its borders are blurrier than the drawings suggest.
