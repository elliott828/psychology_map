Three-Stage Model of Trauma Recovery

Theory·1992·创伤复原三阶段

Recovery has three steps: establishing safety and stability, remembering and mourning the trauma, and reconnecting with life and other people.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Trauma Studies and PTSD
Landmark
Herman, 'Trauma and Recovery'.
What the evidence says
This is a framework widely used in clinical work. There is debate over the order of 'stabilize first, then process memories'. Some evidence suggests processing can begin directly.
