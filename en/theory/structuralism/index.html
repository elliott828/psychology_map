Structuralism and Introspection

Theory·1898·构造主义与内省分析

Consciousness is broken down into its most basic sensations, images, and feelings. Observers are trained to report their own experiences.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Experimental Psychology
Landmark
Titchener, 'The Postulates of a Structural Psychology'.
What the evidence says
Introspective reports from different labs could not be made consistent, so the method was abandoned. But it pushed precise experiments on sensation forward.
