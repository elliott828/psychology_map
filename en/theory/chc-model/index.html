Cattell-Horn-Carroll Model

Theory·1993·卡特尔—霍恩—卡罗尔模型

Abilities can be arranged in three layers. At the top is general intelligence, g. In the middle are about ten broad abilities. At the bottom are many specific skills.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
General Intelligence and Factor Analysis
Landmark
Carroll, 'Human Cognitive Abilities', reanalyzed hundreds of datasets using factor analysis.
What the evidence says
There is strong consensus on the three-layer structure, and it has been adopted by mainstream intelligence tests.
