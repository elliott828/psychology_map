Universal Grammar

Theory·1957·普遍语法

Humans are born with innate knowledge about the structure of language, which is why children learn grammar quickly from limited input.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Cognitive Revolution
Landmark
Chomsky, 'Syntactic Structures'.
What the evidence says
Some evidence supports the claim that language has an innate basis. But the specific content of universal grammar is hard to test, and it has been debated for a long time against usage-based theories.
