Cheater-Detection Module

Theory·1989·作弊者侦测机制

The same logic puzzle gets far more correct answers when framed as catching someone who breaks a rule. This suggests humans may have evolved a specialized reasoning mechanism for spotting cheaters in reciprocal exchange.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Evolutionary Psychology
Landmark
Cosmides tested social contract reasoning with the Wason selection task.
What the evidence says
People do perform better on the social contract version of the task. But whether this requires a specialized 'module' has been hotly debated in later research.
