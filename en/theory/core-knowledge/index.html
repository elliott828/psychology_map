Core Knowledge

Theory·1990·核心知识

Infants are born with a small number of basic intuitions about objects, number, space, and other people. These serve as the starting point for later learning.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Infant Cognition
Landmark
Spelke, 'Principles of Object Perception', later with Kinzler, organized as 'core knowledge systems'.
What the evidence says
There are many experiments on infants' early responses to objects, number, and space. Whether these amount to 'innate knowledge' is interpreted in different ways.
