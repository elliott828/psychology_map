Intersubjectivity and Mutual Recognition

Theory·1988·主体间性与相互承认

In therapy, both people are subjects. Change happens through mutual recognition and interaction, not through one-sided interpretation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Relational Psychoanalysis
Landmark
Mitchell, 'Relational Concepts in Psychoanalysis'; Benjamin, 'The Bonds of Love'.
What the evidence says
This is mainly clinical and theoretical discussion. It fits the empirical finding that the therapy relationship influences outcomes.
