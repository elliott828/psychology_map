Likes Predict Personality

Theory·2013·点赞可以预测人格

The posts a person likes on social media can predict their gender, political leanings, and Big Five personality scores fairly accurately. This shows that digital footprints carry private information.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Digital Footprints and Personality Prediction
Landmark
Kosinski, Stillwell, and Graepel, 'Private Traits and Attributes Are Predictable from Digital Records of Human Behavior' (2013).
What the evidence says
Prediction reaches moderate accuracy in large samples, but individual-level error is still substantial. The work also raises ethical and privacy concerns.
