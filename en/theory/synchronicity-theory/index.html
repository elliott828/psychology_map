Synchronicity

Theory·1952·共时性

Two events with no causal link occur together because of their meaning. For example, you dream of someone and that same day you receive a letter from them.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung, 'Synchronicity: An Acausal Connecting Principle'.
What the evidence says
There is no replicable evidence. So-called meaningful coincidences are better explained by probability and cognitive bias.
