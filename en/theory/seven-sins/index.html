Seven Sins of Memory

Theory·1999·记忆的七宗罪

Seven kinds of memory error: transience, absent-mindedness, blocking, misattribution, suggestibility, bias, and persistence. Many of them are side effects of normal memory.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Schema and Reconstructive Memory
Landmark
Schacter proposed the seven memory errors in 'The Seven Sins of Memory' in American Psychologist.
What the evidence says
As a classification scheme it is clear and useful. Each 'sin' has solid research behind it, but it is unclear whether they form a unified theory.
