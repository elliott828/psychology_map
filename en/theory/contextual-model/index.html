Contextual Model of Psychotherapy

Theory·2001·心理治疗的情境模型

Therapy works through three routes: a real relationship, expectations of change, and a credible plan of action offered by the therapist. Specific techniques are only one part of this.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

Landmark

What the evidence says
It integrates evidence on relationships, expectations, and specific techniques. But the relative size of each route is still debated.
