Hedonic Adaptation

Theory·1971·享乐适应

Whether someone wins a lottery or is injured, their emotional reaction to the change usually gradually returns to their earlier level.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Positive Psychology
Landmark
Brickman and Campbell proposed the 'hedonic treadmill'.
What the evidence says
People adapt to many positive and negative events. But events such as unemployment and widowhood have lasting effects, and the idea of a fixed 'set point' of happiness has been revised.

Key people
