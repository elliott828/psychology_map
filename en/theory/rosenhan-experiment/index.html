Rosenhan Experiment

Theory·1973·罗森汉的“精神病院里的正常人”

Eight people with no mental illness claimed to hear voices and were admitted to psychiatric hospitals. After admission they behaved normally but were not recognized as sane. It was used to show that diagnosis is unreliable.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

DSM / ICD Diagnostic Systems
Landmark
Rosenhan published 'On Being Sane in Insane Places' in Science.
What the evidence says
A 2019 investigative book pointed to doubtful data and procedures in the study. It is better regarded as a landmark that drove reform than as reliable evidence.
