Dopamine Hypothesis of Schizophrenia

Theory·1963·多巴胺假说

The hallucinations and delusions of schizophrenia are linked to a dysregulated dopamine signal in the brain. All effective antipsychotic drugs block dopamine receptors.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Psychopharmacology
Landmark
Carlsson and Lindqvist found that antipsychotic drugs affect dopamine metabolism.
What the evidence says
There is a lot of evidence linking the dopamine system to psychotic symptoms. But the original 'too much dopamine' claim has been revised into a version specific to brain regions and pathways.
