Neonatal Imitation

Theory·1977·新生儿模仿

Newborns a few days old stick out their tongues when they see an adult do it. This has been taken as evidence that humans are born with a tendency to imitate.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Infant Cognition
Landmark
Meltzoff and Moore: newborns imitate tongue protrusion.
What the evidence says
A large longitudinal study in 2016 failed to replicate the finding. Some researchers still believe small-scale imitation exists.
