Identity Statuses

Theory·1966·同一性状态

Identity can be sorted into four statuses based on whether a person has explored options and whether they have made a commitment: achievement, moratorium, foreclosure, and diffusion.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Psychosocial Development
Landmark
Marcia published interview studies of ego identity statuses.
What the evidence says
Many studies use the four-way classification. But whether the statuses form developmental stages is still debated.
