Engel's Critique of the Biomedical Model

Theory·1977·对生物医学模型的批评

It is not enough for doctors to look only at biological markers of disease. They must also consider the patient's psychological state, family, and social circumstances.

What the evidence says
It criticizes the biomedical model as overly reductionist and calls for integration. But it does not offer an operational method for how to integrate.
