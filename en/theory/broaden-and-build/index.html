Broaden-and-Build Theory

Theory·1998·积极情绪的拓展—建构理论

Positive emotions widen a person's attention and thinking, helping them build long-term resources such as skills, friendships, and resilience.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Positive Psychology
Landmark
Fredrickson published work on the role of positive emotion.
What the evidence says
Experiments showing positive emotions widen attention provide support. Her '3:1 positivity ratio' was overturned by later mathematical criticism and withdrawn.
