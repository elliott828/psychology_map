Lexical Hypothesis

Theory·1936·词汇学假设

In human language, every important individual difference gradually acquires a word to describe it. So the basic dimensions of personality can be found by analyzing the dictionary.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Trait Theory
Landmark
Allport and Odbert extracted 17,953 personality-describing words from the dictionary.
What the evidence says
The hypothesis is broadly supported across several languages. But some personality traits have no matching word in some languages.
