Psilocybin and Mystical-Type Experience

Theory·2006·赛洛西宾与神秘体验

Taking psilocybin in a controlled setting with support can produce intense 'mystical-type' experiences in some people. These people report improvements in meaning and mood months later.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy
Landmark
Griffiths and colleagues, 'Psilocybin Can Occasion Mystical-Type Experiences Having Substantial and Sustained Personal and Spiritual Significance' (2006).
What the evidence says
Small controlled trials and follow-up studies suggest benefits. But most trials have small samples and cannot be fully blinded, and long-term safety and effects are still being examined.
