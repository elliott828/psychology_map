Dual Systems Model of Adolescent Brain Development

Theory·2008·青春期的双系统模型

The drive for novelty and reward strengthens early in adolescence, while the systems responsible for planning and self-control mature later. In this period of 'gas before brakes', risk-taking becomes more likely.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
ABCD Study
Landmark
Steinberg, Casey, and colleagues proposed that the reward system matures before the control system, and that this mismatch increases risk-taking (2008).
What the evidence says
Behavioral data support increased risk-taking in adolescence. But brain evidence for the mismatch in maturation is inconsistent, and several revisions and critiques have appeared in recent years.
