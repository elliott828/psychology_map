Holding Environment and the Good-Enough Mother

Theory·1960·抱持环境与“足够好的母亲”

Infants need a stable environment. The mother does not have to be perfect, only 'good enough' in responding. Without this holding, a 'false self' tends to form.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Object Relations
Landmark
Winnicott, 'Theory of the Parent-Infant Relationship'.
What the evidence says
The idea that 'responsive but not perfect' care is what matters fits attachment research. The specific terms are mainly used clinically.
