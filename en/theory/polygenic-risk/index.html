Polygenic Architecture of Psychiatric Disorders

Theory·2009·精神疾病的多基因架构

Schizophrenia, depression, and similar disorders are not determined by a single 'causal gene'. They arise from many gene variants with tiny effects, which add up, together with environmental factors, to create risk.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Psychiatric Genomics
Landmark
The International Schizophrenia Consortium found many common variants with small effects that together raise risk (2009). In 2014 the number of loci was expanded to 108.
What the evidence says
Large genomic studies repeatedly support the picture of 'thousands of loci with very small effects'. The predictive power of any single gene is small, and individual prediction remains limited.
