Working Alliance

Theory·1979·工作联盟

Whether therapist and client agree on goals and methods, and whether they trust each other, is one of the most stable predictors of therapy outcome.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Common Factors
Landmark
Bordin proposed that the alliance consists of goals, tasks, and emotional bond.
What the evidence says
The link between the alliance and outcome is stable and moderate (correlation about 0.28). It holds across types of therapy.
