Contagion and Suggestibility in Crowds

Theory·1895·群体感染与暗示

In a crowd, individual responsibility is diluted, emotions spread quickly by contagion, and people are more easily swept along by simple slogans.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Crowd Psychology
Landmark
Le Bon, 'The Crowd'.
What the evidence says
This account influenced politics and advertising. But later research shows crowd behavior often follows clear norms and is not simply a loss of reason.
