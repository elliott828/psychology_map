Kohlberg's Six Stages

Theory·1969·道德判断六阶段

Moral reasoning develops step by step from low to high: from 'avoid punishment' and 'obey the rules', to 'uphold the social contract' and 'follow universal ethical principles'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Moral Development
Landmark
Kohlberg proposed three levels and six stages, measured with the 'Heinz dilemma'.
What the evidence says
The order of the stages has some support across cultures. The higher stages are rare in many societies, and the link between reasoning and behavior is weak.
