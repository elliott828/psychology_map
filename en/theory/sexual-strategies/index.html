Sexual Strategies Theory

Theory·1993·性策略与择偶偏好

Men and women show statistical differences in their preferences for short-term and long-term mates. These differences are explained as the result of unequal parental investment.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Evolutionary Psychology
Landmark
Buss and Schmitt proposed short-term and long-term mating strategies.
What the evidence says
Cross-cultural surveys support the direction of the sex differences, but the effect size is moderate and varies with culture and the level of gender equality. Many of the explanations are post hoc.
