Ecological Rationality

Theory·1999·生态理性

Heuristics are not flaws. When a rule matches the structure of the environment, a simple shortcut is often more accurate than a complex calculation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Landmark
Gigerenzer and colleagues, 'Simple Heuristics That Make Us Smart'.
What the evidence says
Under uncertainty, there is empirical support for the claim that simple rules often do as well as complex models. Its range of application is still discussed.
