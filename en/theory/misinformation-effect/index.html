Misinformation Effect

Theory·1974·错误信息效应

Suggestive information received after an event quietly changes a person's memory of the original event, and people can feel very sure of the altered memory.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Schema and Reconstructive Memory
Landmark
Loftus and Palmer: asking about a car crash with different verbs produced different speed estimates.
What the evidence says
Replicated in hundreds of experiments, and especially important for legal testimony. How closely implanted memories resemble real ones is still debated.

Key people

  • Elizabeth Loftus 1944–
