Grit

Theory·2007·坚毅

Perseverance and passion for long-term goals are thought to predict achievement better than talent does.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Positive Psychology
Landmark
Duckworth and colleagues, 'Grit: Perseverance and Passion for Long-Term Goals'.
What the evidence says
Its predictive power comes mainly from the perseverance part. It overlaps heavily with conscientiousness, and interventions have produced smaller effects.
