Neuron Theory

Theory·1888·神经元是分立的细胞

The nervous system is not one continuous network. It is made of countless separate nerve cells that pass signals through contact with one another. This doctrine replaced the 'reticular theory' and laid the groundwork for today's talk of neural circuits.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Neuron Doctrine
Landmark
Cajal showed with Golgi staining that nerve cells are separate (1888). Waldeyer named the 'neuron' in 1891.
What the evidence says
After electron microscopy, the synaptic gaps between neurons could be seen directly. Electrical synapses between some cells slightly modify the claim that they are completely separate.
