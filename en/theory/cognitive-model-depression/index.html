Cognitive Model of Depression

Theory·1967·抑郁的认知模型

Depressed people hold a negative 'cognitive triad' about themselves, the world, and the future, and treat these views as facts. Therapy changes mood by testing these thoughts.

Historical influence ●●●●●·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Cognitive Therapies
Landmark
Beck, 'Depression: Clinical, Experimental, and Theoretical Aspects'.
What the evidence says
Negative cognition is linked to depression, and cognitive therapy has strong support for treating depression. Whether cognition comes before mood, causally, is still debated.
