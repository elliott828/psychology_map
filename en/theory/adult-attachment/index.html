Adult Attachment

Theory·1987·成人依恋

Adults also have attachment patterns in close relationships. Some people readily worry about being abandoned (anxiety), while others are reluctant to depend on others (avoidance).

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Attachment Theory
Landmark
Hazan and Shaver, 'Romantic Love Conceptualized as an Attachment Process'.
What the evidence says
The two dimensions of adult attachment, anxiety and avoidance, have strong research support. Their continuity with infant attachment is only moderate.
