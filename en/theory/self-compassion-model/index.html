Three-Component Model of Self-Compassion

Theory·2003·自我关怀的三成分模型

Self-compassion has three parts: being kind to yourself rather than harsh when you fail, recognizing that suffering is a shared human experience, and being aware of pain without being overwhelmed by it.

What the evidence says
The Self-Compassion Scale is consistently correlated with lower depression and anxiety, and intervention studies show some effects. The factor structure and the division into components are still debated.
