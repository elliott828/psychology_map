Concept of Schizophrenia

Theory·1911·精神分裂症概念

The core of the illness is not necessarily decline into dementia. It is a 'splitting' between thought, emotion, and reality, and its course varies.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Modern Psychiatric Classification
Landmark
Bleuler, 'Dementia Praecox or the Group of Schizophrenias'.
What the evidence says
The core diagnostic features (splitting of thought, emotion, and will) have been revised by later research. Whether it is a single disease is still debated.
