Aplysia Sensitization and Synaptic Memory

Theory·1982·海兔的敏感化与突触记忆

After sea slugs (Aplysia) are repeatedly given electric shocks, their sensory neurons release more neurotransmitter. Long-term memory also requires the synthesis of new proteins to consolidate synaptic changes.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Molecular Basis of Memory
Landmark
Kandel and Schwartz summarized how cAMP signaling changes neurotransmitter release at synapses.
What the evidence says
The core mechanisms (cAMP, CREB, and protein synthesis) have been confirmed in several species. The match to complex human memory is limited.
