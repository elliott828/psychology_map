Dissociative Identity Disorder Debate

Theory·1989·解离性身份障碍的成因之争

Whether a person really splits into several 'personalities', or whether this is a performance shaped by suggestion and role expectations, is still debated.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Hypnosis, Hysteria and Dissociation
Landmark
Putnam, 'Diagnosis and Treatment of Multiple Personality Disorder'.
What the evidence says
Trauma models and sociocognitive models each have evidence. The diagnosis was widely overdiagnosed in the 1980s and 1990s.
