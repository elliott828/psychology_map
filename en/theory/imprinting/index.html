Imprinting and Sensitive Periods

Theory·1935·印刻与敏感期

A newly hatched gosling takes the first moving object it sees as its 'mother' and follows it for a long time. This is seen as an example of early experience shaping behavior in a way that cannot be undone.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Ethology
Landmark
Lorenz described goslings following the first moving object they saw after hatching.
What the evidence says
Imprinting in birds is well established. The 'critical period' was later renamed the more flexible 'sensitive period'.
