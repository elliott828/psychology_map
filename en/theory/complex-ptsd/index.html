Complex PTSD

Theory·1992·复杂性创伤后应激障碍

The effects of long-term, repeated trauma, such as childhood abuse or prolonged domestic violence, go beyond flashbacks and avoidance. They include difficulties with emotion regulation, a negative sense of self, and relationship problems.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

ICD-11 and Complex PTSD
Landmark
Herman, 'Complex Posttraumatic Stress Disorder' (1992). The ICD-11 formally included this diagnosis in 2019.
What the evidence says
The ICD-11 criteria have been validated in research across several countries and distinguished from ordinary PTSD. Its relationship to borderline personality disorder and other conditions is still discussed.
