Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology

Theory·2017·HiTOP 分层精神病理学

Based on how symptoms co-occur, arranged in layers from bottom to top: specific symptoms, syndromes, subfactors, spectra, and at the top a general 'p factor'.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Dimensional Models (RDoC / HiTOP)
Landmark
Kotov and colleagues, 'The Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology (HiTOP)'.
What the evidence says
It is based on the structure of symptom covariation, and there is a substantial amount of data. Its clinical application is still being explored.
