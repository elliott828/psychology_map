Theory-of-Mind Tests for Language Models

Theory·2023·大语言模型的心智理论测试

Gives large language models human theory-of-mind tasks to see whether they understand that others can have thoughts different from their own. The results were surprising, but there is no consensus on how to interpret them.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Large Language Models and Cognition
Landmark
Kosinski tested large language models with false-belief tasks (2023 preprint; published in PNAS in 2024).
What the evidence says
Changing the wording or making small modifications noticeably lowers the models' performance. Researchers disagree on whether this means they understand other people's minds.
