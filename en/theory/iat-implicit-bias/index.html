IAT and Implicit Bias

Theory·1998·内隐联想测验与内隐偏见

The Implicit Association Test measures people's automatic associations between social groups and good or bad words, using differences in response speed. These results are used to estimate 'implicit bias'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Prejudice Research
Landmark
Greenwald, McGhee, and Schwartz published the Implicit Association Test.
What the evidence says
The test's test-retest reliability is low, and its prediction of individual behavior is weak. It is more suitable for group-level research.
