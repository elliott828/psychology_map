Conditioned Reflex: Acquisition, Extinction, Generalization

Theory·1927·条件反射的形成、消退与泛化

After a conditioned stimulus is paired with an unconditioned stimulus, the conditioned stimulus alone can trigger the response. If the pairing stops for a long time, the response fades, but it may spontaneously recover after a while.

Historical influence ●●●●●·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Landmark
The English translation of Pavlov's 'Conditioned Reflexes' was published, summarizing more than twenty years of experiments.
What the evidence says
Acquisition, extinction, spontaneous recovery, generalization, and discrimination have been repeatedly observed in every species tested.
