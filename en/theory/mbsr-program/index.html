MBSR Eight-Week Program

Theory·1979·MBSR 八周课程

An eight-week course of body scan, sitting meditation, yoga, and daily practice. Participants learn to notice the present moment with a non-judgmental attitude.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction
Landmark
Kabat-Zinn opened a stress reduction clinic at the University of Massachusetts Medical Center. The first paper was published in 1982.
What the evidence says
The evidence for stress and chronic pain shows moderate effects. Many studies lack an active control group.
