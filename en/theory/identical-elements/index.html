Identical Elements Theory of Transfer

Theory·1901·相同要素迁移论

How much learning one thing helps with another depends on how many components the two share. So studying Latin does not make people better at thinking in general.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Law of Effect
Landmark
Thorndike and Woodworth published 'The Influence of Improvement in One Mental Function upon the Efficiency of Other Functions'.
What the evidence says
The basic finding about transfer, that skills transfer less than people expect, holds up. But the 'identical elements' explanation is too narrow.
