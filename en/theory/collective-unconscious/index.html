Collective Unconscious and Archetypes

Theory·1919·集体无意识与原型

Humans share an innate psychological structure, which appears as recurring images such as the mother, the hero, the wise old man, and the shadow.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung introduced the word 'archetype' (1919) and developed the collective unconscious systematically in the 1930s.
What the evidence says
Myths and dreams across cultures share similar motifs. But whether this needs 'inherited archetypes' to explain it has not been tested empirically, and cultural transmission offers other explanations.
