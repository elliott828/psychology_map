Mirror Stage

Theory·1949·镜像阶段

An infant recognizes themselves in a mirror and gains a complete image. But this image is external, so the self is alienated from the start.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Lacanian Psychoanalysis
Landmark
Lacan, 'The Mirror Stage' (first reported in 1936).
What the evidence says
This is a philosophical theory. It has only a surface connection to experimental studies of mirror self-recognition, which appears around 18 months in children.
