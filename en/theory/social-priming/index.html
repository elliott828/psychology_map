Social Priming

Theory·1996·社会启动（“老年人”启动）

Being unconsciously exposed to words related to 'the elderly' makes people walk more slowly. The finding was highly influential, but it has largely failed to replicate.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Social Cognition
Landmark
Bargh, Chen, and Burrows: exposure to words about old age led people to walk more slowly.
What the evidence says
Several independent replications failed to reproduce the original result. It has become a leading example of the replicability crisis.

  • John Bargh 1955–
