Interpersonal Theory of Psychiatry

Theory·1953·人际精神病学

Personality is not something inside a person alone. It is a pattern of recurring interpersonal situations. Anxiety comes from the experience of not being approved of by significant others.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Neo-Freudian School
Landmark
Sullivan, 'The Interpersonal Theory of Psychiatry'.
What the evidence says
The view that personality forms through relationships was later taken up by several schools. The specific theory has not been tested.
