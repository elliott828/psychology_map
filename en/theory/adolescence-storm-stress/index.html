Adolescent Storm and Stress

Theory·1904·青春期的“疾风怒涛”

Adolescence is a stage of intense emotion, conflict, and more risk-taking. It is a kind of 'rebirth'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Child Study Movement
Landmark
Hall, 'Adolescence'.
What the evidence says
Emotional swings and risk-taking do rise during adolescence. But most teenagers do not go through a 'storm'. Individual differences matter more.
