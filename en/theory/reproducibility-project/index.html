Reproducibility Project: Psychology

Theory·2015·心理学可重复性计划

A project tried to repeat 100 studies published in three leading journals. Fewer than half succeeded, which prompted a rethinking of practices across the field.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Replication Crisis and Open Science
Landmark
The Open Science Collaboration reported the replication results of 100 experiments in Science.
What the evidence says
About a third to four in ten replicated, and effect sizes shrank by half on average. Replication rates differ widely between subfields.
