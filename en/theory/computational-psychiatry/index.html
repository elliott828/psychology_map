Computational Psychiatry

Theory·2012·计算精神病学

Symptoms such as hallucinations, delusions, and addiction are understood as biased learning and inference in the brain. Mathematical models describe these biases.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Predictive Processing
Landmark
Montague, Dolan, Friston, and Dayan published 'Computational Psychiatry'.
What the evidence says
Research describing symptoms with learning models is just beginning. It has produced several findings, but there are no clinical tools ready for use yet.
