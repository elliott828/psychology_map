Insight in Problem Solving

Theory·1917·顿悟式问题解决

Solving a problem is often not a matter of step-by-step trial and error. It can come suddenly as an 'aha' after the problem is reinterpreted.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Gestalt Psychology
Landmark
Köhler, 'The Mentality of Apes', described a chimpanzee suddenly thinking to stack boxes to reach a banana.
What the evidence says
Experiments support the insight phenomenon in humans. Whether it is fundamentally different from gradual problem solving is still disputed.
