Active Imagination

Theory·1916·积极想象

While awake, the person lets inner images arise on their own, then engages with them through painting, writing, or dialogue. This brings unconscious content into awareness.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung, 'The Transcendent Function' (written in 1916 but published decades later), the earliest account of this method.
What the evidence says
It is a clinical method, based mainly on case studies and experiential reports. Controlled experiments are lacking.

Key people

  • Carl Gustav Jung 1875–1961
