How People Learn Framework

Theory·1999·《人是如何学习的》框架

Learners enter the classroom with existing ideas. To understand, they need to build conceptual frameworks on top of facts and learn to monitor their own learning.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Cognitive Psychology
Landmark
Bransford, Brown, and Cocking, eds., 'How People Learn'.
What the evidence says
It summarizes research on expert-novice differences, prior knowledge, and metacognition. These conclusions are broadly solid, but their application in classrooms has been uneven.
