Observational Learning

Theory·1961·观察学习

Seeing someone else do something and get a good result makes you more likely to do it yourself. Seeing a bad result makes you avoid it. No direct trial is needed.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Social Learning Theory
Landmark
Bandura and Ross and Ross published the Bobo doll experiments.
What the evidence says
Children imitating role models has been demonstrated many times. Its long-term effect on violent behavior is unclear.
