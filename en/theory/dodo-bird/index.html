Dodo Bird Verdict

Theory·1975·渡渡鸟裁决

In 'Alice's Adventures in Wonderland', the Dodo says 'everyone has won and all must have prizes'. The average effects of different psychotherapies are about the same.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Common Factors
Landmark
Luborsky and colleagues published a review comparing psychotherapy outcomes, drawing on Rosenzweig's 1936 metaphor.
What the evidence says
There is considerable support for the view that the overall effects of major therapies are similar. But for specific problems, such as phobias and obsessive-compulsive disorder, differences do exist.
