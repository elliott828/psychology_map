Zone of Proximal Development

Theory·1934·最近发展区

Between what a child can do alone and what they can do with help, there is a zone that is best suited for learning. Teaching should target this zone.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Sociocultural Theory
Landmark
Vygotsky, 'Thought and Language', proposed the gap between what children do independently and what they do with help.
What the evidence says
It is widely used in teaching research, but it is very hard to measure precisely.
