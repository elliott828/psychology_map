Flow Model

Theory·1990·心流模型

To enter flow you need clear goals, immediate feedback, and a challenge matched to your skill. Too easy and you become bored; too hard and you become anxious.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

Landmark

What the evidence says
The challenge-skill balance predictions have partial support. But the neural and measurement basis of flow is still not unified.
