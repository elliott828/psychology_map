Social Identity Theory (Core)

Theory·1979·社会认同理论的核心

Once a person places themselves in a group, they protect their self-esteem by favoring their own group. This produces preference for the in-group and prejudice against outsiders.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Social Identity Theory
Landmark
Tajfel and Turner.
What the evidence says
People tend to favor their own group. This appears under almost any grouping condition and has been replicated extensively.
