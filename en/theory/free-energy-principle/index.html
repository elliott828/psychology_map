Free-Energy Principle

Theory·2010·自由能原理

All living systems try to reduce their 'surprise' about the states they are in. Perception and action are both ways of reducing surprise.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Predictive Processing
Landmark
Friston, 'The Free-Energy Principle: A Unified Brain Theory?'
What the evidence says
It is mathematically self-consistent, but it can explain almost any result. Many researchers argue it has not yet made falsifiable predictions.
