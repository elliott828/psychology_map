Basic Psychological Needs

Theory·2000·三种基本心理需要

When the three needs of autonomy, competence, and relatedness are met, people are more engaged and happier. When they are controlled, motivation actually declines.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Self-Determination Theory
Landmark
Ryan and Deci, 'Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being'.
What the evidence says
Large cross-cultural and experimental studies show that need satisfaction is linked to well-being and engagement. Whether every one of these needs is truly 'basic' is still debated.
