Researcher Degrees of Freedom and p-hacking

Theory·2011·研究者的自由度与 p 值操纵

If you try several analysis methods on the same data and look at many outcomes, you can nearly always find a 'significant' result. So published significant results may be false.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Replication Crisis and Open Science
Landmark
Simmons, Nelson, and Simonsohn, 'False-Positive Psychology'.
What the evidence says
Simulation and meta-science research both show that flexible analysis greatly raises the false-positive rate. Preregistration can effectively limit it.
