Reinforcement and Shaping

Theory·1953·强化与塑造

A complex behavior is broken into small steps, and each step closer to the goal is reinforced. This gradually shapes a new behavior, such as training pigeons to play table tennis.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Operant Conditioning
Landmark
Skinner, 'Science and Human Behavior'.
What the evidence says
Reinforcement and gradual shaping have been shown to work in animal training, special education, and behavior modification.
