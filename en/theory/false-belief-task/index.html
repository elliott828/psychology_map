False-Belief Task

Theory·1983·错误信念任务

A child sees a toy moved, then is asked where the person who left will look for it. Most children under four say 'the new place', showing they do not yet understand that others can hold mistaken ideas.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Theory of Mind
Landmark
Wimmer and Perner's Maxi task.
What the evidence says
The developmental shift between ages three and four has been confirmed in many countries. Whether earlier results on implicit tests amount to 'understanding' is disputed.
