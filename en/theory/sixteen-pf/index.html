Cattell 16PF

Theory·1949·卡特尔 16 种人格因素

Factor analysis was used to extract 16 'source traits', and a questionnaire was built to measure them.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Factor-Analytic Personality Models
Landmark
Cattell published the 16PF questionnaire.
What the evidence says
Later studies could not consistently reproduce 16 factors. The five higher-order factors are closer to the Big Five.
