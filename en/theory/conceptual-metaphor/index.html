Conceptual Metaphor Theory

Theory·1980·概念隐喻理论

We understand abstract concepts through concrete bodily experience. Phrases like 'argument is war' and 'time is money' reflect how we think.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Embodied Cognition
Landmark
Lakoff and Johnson, 'Metaphors We Live By'.
What the evidence says
Metaphors in language are systematic. But the stronger claim that metaphors actually shape thought is poorly supported.
