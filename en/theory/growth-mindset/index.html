Growth Mindset

Theory·2006·成长型思维

People who believe ability can be changed through effort are more willing to take on challenges. People who believe ability is fixed tend to avoid failure.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Positive Psychology
Landmark
Dweck, 'Mindset'.
What the evidence says
The belief is only weakly linked to academic performance. Large-scale trials of academic interventions show a very small average effect, though it helps some students.
