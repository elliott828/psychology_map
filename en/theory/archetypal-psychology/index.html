Archetypal Psychology

Theory·1975·原型心理学

The psyche is not run by the ego. It is a theater of many images. Symptoms are the soul's way of speaking, and therapy should listen to these images rather than try to 'fix' them.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Post-Jungian and Archetypal Psychology
Landmark
Hillman, 'Re-Visioning Psychology'.
What the evidence says
This is an interpretive tradition centered on images, myth, and soul. It is not tested empirically, and it deliberately keeps a distance from empirical psychology.

Key people
