Developmental School of Analytical Psychology

Theory·1944·发展学派（福德姆与伦敦学派）

Fordham argued that the 'self' exists from infancy and unfolds gradually through the relationship with the mother. Neumann traced the development of consciousness through myth.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Post-Jungian and Archetypal Psychology
Landmark
Fordham, 'Children as Individuals'.
What the evidence says
By bringing infant observation and object relations into Jungian thinking, this school can engage with attachment research. But it still relies mainly on clinical observation.
