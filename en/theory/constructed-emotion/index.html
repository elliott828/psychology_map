Theory of Constructed Emotion

Theory·2017·建构情绪理论

Emotions are not a ready-made 'anger circuit' in the brain that gets triggered. Instead, the brain uses body signals and past experience to sort a mass of feeling into 'anger' or 'anxiety'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Affective Neuroscience
Landmark
Barrett, 'How Emotions Are Made'.
What the evidence says
It offers a set of predictions that oppose the basic emotions view. Some evidence supports it, but the overall framework has not yet been fully tested.
