Reciprocal Determinism

Theory·1978·三元交互决定论

A person's thoughts, behavior, and environment shape one another. None of the three one-way determines the others.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Social Learning Theory
Landmark
Bandura published 'Reciprocal Determinism' and laid it out systematically in 1986.
What the evidence says
This is a framework describing how person, behavior, and environment influence each other. It is more an explanatory stance than a falsifiable hypothesis.
