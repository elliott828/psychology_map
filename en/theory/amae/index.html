Amae

Theory·1971·“甘え”（依赖与撒娇）

The wish to be indulged by others and to depend on their goodwill. It is common in Japanese families and workplaces. Doi argued it is the key to understanding the Japanese mind.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Indigenous Psychology
Landmark
Doi, 'The Anatomy of Dependence' (Amae no kozo).
What the evidence says
This is a cultural interpretive concept, used to explain Japanese interpersonal relations. It lacks systematic empirical research, and it is closer to psychoanalysis.
