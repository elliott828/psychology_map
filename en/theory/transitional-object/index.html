Transitional Object

Theory·1953·过渡性客体

The blanket or toy an infant clings to is a bridge from 'being one with the mother' to 'the outside world'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Object Relations
Landmark
Winnicott, 'Transitional Objects and Transitional Phenomena'.
What the evidence says
The widespread attachment of children to comfort objects is well documented. But the explanation of its developmental meaning has not been rigorously tested.
