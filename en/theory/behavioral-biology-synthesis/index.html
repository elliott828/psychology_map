Multi-level Biology of Behavior

Theory·2017·行为的多层次生物学综合

The same behavior can be explained layer by layer across time scales: the nerve firing of the previous second, hormones of a few hours earlier, childhood experience, prenatal development, and culture and genetic evolution.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Ethology
Landmark
Sapolsky's 'Behave' traces behavior back from the previous second to millions of years ago.
What the evidence says
This is a review-style integrative framework. The evidence for each layer varies in strength, and the direction of causation is still debated.
