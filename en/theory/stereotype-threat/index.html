Stereotype Threat

Theory·1995·刻板印象威胁

When people worry that their performance will confirm negative views about their group, they actually perform worse.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Prejudice Research
Landmark
Steele and Aronson, 'Stereotype Threat and the Intellectual Test Performance of African Americans'.
What the evidence says
The original effect shrank noticeably in later large-sample replications, and some studies found no effect at all. Publication bias appears to have played a significant role.

Key people

  • Claude Steele 1946–
  • Elliot Aronson 1932–
