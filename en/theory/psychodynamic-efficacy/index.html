Efficacy of Psychodynamic Psychotherapy

Theory·2010·心理动力治疗的疗效证据

A meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials shows that psychodynamic therapy is roughly as effective as established therapies such as cognitive behavioral therapy, and its effects continue after treatment ends.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Evidence for Psychodynamic Therapy
Landmark
Shedler, 'The Efficacy of Psychodynamic Psychotherapy', in American Psychologist (2010).
What the evidence says
Several meta-analyses show psychodynamic therapy has moderate to large effects on common disorders, close to other therapies. The studies vary widely in quality and in control conditions.
