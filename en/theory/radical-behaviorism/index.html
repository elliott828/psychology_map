Radical Behaviorism

Theory·1945·彻底行为主义

Inner thoughts and feelings also count as behavior, only they are not visible. To explain behavior, you only need to find the antecedents and consequences in the environment, without assuming an inner 'entity' in the mind.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Behaviorism
Landmark
Skinner, 'Operational Analysis of Psychological Terms'.
What the evidence says
It is a philosophical position on how psychology should explain behavior. Its methodological claims were heavily revised by cognitive science.
