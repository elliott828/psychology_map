Diathesis-Stress Model

Theory·1977·素质—压力模型

Mental disorders are triggered by an interaction between an inherited or early-formed vulnerability and later stressful events.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Biopsychosocial Model
Landmark
Zubin and Spring published 'Vulnerability: A New View of Schizophrenia'.
What the evidence says
The interaction of 'vulnerability plus stressful events' has been repeatedly confirmed across many disorders. But the specific vulnerability factors are often unknown.
