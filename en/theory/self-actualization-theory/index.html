Self-Actualization Theory

Theory·1954·自我实现理论

A small number of people fully develop their potential. They accept reality, are creative, care for others, and often have 'peak experiences'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Hierarchy of Needs and Self-Actualization
Landmark
Maslow, 'Motivation and Personality'.
What the evidence says
The traits of 'self-actualizers' came from subjective selection of a few famous people. They have not been rigorously tested empirically.
