Personality Continuity and Maturity

Theory·2000·人格的连续性与成熟

A person's position in the population on personality traits is quite stable from middle age onward. At the same time, most people become more conscientious, more settled, and less anxious as they age.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Big Five Personality
Landmark
Roberts and DelVecchio reviewed the stability of personality traits over time.
What the evidence says
Several longitudinal studies find that the ranking of people on personality is quite stable. Average levels, however, shift with age toward greater conscientiousness and emotional stability.
