REBT ABC Model

Theory·1955·理性情绪行为疗法的 ABC 模型

Emotions are caused not by the event (A) but by your beliefs about the event (B), and beliefs have consequences (C). Emotions are changed by challenging irrational beliefs.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Cognitive Therapies
Landmark
Ellis proposed rational therapy.
What the evidence says
Some controlled studies support it, and its effects are comparable to cognitive therapy. The model's list of 'irrational beliefs' has not been consistently tested.
