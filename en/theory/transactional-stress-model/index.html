Transactional Model of Stress and Coping

Theory·1984·压力的认知评价与应对

Stress depends not only on the event itself, but on how you appraise it (as a threat or a challenge) and how many resources you believe you have.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Stress Physiology
Landmark
Lazarus and Folkman, 'Stress, Appraisal, and Coping'.
What the evidence says
Many studies support the claim that appraisal shapes the stress response. The model is largely descriptive, and its specific predictions are limited.
