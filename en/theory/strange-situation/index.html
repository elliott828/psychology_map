Strange Situation

Theory·1970·陌生情境

One-year-olds are separated from their mothers and reunited in a laboratory. Their reactions are observed, and attachment is classified as secure, avoidant, or ambivalent. Disorganized attachment was added later.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Attachment Theory
Landmark
Ainsworth and Bell introduced the Strange Situation procedure, distinguishing secure, avoidant, and ambivalent attachment.
What the evidence says
The categories have been replicated in several countries. But the cultural applicability and stability of the classification are limited.
