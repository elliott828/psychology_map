Chomsky's Review of Verbal Behavior

Theory·1959·乔姆斯基对《言语行为》的评论

Language creativity, the ability to produce sentences never heard before, cannot be explained by stimulus and reinforcement. It requires an internal system of rules.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Cognitive Revolution
Landmark
Chomsky published a long review of Skinner's 'Verbal Behavior'.
What the evidence says
It exposed the shortcomings of a stimulus-response account of language. The field often treats it as a turning point. But some researchers later argued that it misreads Skinner.
