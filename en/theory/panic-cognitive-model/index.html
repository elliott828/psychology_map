Cognitive Model of Panic

Theory·1986·惊恐的认知模型

A panic attack happens when bodily sensations, such as a racing heart, are misread as 'I am dying'. This triggers stronger anxiety and creates a vicious cycle.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy
Landmark
Clark, 'A Cognitive Model of Panic'.
What the evidence says
The model, which explains bodily sensations as catastrophic signs of danger, is supported by many experiments and outcome studies.
