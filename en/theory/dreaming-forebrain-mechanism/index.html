Dreaming and the Forebrain Mechanism

Theory·2000·做梦由前脑机制控制

Studies of patients with brain damage show that REM sleep and dreaming do not always coincide. Damage to certain forebrain regions linked to motivation and reward can stop dreaming.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Neuropsychoanalysis
Landmark
Solms published a paper in Behavioral and Brain Sciences arguing that dreaming and REM sleep are controlled by different brain mechanisms (2000).
What the evidence says
Lesion studies support the view that dreams and REM sleep do not always occur together. But his motivational account of dreams has not been widely accepted.
