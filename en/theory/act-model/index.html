Acceptance and Commitment Therapy

Theory·1999·接纳承诺疗法的心理灵活性

Six processes: acceptance, cognitive defusion, being present, self as context, values, and committed action. The goal is to keep doing valued things even when pain is present.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Third-Wave Therapies
Landmark
Hayes, Strosahl, and Wilson, 'Acceptance and Commitment Therapy'.
What the evidence says
Hundreds of trials support it. The methodological quality of some studies varies, and the claim of a unique mechanism is disputed.
