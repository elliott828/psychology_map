Hierarchy of Defenses

Theory·1977·防御机制的层次

Defenses can be sorted into pathological (such as projection), immature, neurotic, and mature (such as humor and sublimation). The more mature the defense, the better it supports adjustment.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Ego Psychology
Landmark
Vaillant, 'Adaptation to Life'.
What the evidence says
Long-term follow-up studies find that mature defenses are linked to better social adjustment and health.
