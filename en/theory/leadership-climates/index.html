Leadership Climates Experiment

Theory·1939·三种领导氛围

The same group of boys, under authoritarian leadership, showed more dependence and aggression. Under democratic leadership they were more cooperative. Under laissez-faire leadership, they were less productive.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Field Theory and Group Dynamics
Landmark
Lewin, Lippitt, and White compared boys' groups under authoritarian, democratic, and laissez-faire leadership.
What the evidence says
The study was very small and the controlled conditions were limited. But the conclusion that democratic leadership produces better cooperation has broadly been supported by later research.
