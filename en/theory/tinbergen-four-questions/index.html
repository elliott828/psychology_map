Tinbergen's Four Questions

Theory·1963·廷伯根四问

Explaining a behavior means answering four different questions: what mechanism produces it, how it develops, what adaptive function it serves, and what its evolutionary history is.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Ethology
Landmark
Tinbergen, 'On Aims and Methods of Ethology'.
What the evidence says
This is an explanatory framework, not a falsifiable hypothesis. The same behavior can be answered separately from the four angles of mechanism, development, function, and evolution.
