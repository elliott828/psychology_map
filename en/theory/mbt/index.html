Mentalization-Based Treatment

Theory·1999·基于心智化的治疗

The core of treatment is to help the patient pause when emotions run out of control, and try to think about their own and others' thoughts and feelings.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Mentalization
Landmark
Bateman and Fonagy published a randomized controlled trial of partial hospitalization for borderline personality disorder.
What the evidence says
Several randomized controlled trials for borderline personality disorder show it is effective, with results close to other specialized therapies.
