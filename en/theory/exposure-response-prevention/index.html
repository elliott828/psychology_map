Exposure and Response Prevention

Theory·1966·暴露与反应预防

People with obsessive-compulsive disorder are exposed to the things that cause anxiety while not performing rituals such as handwashing. Anxiety naturally declines, and the compulsions weaken with it.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy
Landmark
Meyer treated obsessive-compulsive disorder with exposure and response prevention.
What the evidence says
It is the method with the strongest evidence for treating obsessive-compulsive disorder, supported by several controlled trials.
