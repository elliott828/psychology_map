Jung's Model of the Psyche

Theory·1912·荣格的心灵模型

The psyche includes the conscious ego, the personal unconscious (which contains complexes), and the collective unconscious (which contains archetypes). The task of life is individuation.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung, 'Symbols of Transformation'.
What the evidence says
This is an explanatory framework, and its core concepts are hard to test directly. Some elements, such as complexes and introversion-extraversion, were later taken up in empirical research.
