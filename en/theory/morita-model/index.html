Morita's Model of Shinkeishitsu

Theory·1928·“顺其自然、为所当为”

Neurotic people pay excessive attention to their discomfort, and the more they focus on it, the worse it gets. Treatment is to help them accept their symptoms while returning to everyday action.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Morita Therapy
Landmark
Morita Masatake, 'The Essence of Shinkeishitsu and Its Treatment'.
What the evidence says
Some studies suggest it helps anxiety disorders. There have been few large-scale comparisons with mainstream therapies.
