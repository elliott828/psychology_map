Taste Aversion and Biological Preparedness

Theory·1966·味觉厌恶与生物准备性

If someone gets sick after eating a food, they can develop a lasting dislike for its taste, even when the illness comes hours later. Pairing light with nausea, by contrast, is hard to learn.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Classical Conditioning
Landmark
Garcia and Koelling found that rats readily link nausea with taste, but not with light.
What the evidence says
It has been replicated across several species. It is a hallmark example of learning being constrained by biology.
