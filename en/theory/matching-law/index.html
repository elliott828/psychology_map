Matching Law

Theory·1961·匹配律

When animals distribute their time or responses between two options, the proportion roughly matches the proportion of rewards each option gives.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Operant Conditioning
Landmark
Herrnstein published research on pigeons' choice behavior.
What the evidence says
It fits animal and some human choice data well, but deviates under some conditions.
