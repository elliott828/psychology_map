Terror Management Theory

Theory·1986·恐惧管理理论

Knowing we will die, people are driven by this fear to defend their cultural worldview and self-esteem. When reminded of death, people become more hostile toward different views.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Existential Psychotherapy
Landmark
Greenberg, Pyszczynski, and Solomon proposed terror management theory.
What the evidence says
The mortality-salience effect has appeared in several experiments. But some large recent replications have failed, and the theoretical explanation is also questioned.
