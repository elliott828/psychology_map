Correlation and Regression

Theory·1888·相关与回归

The degree to which two variables change together can be quantified. Extreme values tend to be closer to the average on the next measurement. This is 'regression'.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Individual Differences
Landmark
Galton introduced the concept of correlation, and Pearson later gave the formula for the correlation coefficient.
What the evidence says
These are the foundations of modern statistics and are not in dispute. Their use and misuse, such as treating correlation as causation, is a separate matter.

Key people

  • Francis Galton 1822–1911
  • Karl Pearson 1857–1936
