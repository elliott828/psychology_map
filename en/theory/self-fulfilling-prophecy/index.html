Self-Fulfilling Prophecy

Theory·1968·期望效应（皮格马利翁）

When teachers believe certain students have potential, those students may later perform better. This is linked to the attention and feedback teachers give without intending to.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Social Cognition
Landmark
Rosenthal and Jacobson, 'Pygmalion in the Classroom'.
What the evidence says
Teacher expectations do have an effect. But the original finding of large IQ gains was criticized on methodological grounds, and later meta-analyses show a smaller effect.
