Structural Family Therapy

Theory·1974·结构式家庭治疗

A family is made up of boundaries, alliances, and hierarchy. Symptoms often come from structural imbalance. The therapist adjusts the structure by changing seating, alliances, and similar elements.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Landmark

What the evidence says
Research supports it for adolescent conduct problems and eating disorders. The theory's concept of 'boundaries' is hard to measure.
