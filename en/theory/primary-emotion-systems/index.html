Primary Emotion Systems

Theory·1998·基本情绪系统

Mammalian brains contain innate emotional circuits, such as 'seeking', 'fear', and 'care'. They come before thought and drive behavior.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Affective Neuroscience
Landmark
Panksepp, 'Affective Neuroscience', proposed seven subcortical systems including seeking, fear, and rage.
What the evidence says
Animal experiments offer rich clues. But treating animal behavior as direct evidence of subjective feeling, and the seven-part classification itself, are both disputed.
