Self-Perception Theory

Theory·1967·自我知觉理论

People do not always know what they think. Like observers, they infer from their own behavior that they probably like something.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Cognitive Dissonance
Landmark
Bem proposed that people infer their own attitudes by observing their own behavior.
What the evidence says
It explains well situations where attitudes are weak or unclear. It is more limited for strong attitudes, and it coexists with dissonance theory.
