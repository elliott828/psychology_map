Eysenck PEN Model

Theory·1967·艾森克的 PEN 模型

The core of personality is a small number of dimensions, each with a biological basis. For example, extraverts have lower cortical arousal, so they seek more stimulation.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Factor-Analytic Personality Models
Landmark
Eysenck, 'The Biological Basis of Personality': extraversion, neuroticism, psychoticism, and their physiological bases.
What the evidence says
Extraversion and neuroticism have been replicated many times. Psychoticism has a weaker structure. The physiological explanation (arousal level) is partly supported.
