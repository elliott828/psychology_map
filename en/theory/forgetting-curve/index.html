Forgetting Curve

Theory·1885·遗忘曲线

Material learned is forgotten fastest in the first few hours to days, then more slowly. Spaced repetition flattens the curve.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Experimental Study of Memory
Landmark
Ebbinghaus, 'Memory: A Contribution to Experimental Psychology'.
What the evidence says
A 2015 replication again found a similar curve. The rate of forgetting varies with the material and the way it was learned.
