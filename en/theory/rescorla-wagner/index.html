Rescorla-Wagner Model

Theory·1972·雷斯科拉—瓦格纳模型

Learning happens only when the outcome is unexpected. If a signal already fully predicts food, adding a second signal teaches almost nothing new.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Classical Conditioning
Landmark
Rescorla and Wagner proposed that learning depends on 'prediction error'.
What the evidence says
The idea of prediction error has been further supported by neuroscience (the dopamine signal) and machine learning. Its description of complex situations is limited.
