Prospect Theory

Theory·1979·前景理论

People judge gains and losses not by final absolute amounts, but by change relative to a reference point. An equal-sized loss hurts more than a gain feels good.

Historical influence ●●●●●·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Heuristics and Biases
Landmark
Kahneman and Tversky, 'Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk'.
What the evidence says
Loss aversion and reference dependence are broadly robust. But the weight given to losses varies a great deal across situations.
