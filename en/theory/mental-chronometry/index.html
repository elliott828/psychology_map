Mental Chronometry

Theory·1868·心理计时法

To find out how long a mental step takes, compare reaction times on tasks with and without that step.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Experimental Psychology
Landmark
Donders measured the time needed for mental processing using differences in reaction time.
What the evidence says
The reaction-time method is still a core tool in cognitive experiments. Its assumption of 'pure insertion' has been revised.
