PERMA Model of Well-Being

Theory·2011·幸福的 PERMA 模型

Well-being has five parts: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Positive Psychology
Landmark
Seligman, 'Flourish'.
What the evidence says
Each of the five elements is linked to well-being. But that these are exactly the right five has not been tested.
