Parenting Styles

Theory·1966·养育方式

Parents can be divided into: warm and rule-setting (authoritative), demanding with little warmth (authoritarian), and warm with few rules (permissive).

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Ecological Systems Theory
Landmark
Baumrind published the distinction between authoritative, authoritarian, and permissive parenting.
What the evidence says
Authoritative parenting, which is warm and demanding, is linked to better outcomes. But correlation cannot be read directly as causation, and the effects differ across cultures.
