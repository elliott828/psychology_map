Infants as Scientists

Theory·1999·婴儿是小科学家

Infants learn by observing, experimenting, picking up statistical regularities, and imitating. They act like little scientists, forming hypotheses and adjusting them based on evidence.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Infant Cognition
Landmark
Gopnik, Meltzoff, and Kuhl, 'The Scientist in the Crib'.
What the evidence says
There are many experiments showing infants update their judgments based on evidence. But equating infant learning with a scientist's reasoning is still a metaphor.
