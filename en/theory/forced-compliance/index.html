Forced Compliance Study

Theory·1959·强迫顺从与“一美元”实验

Participants did a boring task and told the next person it was interesting. Those paid one dollar rated the task as more enjoyable than those paid twenty dollars, because a dollar gave them too little outside justification.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Cognitive Dissonance
Landmark
Festinger and Carlsmith: people who lied for one dollar came to believe their own lie more.
What the evidence says
Replicated many times. But the explanation based on reward and motivation has been challenged by alternatives such as self-perception theory.
