The Authoritarian Personality

Theory·1950·权威主义人格

People who are submissive to authority and hostile to outsiders were thought to have experienced harsh discipline in childhood. The explanation sparked a great deal of research and drew many criticisms.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Prejudice Research
Landmark
Adorno and colleagues, 'The Authoritarian Personality'.
What the evidence says
The methods were heavily criticized. The later 'right-wing authoritarianism' scale has better measurement properties, but the original psychoanalytic explanation has been abandoned.
