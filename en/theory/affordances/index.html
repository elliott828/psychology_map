Affordances

Theory·1979·可供性

What we directly perceive is not just shape and color, but what the environment lets us do. A chair is 'sit-on-able' and a door handle is 'pull-able'.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Embodied Cognition
Landmark
Gibson, 'The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception'.
What the evidence says
The idea of affordances has proven useful in human-computer interaction and robotics. As a theory of perception, it is still debated in terms of representation.
