The Human Connectome

Theory·2005·连接组：全脑的接线图

Treats the whole brain as a graph of regions and connections, and studies its structural and functional connectivity, rather than studying one region at a time.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Human Connectome
Landmark
Sporns, Tononi, and Kotter coined the term 'human connectome' (2005).
What the evidence says
Methods for measuring connectomes, such as diffusion imaging, are now well developed. But the expectation that knowing all the wiring would explain the mind has not been realized.
