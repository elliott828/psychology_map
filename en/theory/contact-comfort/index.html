Contact Comfort

Theory·1958·接触安慰

Infant monkeys preferred clinging to a soft 'cloth mother' over a wire 'mother' that provided food. This shows attachment is not only about feeding.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Attachment Theory
Landmark
Harlow, 'The Nature of Love': infant monkeys attached to a soft cloth mother rather than a wire mother that provided milk.
What the evidence says
The results have been supported by later studies. But the experiments caused serious harm to the animals, and they would not be approved today.
