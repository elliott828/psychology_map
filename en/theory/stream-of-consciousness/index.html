Stream of Consciousness

Theory·1890·意识流

Consciousness is not a string of isolated sensations. It is a continuous, shifting flow with fringes around it, and each person's consciousness is private.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Interpretive framework·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Functionalism
Landmark
James, 'The Principles of Psychology'.
What the evidence says
This is a descriptive account of experience rather than a testable hypothesis. It still shapes discussions of attention and the self.
