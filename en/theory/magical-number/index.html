The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two

Theory·1956·神奇的数字 7±2

The number of units a person can hold in short-term memory is limited, about seven. But this can be expanded through chunking, which combines several items into one.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Cognitive Revolution
Landmark
Miller, 'The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two', in Psychological Review.
What the evidence says
The concept of chunking holds up. The capacity figure was later revised to about four chunks.
