Hereditary Genius and Eugenics

Theory·1869·遗传的天才与优生学

Outstanding people often come from the same families. From this, Galton concluded that talent is mainly inherited. He advocated improving humanity through selective breeding, a claim later used to justify discrimination and forced sterilization.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Individual Differences
Landmark
Galton, 'Hereditary Genius'. He coined the term 'eugenics' in 1883.
What the evidence says
Family resemblance can also come from shared environments, so his conclusions have been overturned. The historical harm caused by eugenics must be remembered.
