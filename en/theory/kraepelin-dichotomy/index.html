Kraepelin's Dichotomy

Theory·1899·克雷佩林的早发性痴呆与躁狂抑郁症之分

Psychotic illnesses are divided into two major groups: one begins early in life and declines (dementia praecox), and the other occurs in episodes and can recover (manic-depressive illness).

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Modern Psychiatric Classification
Landmark
Kraepelin's sixth edition of his textbook.
What the evidence says
The dichotomy was later found to have many overlaps, and it is now a target of criticism in current discussions of a psychosis spectrum.
