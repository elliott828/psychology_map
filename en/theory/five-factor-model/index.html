Five-Factor Model

Theory·1992·五因素模型

Openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism. Each of the five dimensions can be divided into facets, and every person has a position on each dimension.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Big Five Personality
Landmark
Costa and McCrae, 'NEO-PI-R Professional Manual'.
What the evidence says
The five-factor structure largely replicates in samples from many countries. It is not fully consistent in small-scale societies or at finer levels of detail.
