Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI)

Theory·1962·MBTI（迈尔斯—布里格斯类型指标）

Turns Jung's typology into a self-report questionnaire. People are placed at one pole on each of four pairs (extraversion-introversion, sensing-intuition, thinking-feeling, judging-perceiving), which combine into 16 types.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Trait Theory
Landmark
The Educational Testing Service (ETS) published the first MBTI manual (1962). The mother-daughter authors had been developing the instrument since the 1940s.
What the evidence says
On retest, a substantial share of people receive a different type. Dimension scores are continuous rather than bipolar, and predictive power for job performance is weak. The Big Five model is more reliable. See the entry on popular misreadings.
