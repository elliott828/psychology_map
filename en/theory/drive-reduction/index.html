Drive Reduction Theory

Theory·1943·驱力还原论

Internal deficits such as hunger and thirst create drives. Behavior serves to reduce those drives, and responses that reduce them get reinforced.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mostly historical·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Neo-Behaviorism
Landmark
Hull, 'Principles of Behavior'.
What the evidence says
'Reinforcement equals reduction of physiological drive' cannot explain exploration or curiosity, so the theory has been abandoned. But it dominated American learning psychology for about two decades.
