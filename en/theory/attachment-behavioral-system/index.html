Attachment Behavioral System

Theory·1969·依恋行为系统

Infants are born with a set of behaviors that seek closeness to a caregiver. They are activated by threat, in order to gain protection and comfort. The caregiver's responses shape the infant's internal expectations about relationships.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●●●○

What the evidence says
Infants seeking closeness and using the caregiver as a secure base has been observed repeatedly in studies across many countries.
