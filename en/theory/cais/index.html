Cognitive-Affective Personality System

Theory·1995·认知—情感人格系统

Personality is not 'a person who is always extraverted'. It is 'extraverted in some situations and quiet in others'. The stable pattern is this if-then profile.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Person-Situation Debate
Landmark
Mischel and Shoda proposed the if-then behavioral signatures.
What the evidence says
There is considerable evidence that behavior follows stable patterns in certain situations. But the measurement of an individual's 'behavioral signature' is still rough.
