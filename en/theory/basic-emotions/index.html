Basic Emotions

Theory·1971·基本情绪与面部表情

Six or seven emotions, such as happiness, anger, and fear, have facial expressions that are common across cultures. This view is widely used in popular science and continues to be questioned by researchers.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Affective Neuroscience
Landmark
Ekman and Friesen published 'Constants Across Cultures in the Face and Emotion'.
What the evidence says
Recognition accuracy is high in several cultures. But the forced-choice design and the ability to predict natural expressions have been criticized in later research.
