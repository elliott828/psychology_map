Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy

Theory·1988·伴侣情绪聚焦治疗

Repeated conflict between partners is seen as a cycle in which attachment needs cannot be expressed. Through helping each partner voice hidden emotions, safety in the relationship is rebuilt.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

Landmark

What the evidence says
Several controlled trials and follow-up studies support it. Most of the research comes from the developers' own teams.
