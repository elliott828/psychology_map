Expectancy Theory of Work Motivation

Theory·1964·工作动机的期望理论

Whether a person puts in effort depends on three beliefs: that effort will lead to performance, that performance will lead to rewards, and that the rewards are valuable to them.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

What the evidence says
The expectancy-times-value-times-instrumentality framework for predicting effort has some support. But its parameters are hard to measure.
