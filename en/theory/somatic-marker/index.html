Somatic Marker Hypothesis

Theory·1994·躯体标记假说

When making decisions, emotional reactions in the body tag options as 'good' or 'bad'. Without these tags, even intelligent people make poor decisions.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Affective Neuroscience
Landmark
Damasio, 'Descartes' Error'.
What the evidence says
Clinical observations that patients with damage to the prefrontal cortex have impaired decision-making are reliable. Whether body signals are necessary is questioned, and the interpretation of the Iowa Gambling Task has been challenged.
