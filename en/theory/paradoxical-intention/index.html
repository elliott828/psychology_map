Paradoxical Intention

Theory·1960·矛盾意向法

The things we most try to avoid are the most likely to happen. So deliberately wishing for them to happen can break the cycle of anxiety.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Logotherapy
Landmark
Frankl applied the technique to anxiety and insomnia.
What the evidence says
Some controlled studies support it for insomnia and anticipatory anxiety. Its effectiveness varies by condition.
