Psychological Types

Theory·1921·心理类型

People have two attitudes, introversion and extraversion, and four functions: thinking, feeling, sensation, and intuition. Each person leans toward one or two of them. The MBTI was built on this framework.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Analytical Psychology
Landmark
Jung, 'Psychological Types'.
What the evidence says
Introversion and extraversion have been confirmed by research. The four-function division and the paired types have not been supported. Personality measures usually show continuous distributions.
