Stanford Prison Experiment

Theory·1971·斯坦福监狱实验

Zimbardo claimed that randomly assigned college students quickly became cruel guards. But the archives show the experimenters suggested the behavior, so it is hard to prove the power of the roles themselves.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●●

School
Conformity and Obedience
Landmark
Zimbardo had college students play guards and prisoners. It was stopped early, after six days.
What the evidence says
Materials disclosed later show the guards were coached and there were clear demand characteristics. It cannot be used as evidence that situations make good people do evil.
