Psychological Automatism

Theory·1889·心理自动症与观念固着

Some experiences are 'split off' from the main consciousness and operate like a separate small system. This causes forgetfulness, paralysis, or states that feel like being 'another person'.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Hypnosis, Hysteria and Dissociation
Landmark
Janet, 'Psychological Automatism'.
What the evidence says
Later research supports the idea that severe trauma narrows consciousness and produces dissociation. But Janet's specific model has been revised.
