Early Intensive Behavioral Intervention

Theory·1987·早期高强度行为干预

Young autistic children receive several dozen hours of behavioral training a week to improve language and social skills.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Mixed findings·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Applied Behavior Analysis
Landmark
Lovaas published an intervention study of autistic children, reporting that nearly half came close to typical functioning.
What the evidence says
Several studies have found it helps some children. But the comparison-group design of the original data has been criticized, and debates about its effectiveness and ethics continue.
