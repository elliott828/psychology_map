Allostatic Load

Theory·1993·稳态应变负荷

The body adjusts constantly to cope with stress. The cost of repeated adjustment over time builds up as 'load', showing up as an overall worsening of blood pressure, blood sugar, inflammation, and other markers.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Stress Physiology
Landmark
McEwen and Stellar published 'Stress and the Individual'.
What the evidence says
Several population and animal studies have found that cumulative stress markers predict disease and death. The exact ways of measuring load are still not standardized.
