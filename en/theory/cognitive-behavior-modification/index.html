Cognitive-Behavior Modification

Theory·1977·认知行为矫正

What people say to themselves affects their behavior. Learning more helpful 'inner dialogue' can improve impulsivity, anxiety, and stress coping.

Historical influence ●●●●○·Evidence: Strong evidence·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy
Landmark
Meichenbaum, 'Cognitive-Behavior Modification'.
What the evidence says
Self-instruction training, stress inoculation training, and similar methods have controlled-trial support for several problems.
