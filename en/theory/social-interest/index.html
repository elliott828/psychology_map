Social Interest

Theory·1927·社会兴趣

The sign of mental health is care for and contribution to others and to the community, not a focus on one's own superiority.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●○○

School
Individual Psychology
Landmark
Adler, 'Understanding Human Nature'.
What the evidence says
Research supports the link between connection with others and mental health. Social interest has been measured little as an independent construct.
