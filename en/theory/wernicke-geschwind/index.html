Wernicke-Geschwind Model

Theory·1874·语言的布洛卡—韦尼克区模型

The motor part of speech lies in the front of the frontal lobe, and the comprehension part lies in the back of the temporal lobe. The two are linked by nerve fibers. Modern research holds that language involves a wider network.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Cerebral Localization
Landmark
Wernicke described sensory aphasia (1874). Geschwind organized it into the classic model in 1965.
What the evidence says
The broad picture (a left-hemisphere language network) holds up. The classic 'two centers plus one pathway' view is too simple.
