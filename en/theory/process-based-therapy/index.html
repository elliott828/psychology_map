Process-Based Therapy

Theory·2018·基于过程的治疗

Instead of asking 'which therapy works for which diagnosis', it asks 'which processes maintain this person's problem, and what methods can change those processes'.

Historical influence ●●○○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Process-Based Therapy
Landmark
Hayes and Hofmann, 'Process-Based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy' (2018).
What the evidence says
This is still an emerging framework. It proposes shifting the unit of evaluation from 'therapy-diagnosis' to 'process-individual'. Large-scale testing has only just begun.
