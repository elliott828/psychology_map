Eight Stages of Man

Theory·1950·人生八阶段

Eight stages center on trust, autonomy, initiative, industry, identity, intimacy, generativity, and integrity. Failing to handle a stage's task leaves lasting difficulties.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●●●○

School
Psychosocial Development
Landmark
Erikson, 'Childhood and Society'.
What the evidence says
Themes such as basic trust, identity, and intimacy have research support. The order of the stages and their universality have not been properly tested.
