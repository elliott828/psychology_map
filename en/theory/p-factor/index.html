The p Factor

Theory·2014·p 因素

Psychiatric problems are correlated with one another. This suggests there may be a common 'general psychopathology factor' behind them, similar to g in intelligence testing.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Supported, with limits·Public familiarity ●○○○○

School
Dimensional Models (RDoC / HiTOP)
Landmark
Caspi and colleagues, 'The p Factor'.
What the evidence says
Several longitudinal studies support a shared vulnerability factor. But what it actually represents is still debated.
