Compassion-Focused Therapy

Theory·2005·慈悲聚焦疗法

Emotion regulation is divided into three systems: threat, drive, and soothing. Self-compassion is cultivated to soothe people who have long lived in a threat state.

Historical influence ●●●○○·Evidence: Little research·Public familiarity ●●○○○

School
Third-Wave Therapies
Landmark
Gilbert, 'The Compassionate Mind'.
What the evidence says
It improves shame and self-criticism in those who have them. But the evidence comes mostly from small studies, and larger controlled trials are still few.
