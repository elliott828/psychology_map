Confucian and Daoist Self-Cultivation

Source·pre-Qin era (before 221 BCE)·儒道的心性修养

Treats personality as something formed through relationships and self-cultivation, not as an inner property of an isolated individual. This is a resource that Chinese indigenous psychology often returns to.

When
pre-Qin era (before 221 BCE)
What remains today
Chinese indigenous psychology has built its own theories around concepts such as 'relationships,' 'face' and 'self-cultivation,' and often contrasts them with Western psychology, which emphasizes the individual.
Later influenced
Indigenous Psychology
