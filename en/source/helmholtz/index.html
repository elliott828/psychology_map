Sensory Physiology

Source·1850·感官生理学

Helmholtz measured the speed of nerve conduction, showing that mental processes take time and can be measured. He also proposed that perception involves 'unconscious inference,' providing Wundt's laboratory with methods and questions.

When
1850
What remains today
Measuring nerve conduction speed made reaction-time research possible, and cognitive experiments still use reaction time as a basic measure. His view of perception as unconscious inference has been revived in predictive processing.
Later influenced
Experimental Psychology
