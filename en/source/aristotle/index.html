Aristotle's De Anima

Source·around 350 BCE·亚里士多德《论灵魂》

Studied the soul as the function of a living body. In On Memory and Recollection he proposed that ideas are linked by similarity, contrast and contiguity, the source of associationism.

When
around 350 BCE
What remains today
The three principles of association (similarity, contrast and contiguity) are still the starting point for textbook discussions of associative memory. The view that the soul is a function of the living body echoes today's debates in functionalism and philosophy of mind.
