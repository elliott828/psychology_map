Rousseau's View of Childhood

Source·1762·卢梭的儿童观

Emile argues that childhood has its own stages and value, and that children are not 'small adults.' This idea was one of the starting points for child study and later developmental stage theories.

When
1762
What remains today
'Children are not miniature adults, and childhood has its own stages' became a basic premise of developmental psychology, and it can still be seen in contemporary early education ideas.
Later influenced
Child Study Movement
