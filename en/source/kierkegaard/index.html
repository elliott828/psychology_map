Beginnings of Existential Philosophy

Source·1844·存在哲学的开端

In The Concept of Anxiety, Kierkegaard argues that anxiety comes from the vertigo of facing freedom and possibility, not only from fear of specific dangers.

When
1844
What remains today
The argument that anxiety arises from freedom was cited by existential therapists such as Rollo May, and existentially oriented psychotherapy still uses it to understand anxiety.
Later influenced
Existential Psychotherapy
