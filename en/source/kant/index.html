Kant's A Priori Categories

Source·1781·康德的先验范畴

The Critique of Pure Reason argues that the mind organizes experience using built-in structures (time, space, causality). This idea later influenced Gestalt psychology and constructivist views of development.

When
1781
What remains today
The idea that the mind has built-in structures for organizing experience is often compared with Piaget's 'schemas' and Chomsky's innate grammar. Gestalt psychology is also often traced back to this tradition.
Later influenced
Gestalt Psychology
Threads
Mind and Cognition, Lifespan Development
