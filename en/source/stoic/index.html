Stoicism

Source·3rd century BCE to 2nd century CE·斯多葛学派

Epictetus said that what troubles people is not things themselves but their views of things. Ellis and Beck both explicitly cited this idea as a philosophical forerunner of cognitive therapy.

When
3rd century BCE to 2nd century CE
What remains today
The basic approach of cognitive behavioral therapy, that thoughts shape emotions, is often traced back to this tradition. Its founders, Ellis and Beck, both cited Stoic ideas of this kind.
Later influenced
Cognitive Therapies
