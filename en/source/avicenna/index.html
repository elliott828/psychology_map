Avicenna's Canon of Medicine

Source·1025 CE·伊本·西那《医典》

The Canon of Medicine described and classified psychological conditions such as melancholia, mania and lovesickness, linked them to bodily states, and served as the standard medical textbook of medieval Europe.

When
1025 CE
What remains today
The Canon was used as a medical textbook in Europe and the Islamic world for centuries. Its descriptions of melancholia, mania and similar conditions are among the earlier systematic classifications in the history of psychiatry, and today they are mainly used as references in the history of medicine and psychiatry.
