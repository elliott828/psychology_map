Mesmerism

Source·from 1774·催眠术的前身

Mesmer's 'animal magnetism' was rejected by a scientific commission, but the effects of suggestion were noticed. In 1843 Braid renamed the phenomenon 'hypnosis,' opening research on suggestion and the separation of consciousness.

When
from 1774
What remains today
Hypnosis is still used today for pain management and some forms of psychotherapy. The 1784 French royal commission's test of 'animal magnetism' is often seen as an early controlled experiment with a blinding element, and it made 'suggestion' a theme that later placebo research would take up.
Later influenced
