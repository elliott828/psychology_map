Descartes' Mind-Body Dualism

Source·1641·笛卡尔的身心二元论

The Meditations on First Philosophy divides mind and body into two kinds of substance. This leaves the problem of how the two interact, which later neuroscience and philosophy of mind have continued to address.

When
1641
What remains today
The question of how mind and body interact still appears today in the form of the 'hard problem' of consciousness in philosophy of mind and neuroscience. Damasio's Descartes' Error is titled as a critique of this dualism.
