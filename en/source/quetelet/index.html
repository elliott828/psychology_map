Social Statistics and the Average Man

Source·1835·社会统计与“平均人”

Quetelet's A Treatise on Man applied the normal distribution to human traits such as height and crime rates, and proposed the 'average man.' Galton later built on this and turned to studying deviations from the average.

When
1835
What remains today
The 'average man' became the starting point for describing populations statistically. Today the Body Mass Index is also called the 'Quetelet index.'
