Buddhist Meditation and Analysis of Mind

Source·from around the 5th century BCE·佛教的禅修与心识分析

Systematic insight meditation trains attention to observe the arising and passing of feelings and thoughts, and it developed a detailed classification of mental phenomena. Contemporary mindfulness programs and acceptance-based therapies borrow its practices.

When
from around the 5th century BCE
What remains today
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction, mindfulness-based cognitive therapy and acceptance and commitment therapy have brought the training of 'observing present experience' into hospitals and clinics. Most of these programs strip out the religious content and keep only the practices.
Later influenced
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction
