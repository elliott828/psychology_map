Phrenology

Source·1810·颅相学

Gall held that different mental functions are located in different brain regions. Inferring personality from skull shape is pseudoscience, but the idea of functional localization was kept.

When
1810
What remains today
Phrenology itself has been rejected, but the idea that different brain regions handle different functions was confirmed after Broca's work, and brain imaging research today still maps functional areas.
Later influenced
