Myths

  • “The MBTI can accurately sort people into 16 fixed types” Largely unsupported
  • “Maslow said human needs form a pyramid, and lower levels must be met before people pursue higher ones” Misleading
  • “Some people are “left-brained” (rational), and others are “right-brained” (emotional)” False
  • “The Stanford Prison Experiment proved that when good people are placed in a bad situation, they become abusers” False
  • “People only use 10 percent of their brain” False
  • “Everyone has a learning style (visual or auditory), and teaching that matches it works better” False
  • “Depression is caused by too little serotonin in the brain, and medication replaces the missing chemical” Largely unsupported
  • “Firstborns are conscientious, second children are rebellious, and youngest children are spoiled. Birth order determines personality” Largely unsupported
  • “Memory is like a video recorder. If you think hard enough, you can retrieve it exactly as it was” False
  • “Your attachment style was set in childhood, and it determines your love life for the rest of your life” Misleading
