Purposive Behavior in Animals and Men

Book·Edward C. Tolman, 1932·动物与人的目的性行为

Tolman argues that behavior is purposive and goal-directed. He proposes intervening variables such as "expectancies" and "cognitive maps" to explain how animals learn in mazes.
