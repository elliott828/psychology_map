Psilocybin Can Occasion Mystical-Type Experiences Having Substantial and Sustained Personal Meaning and Spiritual Significance

Book·Roland R. Griffiths et al., 2006·赛洛西宾引发神秘体验

A double-blind experiment showed that high-dose psilocybin could lead volunteers to have mystical experiences, which they still regarded as meaningful and spiritually significant months later.
