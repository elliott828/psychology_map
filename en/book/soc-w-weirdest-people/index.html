The Weirdest People in the World?

Book·Joseph Henrich, Steven J. Heine, Ara Norenzayan, 2010·世界上最怪异的人？

A Behavioral and Brain Sciences paper pointing out that psychology's participants are mostly from Western, educated, industrialized, rich and democratic societies. On many tasks they are not representative of humanity as a whole.
