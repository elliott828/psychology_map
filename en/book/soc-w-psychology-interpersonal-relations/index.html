The Psychology of Interpersonal Relations

Book·Fritz Heider, 1958·人际关系心理学

In this book Heider proposes that people, like "naive psychologists," look for causes of other people's behavior. He distinguishes causes that lie in the person from causes that lie in the situation. Attribution theory begins here.
