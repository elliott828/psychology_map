Gestalt Therapy: Excitement and Growth in the Human Personality

Book·Frederick Perls, Ralph Hefferline, Paul Goodman, 1951·格式塔治疗：人格中的兴奋与成长

The foundational work on Gestalt therapy. The first half offers a set of experiential exercises; the second half sets out the theory, emphasizing awareness in the present, contact, and unfinished "business."
