Adolescence: Its Psychology and Its Relations to Physiology, Anthropology, Sociology, Sex, Crime, Religion and Education (2 vols.)

Book·G. Stanley Hall, 1904·青春期

A two-volume work that first treated adolescence systematically as a distinct developmental stage and proposed the idea of "storm and stress." It was very influential, and many of its views were later revised.
