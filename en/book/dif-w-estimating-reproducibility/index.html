Estimating the Reproducibility of Psychological Science

Book·Open Science Collaboration, 2015·估计心理科学的可重复性

A Science paper. More than a hundred researchers replicated 100 published psychology experiments; roughly a third to a half of the results were reproduced. It became a landmark piece of evidence for the replication crisis.
