Psychotherapy by Reciprocal Inhibition

Book·Joseph Wolpe, 1958·交互抑制心理治疗

Wolpe systematically presents systematic desensitization: in a relaxed state, the patient faces anxiety-provoking situations, moving from weak to strong. An early classic of behavior therapy.
