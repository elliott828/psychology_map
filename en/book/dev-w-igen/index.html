iGen: Why Today's Super-Connected Kids Are Growing Up Less Rebellious, More Tolerant, Less Happy—and Completely Unprepared for Adulthood

Book·Jean M. Twenge, 2017·i 世代

Drawing on decades of national surveys, Twenge describes the generation born after 1995: they date later, sleep less and feel more lonely. She links the change to smartphones.
