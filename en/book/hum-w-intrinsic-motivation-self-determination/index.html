Intrinsic Motivation and Self-Determination in Human Behavior

Book·Edward L. Deci, Richard M. Ryan, 1985·人类行为中的内在动机与自我决定

The first systematic book on self-determination theory. It distinguishes intrinsic from extrinsic motivation, explains when external rewards undermine interest, and describes the roles of the needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness.
