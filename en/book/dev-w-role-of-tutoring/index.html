The Role of Tutoring in Problem Solving

Book·David Wood, Jerome S. Bruner, Gail Ross, 1976·辅导在问题解决中的作用

Bruner and colleagues propose "scaffolding": adults give appropriate support where a child cannot yet manage, and withdraw it as the child improves.
