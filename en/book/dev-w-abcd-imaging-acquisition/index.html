Book·B. J. Casey et al., 2018·青少年脑认知发展（ABCD）研究：21 个站点的成像采集

Describes the brain imaging protocol of this national longitudinal study in the United States: magnetic resonance data were collected in a standardized way at 21 sites from over 10,000 children aged 9 to 10.
