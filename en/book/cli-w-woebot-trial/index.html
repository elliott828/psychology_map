Delivering Cognitive Behavior Therapy to Young Adults With Symptoms of Depression and Anxiety Using a Fully Automated Conversational Agent (Woebot): A Randomized Controlled Trial

Book·Kathleen Kara Fitzpatrick, Alison Darcy, Molly Vierhile, 2017·用全自动对话程序（Woebot）为有抑郁和焦虑症状的青年人提供认知行为治疗

A JMIR Mental Health paper reporting a randomized controlled trial. Young adults who used the chat program showed a larger decline in depressive symptoms than the control group. The sample was small and the duration short.
