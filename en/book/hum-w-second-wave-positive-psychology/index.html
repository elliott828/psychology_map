Second Wave Positive Psychology: Exploring the Positive–Negative Dialectics of Wellbeing

Book·Tim Lomas, Itai Ivtzan, 2016·第二波积极心理学：探索福祉中积极与消极的辩证关系

A paper in the Journal of Happiness Studies that reviews criticism within positive psychology of focusing only on the positive. It proposes a view of well-being in which positive and negative aspects are interdependent. Published online in 2015 and in print in 2016.
