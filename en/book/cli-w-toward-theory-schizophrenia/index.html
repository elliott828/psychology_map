Toward a Theory of Schizophrenia

Book·Gregory Bateson, Don D. Jackson, Jay Haley, John Weakland, 1956·迈向精神分裂症的理论

Proposes the "double bind": when a person receives contradictory messages over a long period and cannot respond, psychotic symptoms may appear. The hypothesis was largely abandoned later.
