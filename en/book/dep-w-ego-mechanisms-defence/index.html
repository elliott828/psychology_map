The Ego and the Mechanisms of Defence

Book·Anna Freud, 1936·自我与防御机制

Anna Freud systematically described defense mechanisms such as repression, projection and reaction formation. It is a foundational work of ego psychology. The German original appeared in 1936; the English translation in 1937.
