An Integrative Theory of Intergroup Conflict

Book·Henri Tajfel, John C. Turner, 1979·群际冲突的整合理论

In this chapter Tajfel and Turner propose that people gain part of their self-identity from their group and therefore favor their own group. The theory was later called social identity theory.
