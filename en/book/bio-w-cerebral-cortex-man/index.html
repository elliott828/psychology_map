The Cerebral Cortex of Man: A Clinical Study of Localization of Function

Book·Wilder Penfield, Theodore Rasmussen, 1950·人的大脑皮质

Based on electrical stimulation of the cerebral cortex of awake patients during epilepsy surgery, it maps the body onto the cortex as the "sensory homunculus" and "motor homunculus."
