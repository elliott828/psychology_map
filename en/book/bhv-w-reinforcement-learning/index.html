Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction

Book·Richard S. Sutton, Andrew G. Barto, 1998·强化学习导论

The standard textbook on reinforcement learning, on how an agent learns to act through trial, error and reward. Its temporal-difference methods were later used to explain dopamine signals in the brain. First edition 1998; second edition 2018.
