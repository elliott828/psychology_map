Elements of Psychophysics, Vol. 1

Book·Gustav Theodor Fechner, 1860·心理物理学纲要

In this book Fechner proposed that the relationship between mental and physical magnitudes can be studied quantitatively with mathematics. It is generally regarded as one of the starting points of experimental psychology. The German original appeared in 1860; the English translation in 1966.
