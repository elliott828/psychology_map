The Spread of True and False News Online

Book·Soroush Vosoughi, Deb Roy, Sinan Aral, 2018·真假新闻在网上的传播

A Science paper analyzing how news spread on Twitter. It found that false news spread faster and farther than true news, driven mainly by people rather than bots.
