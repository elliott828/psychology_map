The Anatomy of Dependence

Book·Takeo Doi, 1971·“甘え”的构造

Takeo Doi uses the Japanese word "amae" (the wish to be indulged and to depend on another's goodwill) to analyze Japanese interpersonal relations and psychology. The original appeared in 1971; the English translation in 1973.
