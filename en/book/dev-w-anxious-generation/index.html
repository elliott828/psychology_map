The Anxious Generation: How the Great Rewiring of Childhood Is Causing an Epidemic of Mental Illness

Book·Jonathan Haidt, 2024·焦虑的一代

Haidt argues that smartphones have shifted childhood from play-based to screen-based, and that this is a major cause of worsening adolescent mental health. He proposes limits on phones. Many researchers have questioned his causal claims.
