Principles of Physiological Psychology, Vol. 1

Book·Wilhelm Wundt, 1874·生理心理学原理

Wundt's foundational work applying the experimental methods of physiology to the study of sensation and consciousness. He founded his psychology laboratory in Leipzig in 1879. The first German edition appeared in 1873–1874.
