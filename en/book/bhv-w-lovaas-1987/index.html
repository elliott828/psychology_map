Book·O. Ivar Lovaas, 1987·对幼年孤独症儿童的行为治疗与正常的教育及智力功能

Lovaas reports that after early intensive behavioral intervention, nearly half of the children went on to regular classrooms. The design of this study was later widely criticized.
